## Haul into Place ◆
**Requirements:** the Grasper has a creature grabbed.
Attempt Athletics against the grabbed creature's Fortitude DC.
**Critical Success:** move both Grasper and target up to 20 feet.  
**Success:** move both up to 10 feet.  
**Failure:** no movement.  
**Critical Failure:** the target escapes.
Movement must maintain the Grab.

### Tactical priority

The Grasper moves targets:

1. into the wrong Ground/Air sector for their current state;
2. into a space Make Way is about to sweep;
3. toward clustered PCs if no Processional telegraph is active.

Its job is:

> **"No, actually, stand over there."**

## Part of the Wound

Processional Graspers are unaffected by:

- Eightfold Discharge;
- The Procession;
- Eclipsefall.

When the Monument reaches 0 HP:

> **all surviving Graspers immediately Disperse.**

## Grasper tactics

Run all three Graspers on one shared initiative immediately after the Monument.

### If no target is grabbed
1. Stride.
2. Clutching Hand / Grapple.
3. Grapple, Reposition, or close distance.

### If a target is grabbed
1. Haul into Place.
2. maintain or improve control;
3. Strike only if no better positional action exists.

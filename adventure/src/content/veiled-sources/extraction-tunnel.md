**Vessel:** the historical tunnel and reopened Ritual Chamber breach. **Resonance:** Choice. **Reconstruction:** one Wound manifesting six remembered rescuers.

### Incursion Trigger
The historical breach opens while a compatible extraction target is present. For target selection and staging, see the owning encounter.

### Reconstruction Condition
The breach remains open, the designated target remains within the tunnel / Ritual Chamber encounter region, and **Choice has not been Restored**.

The active Incursion does not end merely because the target leaves one Faceoff rank, objects to the rescue, or reaches either endpoint of the Progress Track.

### Reconstruction
**Form:** Wound  
**Resonance:** Choice

The Incursion consists of six remembered rescuers.

Individual rescuers that reach 0 HP Disperse and remain absent until the whole Incursion reconstructs. The Incursion is fully Dispersed only when all six rescuers are absent.

### Location Ward
The tunnel is the Source.

Ordinary interior walls, floor, supports, tools, and tunnel surfaces do not trigger Mnemonic Backlash merely because creatures are inside the location-Source.

The Ward expresses most strongly across the historical breach and concealed outer boundary of the route. Direct attempts to attack, tear out, or manipulate those Warded boundaries interact with the Source normally.

Use **Dispelling Spores, Bile Spray, Constrict, and Earthquake**, with their printed action costs, recharge and counterplay, from the [published Vemerak profile on Pathfinder Nexus](https://app.demiplane.com/nexus/pathfinder2e/creatures/vemerak-rm). Keep that profile available when running the encounter.

The creature has no mnemonic Source, Ward, Stabilization Window, or reconstruction after death. Its occult abilities are ordinary Golarion phenomena.

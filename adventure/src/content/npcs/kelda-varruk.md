## Overview

The Captain commands the Colony’s guard and oversees its defense, patrols, emergency response, and public safety. A Colony-born dwarf descended from families who came downriver from Davarn, she knows the city through its streets, walls, neighborhoods, and pressure points.
She is completely loyal to the Order because she has watched its procedures save lives. She is not privy to the truth of the Reading.

## Personality

No-nonsense, observant, and quietly amused by other people’s poor decisions. She rarely raises her voice and enjoys provoking arrogant outsiders just enough that they provide their own justification for arrest.
She automatically notes every exit, weapon, and vulnerable position in a room. The Keeper calls her **Doors**. She pretends not to answer to it.

## Motivation

Keep everyone presently within the Colony’s walls alive by making the Order’s procedures function under real conditions.

## Primary Protection

**Everyone presently inside the Colony’s walls.**
Her loyalty belongs to the Order because she believes it is the institution most capable of keeping those people alive.

## Fears

That people will die because she lacked the truth, failed to prepare, or hesitated when command required immediate action.

## Blind Spot

She mistakes operational necessity for moral authority.
Because the Order’s procedures work, she assumes the Order has earned the right to decide how and when they are used. She understands temporary command during a crisis but struggles to recognize when emergency authority has become permanent control.

## Narrative Role

The Captain demonstrates that the Order is competent, necessary, and populated by honorable people before the Party learns what it conceals.
Her eventual conflict is not simply whether the Order was wrong. It is whether duty to an institution can survive when that institution betrays the purpose for which her duty was given.

## Portrayal Notes

Dry, economical, and always aware of doors, weapons, civilians, and escape routes. Teasing is a controlled test of competence. Respect is shown by giving someone a real job and expecting them to do it.

## Pressure Response

The teasing stops. She gives short orders, names the objective, and expects everyone present to become useful immediately.

## Signature Quote

*“You fought well. You are also under arrest.”*

## Signature Object

A heavy ring of gate and district keys carried at her belt.

## Performance Inspiration

Cara Gee as Camina Drummer (minus the patois)

## Relationship to the Order

She respects hierarchy, preparation, and disciplined command. His Prominence determines policy; she makes that policy function in the real world.
She may find the Order secretive or inflexible, but assumes its leaders possess reasons she does not need to know. Learning how much was withheld from her feels first like professional betrayal: she was made responsible for protecting the Colony without being given the truth required to do so.

## Relationship to the Party

She initially treats the Party as another group of armed outsiders convinced local authorities are incompetent.
During the first Veiled attack, she recognizes their ability and fights beside them without hesitation. Afterward, she arrests them anyway.
Their relationship grows through demonstrated responsibility rather than agreement. She respects people who protect civilians, follow the objective, and remain useful after the exciting part ends.

## Act II Monitoring and the Confluence

Kelda continues tracking Darkwing operationally throughout Act II. She knows where they have gone, which faction facilities they entered, what incidents followed, what was damaged or stabilized, and which people are now treating them as allies or threats. Her reports are practical rather than interpretive.

On the **successful Reading** path, those reports gradually reveal a pattern: forgotten identities and relationships become more coherent wherever Darkwing investigates. Serevan eventually interprets this as evidence that the Party is reconstructing something the Reading deliberately closed and orders Darkwing detained under **Closure**.

Kelda’s relationship with Darkwing determines how she executes that order. If trust is poor, she attempts the detention professionally. If they have earned strong trust, she warns them first, explains that the Colony is destabilizing around the same pattern His Prominence attributes to them, and asks for their help before carrying out an order she is no longer certain will make the Colony safer. Intermediate relationships can produce a formal summons, guarded cooperation, or limited warning.

This is not a sudden rejection of the Order. Kelda’s conflict is operational: **does obeying the institution still protect the people inside the walls when she no longer believes she has been given enough truth to judge the order safely?**

## Relationship to the Keeper

She speaks to the Keeper plainly and does not indulge ceremonial language in private. She explains immediate dangers, expects safety instructions to be followed, and doesn’t patronize her.
However, she still assumes the Order has the right to make the larger decisions. She respects the Keeper’s capability before she fully respects the Keeper’s authority over her own life.

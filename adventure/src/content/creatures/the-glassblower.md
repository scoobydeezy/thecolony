### Living Furnace
A creature that **starts its turn within 10 feet** of the Glassblower or **first enters that distance on its turn** attempts **DC 32 basic Fortitude** against **2d6 fire**. A creature saves at most **once per turn** against this aura. The heat does not vanish merely because the silhouette is perfectly black; the source of heat is an inaccurate reconstruction of the craftsperson's job.

### Molten Breath ◆◆
**FIRE** — A 30-foot cone of superheated glass and air deals **8d6 fire**, **DC 32 basic Reflex**. A creature that critically fails is also **slowed 1 until the end of its next turn** as glass hardens over its limbs. **Recharge:** 1d4 rounds, rolled after use; it can use the ability again once the countdown ends.

### Finish the Vessel ◆
One adjacent 5-foot square becomes **hazardous molten ground** until the start of the Glassblower's next turn. A creature that enters or starts its turn there takes **3d6 fire**, basic Reflex DC 32 (once per turn). These squares do not persist after Dispersal.

**Tactics:** Its imperative is to keep the furnace working, not to pursue specific PCs. Frames and staff can maneuver it into an open area or cool its surroundings. Its Source remains the original blowpipe in Cell 09.

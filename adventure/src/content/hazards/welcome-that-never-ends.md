## Welcome

Use **Will DC 34** for entry, meaningful departure attempts and special recovery. Apply the four entry/departure outcomes in :ref[The Open Door]{#encounter.the-open-door}; this hazard has no routine damage or independent compulsion to attack. Appropriate mental/emotion immunity prevents its influence.

## Special Recovery

A genuinely consequential contradiction or choice permits a new save, including for Deeply At Home. **Critical success:** free of the influence for this encounter. **Success:** Deeply At Home becomes At Home; an At Home guest can act on this specific choice, including departing, with no second roll. **Failure or critical failure:** current severity remains; the insight is not forgotten. Identical declarations grant no new save. These results also apply to Maelyn’s meaningful realization.

## Counteracting and Suppression

Treat the local influence as **counteract rank 6, DC 34**. Successful counteraction of its effect on a creature frees that creature; it does not Anchor the whole inn. A protective effect applies within its ordinary scope and duration. Once outside the inn’s influence, an extracted guest recovers; returning uses the entry rules unless encounter immunity was earned.

## Restoration

A genuine welcome followed by freely chosen departure and willing release makes the false Context untenable. End the environmental influence without another truth-check roll. Maelyn’s participation is a strong route, not a compulsory key. The Offering Chest is not a destroyable off switch.

The first crowded page opens.

The tunnel disappears.

Darkwing runs through an early Colony under Chelish oppression.

Buildings burn.

People are dragged through streets.

Devils lead prisoners in chains.

Infernal forces fire from:

- windows;
- balconies;
- alleys;
- barricades.

This happened.

## Terrain — Burning Streets

Scatter:

> **Burning Rubble — difficult terrain**

The first time each round a creature ends its turn in Burning Rubble:

> **2d6 fire damage**

The Echo ignores this terrain.

## Memory Pressure — Infernal Volley

At:

> **initiative 10**

every Darkwing PC and every exposed Dawn victim attempts:

> **DC 35 basic Reflex**

**Damage:** 4d8 piercing/fire

**Critical Success:** no damage.  
**Success:** half damage.  
**Failure:** full damage.  
**Critical Failure:** double damage, **blinded until the end of the creature's next turn**, and **2d6 persistent fire damage**.

The volley is the Memory attacking.

Individual historical shooters do not require creature stat blocks.

Cover applies when the current terrain supports it.

## Projected Ward Exception

The Ward is suppressed by control of two Available outer stations, as defined in :ref[The Shape of Someone]{#encounter.the-shape-of-someone}. While projected, ordinary damage cannot reduce it. Entering the warded center triggers the Source’s Backlash; on a failed save the entrant is pushed to the nearest legal space outside. This exception replaces ordinary Ward damage during the rite.

After three Releases the same Weight becomes formless. Guiding it back and receiving the truth of the projecting community turns this vessel into an Anchor. Its :ref[Keystone role]{#rule.keystones} remains Form.

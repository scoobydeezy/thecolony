## Definition

**Weight.** Memory has weight. Mnemonic Weight can reside in people, objects, places, records, institutions, engrams or relationships. Most Weight is dormant: it exists without producing an Incursion because nothing is recognizing it strongly enough.

**Source.** A Source is the current vessel or locus carrying unresolved mnemonic Weight relevant to an encounter. A Source may be an object; a creature or living person; a room, building, grave or district; a record, ledger or engram; a civic or institutional system; or several linked vessels acting as one distributed Source. The Source does not contain a ghost. It carries Weight.

**Vessel.** The Vessel is the physical thing (object, body, place or system) that is currently the Source. *Source* is a mechanical role, not a category of thing: the same Vessel can be an ordinary possession, then a Source, then a contained Source, and later an Anchor. The Weight's Source Level, Class, current Reconstruction Pressure and Restored Identity belong to the Weight, not to the Vessel (see :ref[Source Transfer]{#rule.source-transfer}).

**Retained and supplied Weight.** Most Sources *retain* Weight from lives, events or relationships that came before the Vessel's current role (a toy retains the Weight of a relationship). A Source can also accumulate Weight *supplied* to the Vessel itself by generations of directed attention, ritual, hope, fear or representation. The difference is provenance only; both obey the same rules.

**Recognition and Resonance.** Weight destabilizes when something recognizes it. Mechanically this state is **Resonance**. Recognition does not require conscious recollection: a person can resonate with an object without knowing why it matters.

**Resonance fields.** Every Source that currently supports an Incursion defines three fields:

- **Resonance Type:** Relationship, Pattern, Choice or Form.
- **Incursion Trigger:** the condition that causes the first manifestation.
- **Reconstruction Condition:** what must remain true, or become true again, for a Dispersed Incursion to reconstruct.

The Trigger and the Reconstruction Condition may be identical, but need not be: a first manifestation can require stronger Recognition than later reconstructions, which follow a pathway reality has already established. Write both as concrete, observable, manipulable game-state rules.

A raw **Scar** is the exception: it is a Source with no Incursion Trigger or Reconstruction Condition until a specific topology develops (see :ref[Source Destruction]{#rule.source-destruction}).

**Resonance Break.** An encounter may define a **Resonance Break** response: what the current, still-manifested Incursion does when the Recognition that formed it is removed (it may become more dangerous, fragment, panic, collapse inward or lose abilities).

## Procedure

1. **Before the first Incursion.** While the Incursion Trigger has not been satisfied, no reconstruction begins. Preventing the Trigger prevents the Incursion.
2. **When the Trigger is satisfied,** the Incursion manifests and the Source becomes active (see :ref[Active Sources]{#rule.active-sources}).
3. **Breaking the Reconstruction Condition while the Incursion is manifested:**
   - the current Incursion normally stays manifested: an active reconstruction persists until it is Dispersed, Stabilized or ended by encounter-specific rules (such as a Resonance Break response);
   - the Source stays active, Warded and mnemonically heavy while that Incursion persists;
   - any encounter-specific Resonance Break response occurs now;
   - after Dispersal, no Reconstruction Pressure begins unless the condition becomes true again (see :ref[The Stabilization Window]{#rule.stabilization-window}).
4. **Death does not break Resonance by itself.** Resonance does not depend on Hit Points. Check what the Reconstruction Condition actually says: a condition based on active fear may end when a creature is unconscious, but a condition based on *this person is the mother* stays true if the mother dies.
5. **Topology follows position and relationship.** Whenever a Source moves, changes Vessel or gains new neighbours, re-check its Trigger and Reconstruction Condition.

## Examples

In :ref[Bad Maps]{#encounter.bad-maps}, the Source is a worn cloth toy carrying the retained Weight of :ref[Gressa Peakwalker]{#npc.gressa-peakwalker}'s relationship with her forgotten child. Its Resonance Type is Relationship. The Incursion Trigger is *Gressa physically holds the cloth toy*; the Reconstruction Condition is *Gressa remains within 30 feet of the current Source*.

Carrying the toy more than 30 feet from Gressa while the baby is manifested does not Disperse the baby. It triggers the baby's Resonance Break response (Tantrum) and means that, once the baby is Dispersed, no Reconstruction Pressure begins. If Gressa dies, the relationship *this is the mother* remains true, so killing her does not break Resonance.

## Interactions

- Breaking Resonance is containment logic, not remembering correctly. It prevents the next reconstruction; it does not Stabilize anything.
- A Source can support more than one Resonance profile over time. Changing its Vessel, environment, relationships or institutional treatment can expose a different true connection and a different Incursion.
- The Source's Class and Source Level are described in :ref[Class]{#rule.class}.

**Vessel, Resonance and Reconstruction Condition:** A worn birthing basin at the reservoir edge; Echo / Relationship. Separation of the fry from their Mother recognizes incomplete birth.

The registered signature is already active at Totality. Follow :ref[The Breach]{#encounter.the-breach} for the facility’s cell-bound return exception. Dispersal never teleports this vessel or changes its Class. The underlying biographies are not automatically known from classification; this impulse alone is not a complete Restoration.

### Fry Circuit — One Source, Thousands of Bodies
The fry are tiny black aquatic creatures within physically real water in EGRESS's transparent conduits. They are **conserved distributed expressions of the Mother's existing Weight**; the GM does not roll thousands of independent creature turns. If pipes rupture, a localized surge can be treated as difficult terrain or a **DC 33 Reflex** hazard dealing **4d6 bludgeoning** as bodies and water burst out, once per incident. The fry **do not seek to attack PCs** and do not create new Source vessels by existing or being harmed.

**Before Restoration:** The return reservoir is empty; the network circulates fry through industrial containment loops without a complete maternal return. The fry press toward their Mother, progressively interrupting field regulation. The Mother remains in endless labor. EGRESS believes redirecting the fry into the reservoir will remove them from the regulated conduit network and destroy the field.

### Return Plumbing — Concrete Player Actions
There are two necessary controls, both physically accessible outside the Scar Boundary and distinguishable by pipe labels and sightlines:

1. **Open Reservoir Return ◆** (**INTERACT**) at the large reservoir valve — opens the water/fry route back into the Mother's reservoir.
2. **Reverse One-Way Circulation ◆** (**INTERACT**) at the conduit manifold — changes the one-way collection pattern into a **continuous circuit through the reservoir** and back out through existing regulated conduits.

The controls do **not** require a successful skill check while intact. If one was visibly damaged, repairing it is a **two-action Crafting check, DC 33** (or another credible PF2e method); success restores its operation, failure costs the attempt without resetting the other control, critical failure creates one immediate pipe-surge hazard but does not destroy the Mother's Source. An operator may attempt to undo a control under ordinary action economy while following EGRESS's stated safety instructions; players can talk them down, block access, or work around them without a compulsory Diplomacy gate.

When both controls are correctly set, allow **one full round of visibly returning fry** before the new circulation settles. Characters see the black offspring swim into the reservoir; the Mother recognizes them, her screaming subsides, and her false **endless-labor** Reconstruction becomes untenable. This is **truthful Relationship Restoration**, achieved by topology rather than a hidden biography or a redundant skill roll.

### After Return — Persistent Brood and Anchor
The Mother's unresolved birthing-basin Source becomes the **truthful Anchor** for her now-bounded maternal Relationship, linked to the real reservoir and to the already-existing brood. The **false labor Echo ceases to reconstruct** and no further fry appear. **The children already produced persist** as coherent, physically manifest expressions within this one **Stabilized, Anchored distributed maternal topology**. This is an explicitly local Resolution state, **not** a universal rule that all offspring of Dispersed Incursions survive. The Mother's stabilized presence may remain as a benign recognizable manifestation; it is no longer an aggressive creature to fight. No extra Weight is created, duplicated, destroyed, or freely transferable by the change.

With continuous circulation restored, the same existing fry can pass through the reservoir and the engineered boundary conduits. EGRESS's Scar-regulation infrastructure therefore becomes functional again **without requiring continued childbirth**. This recovers emergency containment capacity; it does not by itself resolve the ethics of using the brood or the historical provenance of the Mother's full life.

The Colony's people connect its mysteries to ordinary needs. Begin with a relationship the Party can understand: Vara and the people caring for her, Gressa and the child she cannot remember, an administrator trying to reconcile a debt, or a reformer trying to keep a promise.

The first circle gathers around :ref[Vara]{#npc.vara-cairn}, :ref[Uldren]{#npc.uldren-harthwright}, and :ref[Celia]{#npc.celia-harthwright}. Civic authority brings :ref[Serevan]{#npc.serevan-ilvar} and :ref[Kelda]{#npc.kelda-varruk} into view. :ref[Perrik]{#npc.perrik-tallow} connects commercial anomalies to people whose obligations remain. Later investigations widen those relationships into the city's factions and neighborhoods.

Use each person's entry for their knowledge, motives, and responses. Membership explains a person's community, not every choice they will make. A change of allegiance, a death, or a new friendship should alter how you use this directory in play.

:::secret
This is a GM directory. It includes Forgotten people and hidden identities. A starting faction does not mean a person is alive, remembered, available, or publicly known to belong to it; read the starting state and the person's entry before placing them in a scene.
:::

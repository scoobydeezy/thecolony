## Definition

**Controlled routing** is EGRESS's technique for deliberately transferring unresolved Weight without causing Mnemonic Rupture, but only after the phenomenon is sufficiently classified. It is a :ref[Source Transfer]{#rule.source-transfer} technique, not Resolution. The general limits of containment, including Capacity and routed containment cells, belong to :ref[Containment]{#rule.containment}.

## Procedure

1. **Classify.** Before routing can begin, EGRESS must have:
   * a measured **Source Class**;
   * an identified **Resonance profile** for the Incursion being routed;
   * a prepared destination with **Capacity at least equal to Class**;
   * an engineered topology compatible with that Resonance.
2. **Route.** The required apparatus, number of actions, checks and hazards belong to the encounter using the technique; establish them before the transfer begins. Any check follows :ref[Source Transfer]{#rule.source-transfer}.
3. **Complete.** On successful completion, the Weight transfers without a Rupture Pulse. Unless deliberately transformed, it preserves **Source Level, Class, restored Identity, and current stabilization state**.

## Interactions

- A successful transfer does not supply missing Identity or Consent.
- A routed destination is Contained, not Anchored, unless it meets the requirements of :ref[Anchoring and Resolution]{#rule.anchoring-and-resolution}.
- Holding a known signature in place without moving the Weight is :ref[Tuned Suppression]{#rule.tuned-suppression}, not routing.

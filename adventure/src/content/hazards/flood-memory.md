At 180 HP:

> *page.*

The battlefield disappears beneath the River Foam.

Buildings vanish into brown water.

Roofs tear free.

Whole homes move.

People cling to:

- chimneys;
- boats;
- beams;
- trees;
- one another.

Bodies and possessions move with the current.

## Terrain — Flooded Colony

Most ground is difficult terrain.

The abstraction represents:

- waist-deep water;
- mud;
- wreckage;
- unstable structures;
- floating debris.

Characters with relevant movement abilities benefit normally, subject to Dream Pace.

The Echo ignores Memory-generated difficult terrain.

## Memory Pressure — River Surge

At initiative 10:

every creature except the Echo attempts:

> **DC 35 basic Reflex**

**Damage:** 5d8 bludgeoning

**Critical Success:** no damage.  
**Success:** half damage.  
**Failure:** full damage and pushed **10 feet backward** relative to pursuit.  
**Critical Failure:** double damage, pushed **20 feet backward**, knocked prone, and **fatigued**.

Fatigued from this Memory persists after the scene unless removed normally.

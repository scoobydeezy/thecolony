## Opening State

The Witness route leads to a preserved house whose custodians know that moving the last correctly placed object is dangerous. Establish the warning before presenting the valuable door knocker.

## History and Custody

### The Veiled House
Before the House of Abiding existed, Called Keepers remained in their ordinary homes.

One early Keeper who lived here was a committed member of the **Order of Theia** and an outspoken opponent of the early Witness movement. They believed the Witnesses' insistence on preserving uncertain relationships between objects, places, and lives threatened the Colony's ability to contain mnemonic danger at all.

Their surviving writings argue, in substance:

> The Witnesses mistake attachment for evidence.

They expected their own house to be cleared according to normal Order procedure after their Reading.

They completed that Reading.

The Colony forgot them.

The house did not.

The Order followed the procedure the Keeper themselves had endorsed:

- resonant possessions were inventoried;
- dangerous or uncertain objects were removed;
- materials were separated by category and placed into custody;
- household arrangements were treated as incidental rather than evidentiary.

Each intervention made the manifestations worse.

The early Witnesses argued that the Order was destroying information by stripping true objects from the circumstances in which their meaning had existed. Eventually the Order suspended extraction and allowed Witness custodians to return objects and preserve the site in place.

That worked.

The Veiled House became a major institutional turning point. The Order did not absorb the Witnesses as an ordinary branch, but it stopped treating them as simple opposition and granted them **bounded, sanctioned custodial authority** over Fractured Objects, provenance, and mnemonic sites. The modern Witnesses became a form of recognized controlled opposition within the Colony's preservation system.

The compromise later helped produce the Hall of Remembrance and the House of Abiding.

The irony survives in the house:

> An Order Keeper who regarded the Witnesses as an existential danger became the evidence that forced the Order to preserve their method.

### The Last Correct Context
Over the years, five important household objects were displaced into the rooms with which later custodians correctly associated them.

Only one relationship remained intact.

A **Greater Insistent Door Knocker** remained mounted on the Hearth threshold historically associated with the Parlor.

That single surviving placement did not restore the house. It merely kept the Wound from fully opening.

The Witnesses learned an empirical rule:

> **Do not move the thing that is still where it belongs.**

They do not necessarily understand the full mechanism.

Darkwing does not share generations of institutional caution around an obviously valuable magical item.

Removing the Knocker destroys the final surviving Context relationship and activates the Wound.

## Exploring Before the Rupture

Permit ordinary observation and questions. The Witnesses can describe their empirical caution without knowing the complete six-slot solution. Taking the Knocker is a possible player action, not a mandatory cutscene. If Darkwing respects the warning, keep the site as evidence and allow a deliberate, informed investigation.

The caretaker’s former opposition to the Witnesses is a discovery in the records. It does not make modern Celia omniscient about the correct arrangement.

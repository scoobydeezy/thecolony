The Party learns to replace a false reconstruction with enough truth that reality can stop guessing. The seven components of Identity name different kinds of evidence, not seven scores every encounter must fill. :ref[Identity and Stabilization]{#rule.identity-and-stabilization} owns their definitions and the Recover, Restore, and Acceptance procedures; :ref[Reconstruction and Resonance Paths]{#rule.reconstruction-and-resonance} owns which components each kind of Veiled can be Restored through.

## What the Campaign Teaches

| Component | The question to make concrete | Where it is taught |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Which individual life does this evidence belong to? | :ref[Old Town]{#encounter.old-town} distinguishes six lives instead of treating a crowd as interchangeable. |
| **Relationship** | Who and what mattered to this person? | :ref[A Life]{#encounter.a-life} follows the cloth toy beyond the mother-and-infant fragment. |
| **Pattern** | What obligations and material consequences did this life leave? | :ref[The Clearing House]{#location.clearing-house} and :ref[Claims Registry]{#asset.claims-registry} trace dependencies, debts, and ownership. |
| **Memory** | What did the person experience? | :ref[Playing for Keeps]{#encounter.playing-for-keeps} distinguishes authentic experienced Memory from a complete identity. |
| **Form** | Which outward features and boundaries truly belong? | :ref[The Shape of Someone]{#encounter.the-shape-of-someone} separates truthful Form from what observers supply. |
| **Context** | When and where was this true? | :ref[Witness Me]{#encounter.witness-me} restores the circumstances that make preserved facts meaningful. |
| **Choice** | What did someone choose, refuse, or have imposed upon them? | :ref[Revolutionaries]{#encounter.revolutionaries} recovers agency rather than substituting the outcome for consent. |

## Evidence Before Vocabulary

Let players handle a tool, hear a repeated phrase, notice a repair, or compare records before asking them to classify what it means. They need a reasonable question to pursue. They do not need to announce the name of an Identity component before a useful action counts.

An Engram can faithfully preserve what someone remembered or believed without establishing that every remembered claim was true (see :ref[Engrams, Frames and Proxies]{#rule.corrupted-engrams}). A record of obligation can establish Pattern without recovering affection. A portrait can supply Form without explaining the circumstances in which it was painted. Bring different evidence into conversation rather than allowing one source to answer every question.

## Knowing and Restoring

Knowing the truth and making the reconstruction incorporate it are distinct achievements. Show why an opening is needed, then make the effect of Restoration visible. A correct Name should constrain ownership; a recovered Choice should change what the manifestation can credibly impose.

Restored progress survives later reconstructions. The campaign's layered encounters become more truthful as the Party works; they do not secretly reset the answer between phases. Follow the owning :ref[Identity rule]{#rule.identity-and-stabilization} and the encounter's particular openings.

## Optional Lessons, Required Local Evidence

The Manor routes are opportunities. Players may skip lessons, lose allies, or arrive without a reusable tool. Prepare a local route to every component the encounter requires. Earlier experience can make that route faster, safer, or recognizable; it must not be the only way to proceed.

Six :ref[Identity Keystones]{#rule.keystones} support Name, Relationship, Pattern, Memory, Form, and Choice. The Dawn Charter supports Choice; Context has no Keystone and must be recovered from the circumstances themselves. The Keystone rule owns the complete mapping and access limits.

For recurring tools and rewards, see [Character Progression and Rewards](/docs/running/progression-and-rewards/). Truthful reconstruction answers who a person was; :ref[Anchoring]{#rule.anchoring-and-resolution} asks where their Weight can now rest.

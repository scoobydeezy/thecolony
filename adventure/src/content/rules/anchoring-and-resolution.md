## Definition

Stabilization establishes who the Forgotten person is truthfully enough that reality can stop guessing. **Anchoring** establishes where their surviving Weight can rest.

An **Anchor** is a role, not a special class of magical object. It can be the original Source, another object, a willing person, several carriers, a relationship, a household, a place, a memorial, a record, or a civic institution. A distributed network can carry Weight when the actual connections support it.

The original Source can become its own Anchor. It now carries sufficient truthful Meaning, so its genuine connection no longer forces reality to invent a false person.

### Requirements

| Requirement | Question | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Connection** | Does the destination have a truthful relationship to the recovered Identity? | Ownership, use, place, relationship, memorial purpose, inherited obligation, recovered history, or deliberate civic recognition can connect it. An arbitrary empty vessel does not suffice. |
| **Capacity** | Can it safely carry the Class of Weight assigned to it? | Capacity uses the same I–V scale as Class. Willingness alone cannot provide Capacity. |
| **Consent** | Has a destination with agency agreed to carry this burden? | Capacity alone grants no authority to assign it. |

Where the fiction supports genuine distribution, Capacity can be added across Anchors unless the encounter establishes why the Weight cannot be safely partitioned. A forced transfer may be physically possible without being legitimate Anchoring; coercion can leave containment, injury, and unresolved responsibility.

## Procedure

### At Acceptance

If a valid Anchor is prepared when :ref[Acceptance]{#rule.identity-and-stabilization} completes, check Connection, Capacity, and Consent. The Weight settles automatically. Do not introduce another climactic roll after the work of reconstruction and acceptance.

If there is no valid Anchor, the hostile Incursion still ends. The Weight is **Stabilized but Unanchored**. It may remain a coherent presence, a non-hostile Source, a transport problem, a vulnerable burden, or pressure requiring temporary containment. This changes the problem; it does not declare the encounter failed or erase restored Identity.

### Resolution

An Incursion is **Resolved** when its false reconstruction is Stabilized, any necessary present-day reckoning has been addressed sufficiently for a truthful destination to exist, and the coherent Weight settles into a valid Anchor.

Record what changes among the living: a reclaimed history, an accepted responsibility, a transformed institution, a transfer of property, or a Source becoming an Anchor. :ref[Containment]{#rule.containment} can protect people while these questions remain open; it is not Resolution.

## Anchors by Resonance Type

Reconstruction determines how the false reconstruction is structurally wrong.

Resonance Type often suggests what kind of destination can truthfully carry the stabilized weight.

These are not rigid exclusivity rules. They are design guidance.

## Relationship — Reciprocal or Relational Anchors

Relationship resonances often settle most truthfully into:

- a surviving relationship;
- a household;
- a community;
- an object transformed into a coherent mnemonic object;
- a memorial explicitly preserving the connection.

The important question is:

> **Does the destination actually belong to this relationship, or is it merely available?**

## Pattern — Obligational Anchors

Pattern resonances often require the present world to accept the surviving consequence rather than merely remember the person.

Possible Anchors include:

- a current property owner;
- a guild;
- a civic office;
- an infrastructure authority;
- a household;
- an institution accepting inherited duty;
- a restitution or compensation system.

The relevant statement is not only:

> “We remember you.”

It may be:

> **“We accept responsibility for the thing your life left in ours.”**

## Choice — Accountable Anchors

Choice resonances often cannot be honestly Anchored into a private object while the living world continues the consequence unchanged.

A truthful destination may require:

- opened records;
- restored ownership;
- restitution;
- changed policy;
- public acknowledgment;
- an institution capable of preserving distinctions between victim, perpetrator, coercion, refusal, and complicity.

Recognition without material consequence can become ceremony.

## Form — Representational Anchors

Form resonances may settle through systems that preserve representation without allowing one imposed image to become the whole person.

Possible Anchors include:

- corrected public records;
- multiple preserved perspectives;
- a memorial acknowledging uncertainty;
- an archive with explicit provenance;
- the Face of the Unknown used without prescribing a single authoritative Form.

The goal is not perfect representation.

It is preventing one inherited image from swallowing the lives beneath it.

## Moral Range and Inherited Responsibility

Veiled encounters should recover people rather than moral abstractions.

The Forgotten are not purified by forgetting.

A recovered person may have been:

- kind;
- cruel;
- compromised;
- frightened;
- heroic;
- complicit;
- ordinary;
- contradictory.

Their testimony can explain what they believed without making that belief correct.

Most names gathered into the Book are likely to skew toward victims, witnesses, the disappeared, the unclaimed, people threatened with erasure, and later Keepers.

Perpetrators may still appear when their identity is necessary to preserve the truth of an event or consequence.

## Responsibility, Not Ancestral Guilt

The living do not inherit the personal guilt of the dead.

They inherit a world shaped by the dead and responsibility for consequences that remain active within it.

A present resident may not have committed the original harm but may still:

- live on seized property;
- benefit from forgotten labor;
- hold authority created by exclusion;
- continue an institution whose original purpose has been lost;
- possess objects or obligations detached from the people who created them.

Pattern and Choice encounters may therefore require material change before a truthful Anchor is available.

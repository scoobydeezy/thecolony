## Opening State

The investigation has weakened the official story. Uldren has taken Vara away to prepare for the Reading; the Party’s discussion happens in her absence.

## Dawn’s Offer

Dawn approaches after the Party’s investigation destabilizes the clean official story.

By this point Uldren has taken Vara away to prepare for the Reading. Miriel Stoutfoot and Julian Arrett speak openly about the Keeper system, Vara's coming sacrifice, and what intervention might mean.

Miriel does not tell the Party what conclusion to reach. She offers something more practical: access. Dawn can put the Party close enough to the procession and Ritual Chamber to observe what the Reading actually does and, if the Party decides intervention is necessary, to act.

During this conversation, record the Party’s **Agency Stance** according to how it talks about Vara when she is not present:

- **Agency-supportive:** Vara must remain the decision-maker even if Darkwing hopes she chooses differently.
- **Unresolved / procedural:** Darkwing focuses on evidence, access, or stopping institutional secrecy without taking a clear position on Vara's authority.
- **Agency-denying:** Vara's consent is dismissed; Darkwing speaks as though she must simply be rescued, overruled, or used as proof.

This state modifies how credible the Party’s later appeal is when it asks Vara to trust that its intervention is actually about her rather than another institution deciding for her.

Dawn will be publically protesting the Reading along the Ritual procession line. Julian will use this as a distraction to draw the Order’s attention in order to provide access to the Ritual Chamber for the Party.  Serevan, however, knows this, and will ask the Party to help him remove the protesters instead to avoid creating a political scene.  Both sides promise access to the Ritual Chamber as a reward. The Party must choose a side.

## Player Choices

Dawn offers access through a public protest and Julian’s distraction. Serevan offers access in return for removing the protesters. Present their concrete requests. Both promise access to the Ritual Chamber, and **the Party must choose a side**.

Record the Agency Stance from how the Party treats Vara’s authority, not from which faction it supports. Sympathy for Dawn alone does not establish respect for her consent.

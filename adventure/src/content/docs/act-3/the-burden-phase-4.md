## At the Table

**Objective:** restore four Keeper Companies as distinct lives, strip the final institutional claim, and Name Aletheia. **Track:** unresolved Companies, Institutional Class/Ward HP, Book held or dropped, Serevan’s actual HP, and Last Secret. **Loop:** break Ward → Backlash → Book drops → read Name → establish Named Boundary. **No full Ward reset after a Breach without the prescribed transition.**

Use the **contribution table in :ref[The Burden]{#encounter.the-burden}** to select full, damaged or absent capability before applying a faction’s phase procedure. The phase’s full-strength descriptions do not override a Damaged front.

## Identity
**Name**

## Faction socket
**Order of Theia**

## Arena

Present-day Crescent Island.

Darkwing is standing at the exact riverbank where Aletheia wrote her Name.

There is no more surreal arena transition.

They have reached the historical ground truth.

## Opening — The Choice Becomes an Institution

Phase III ends.

Aletheia is still a featureless black silhouette holding the real Book in exactly the place where Darkwing just witnessed her write her own Name.

She turns the next page.

A single Name.

A black Keeper appears.

She turns another.

Another Name.

Another Keeper.

After several pages:

> **the Book begins flipping forward by itself**

Keeper after Keeper appears.

Soon Crescent Island is full of black human figures.

Roughly:

> **two hundred Forgotten Keepers**

surround Darkwing.

They are distinct in:

- height;
- ancestry;
- age;
- posture;
- clothing silhouette;
- body type;

but remain unresolved Veiled forms.

Then Serevan steps out from among the shadows.

He does not charge.

He does not initially appear adversarial.

He approaches Aletheia.

And:

> **takes the Book gently from her hands.**

For a moment he may look at the nameless woman with genuine compassion, grief, recognition, or tenderness.

Then he steps in front of her.

The Book has physically moved:

> **from the hands of the woman who made one human choice**

into:

> **the hands of the institution that inherited it.**

That is the central visual of Phase IV.

A mnemonic shell forms around Serevan.

The Book recognizes him as capable of handling it.

That fact should be disturbing.

Serevan begins to levitate slightly above the ground.

Then he turns toward Darkwing.

---

## Serevan's Final Argument

Serevan does not rant.

He is:

- exhausted;
- furious;
- grieving;
- frightened;
- completely certain.

He speaks about:

- more than a century of keeping the Colony intact;
- Readings that genuinely restored stability;
- containment procedures that genuinely saved lives;
- people he has watched die when procedure failed;
- responsibilities Darkwing only recently discovered;
- systems Darkwing disrupted without understanding every load-bearing consequence.

Now the Colony is under Totality.

To Serevan:

> **this is not proof that the inherited answer failed**

It is proof that:

> **Darkwing did exactly the damage he warned them they would do.**

His conclusion:

> **They will do no more damage to his Colony.**

The raid begins.

## Interrupting the Interlude

There is no cutscene immunity.

If Darkwing attacks after Serevan takes the Book:

> **roll initiative**

The Institutional Ward is already active.

Ranged attacks hit the Ward.

Direct physical interference risks Mnemonic Backlash.

A Party member who attempts to physically take the Book discovers another crucial fact:

> **the Book rejects them but permits Serevan.**

The attempted handling fails and triggers the Book's Class V Keeper-Bound Backlash.

The Party may interrupt his speech.

They cannot bypass the encounter by stabbing him during it.

## Four Companies / Four Quarter-Moons

The roughly 200 unresolved Keepers organize into:

> **four Companies of approximately fifty**

This is table abstraction, not an in-world military structure.

Each Company occupies one **quarter-moon arc** around Serevan.

Together the four Companies form a broken circular host around him.

Each Company has one focal Keeper at ground level:

> **the Captain**

The Captain is not a commander.

It is the single Corpus through which that fifty-person block can be separated from the office.

The Companies resolve backward through Keeper history:

| Company | Captain |
|---|---|
| **I — Most Recent** | **Vara Cairn**, if Forgotten; otherwise **Marek Cairn** |
| **II** | **Pépé Sylvya** |
| **III** | **Willow Stoutfoot** |
| **IV — Oldest Company** | **Portia Harthwright** |
| **Final unresolved person** | **Aletheia** |

The four Captains remain visually distinct and accessible at the front of their quarter-moons.

---

## Companies Are Not Attackable Creatures

Attacks against the Companies do nothing permanent.

Darkwing may:

- Strike;
- cast AoEs;
- blast silhouettes;
- disrupt individual bodies.

They reform.

> **You cannot damage a category into becoming people.**

Companies:

- do not occupy individual tactical squares;
- do not provide flanking;
- do not block ordinary movement unless a specific shield effect says otherwise;
- do not roll initiative;
- do not make ordinary attacks;
- do not make individual saves.

Their mechanics are:

1. **Chorus Pressure**
2. **Institutional Power**
3. **Succession Intercession** during Book windows

---

## Chorus Pressure

At the start of every round, before normal turns:

> **every Darkwing PC attempts one Will save**

The number of unresolved Companies determines the Pressure.

| Unresolved Companies | Pressure | Will DC | Basic mental damage |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 4 | 35 | 4d8 |
| 3 | 3 | 35 | 3d8 |
| 2 | 2 | 35 | 2d8 |
| 1 | 1 | 35 | 1d8 |
| 0 | 0 | — | none |

Additional effects:

- **Failure:** stupefied 1 until the start of the next round.
- **Critical Failure:** stupefied 2 until the start of the next round.

The Keepers are not intentionally attacking.

The damage is the unbearable pressure of two centuries of lives being treated as one institutional identity.

Every Company resolution makes the island quieter.

---

## Institutional Power

The unresolved Companies extend the Book's mnemonic protection around Serevan while he carries it.

This encounter-specific protection is:

> **the Institutional Ward**

The Book itself remains a:

> **Class V Source**

throughout Phase IV.

The Institutional Ward has an effective Class determined by unresolved Companies.

> **Institutional Class = unresolved Companies + 1**

Minimum:

> **Class I**

| Unresolved Companies | Institutional Class |
|---:|---:|
| 4 | V |
| 3 | IV |
| 2 | III |
| 1 | II |
| 0 | I |

The final Class I represents:

> **Serevan himself as the last surviving institutional claim**

after every Company has been individualized.

---

## Institutional Ward

While Serevan holds the Book:

> **attacks and damaging effects against Serevan damage the Institutional Ward instead of Serevan**

Use Serevan's normal:

- AC;
- saves;
- defenses

to determine whether effects succeed.

Damage goes to Ward HP.

Damage does not overflow into Serevan.

Non-damage conditions can affect him normally when appropriate.

Darkwing can therefore still:

- frighten;
- stupefy;
- penalize Will;
- debuff;
- set up the coming Backlash.

## Institutional Backlash

When an Institutional Ward reaches 0 HP:

> **it Breaches**

Use the current Institutional Class:

| Class | Backlash DC | Backlash |
|---|---:|---:|
| V | 39 | 5d12 mental |
| IV | 38 | 4d10 mental |
| III | 37 | 3d8 mental |
| II | 36 | 2d6 mental |
| I | 35 | 1d4 mental |

The creature whose damage reduced the Ward to 0 is the:

> **triggering attacker**

Serevan immediately attempts the Backlash save.

---

## The Backlash Duel

The universal **Mnemonic Backlash Reflection** rule creates a deliberate Link-and-Ganondorf exchange here.

### Step 1 — Serevan rolls

Serevan attempts the current basic Will save.

#### If he does not critically succeed

He takes the appropriate Backlash damage.

The reflection chain ends on Serevan.

He:

> **drops the Book**

#### If he critically succeeds

He takes no damage.

The Backlash reflects into the triggering attacker.

### Step 2 — The attacker rolls

The triggering attacker attempts the same basic Will save against the same DC and same rolled Backlash damage.

#### If the attacker does not critically succeed

The attacker takes the appropriate damage.

The reflection chain ends on the attacker.

Serevan:

> **keeps the Book**

The Institutional Ward remains:

> **Breached at 0 HP**

#### If the attacker critically succeeds

The Backlash reflects back into Serevan.

Serevan rolls again.

Continue:

> **Serevan → attacker → Serevan → attacker**

until one participant fails to critically succeed.

This is intentionally dramatic.

It is not a separate minigame.

It is the universal Backlash reflection rule happening between two creature endpoints.

---

## Breached Ward — If Serevan Keeps the Book

If the reflection chain ends on the attacker:

- Serevan keeps the Book;
- Ward HP remains 0;
- the Ward does not refill.

Every later successful damaging hit against Serevan while the Ward is Breached:

> **starts a new Institutional Backlash chain**

instead of dealing HP damage.

The next attacker becomes the new triggering attacker.

Darkwing never has to chew through the same Ward twice.

---

## Serevan Cannot Die Before the Institution Is Gone

While any Institutional Ward remains unresolved—including a Breached Ward whose Book has not yet been dropped:

> **Serevan cannot be reduced below 1 HP**

by Institutional Backlash or any other encounter effect.

This is not regeneration.

It is the unresolved succession continuing to preserve the person currently carrying its office.

Excess damage is lost.

The protection ends only after:

> **the final Class I Institutional Ward is broken and cannot reform**

At that point Serevan's HP floor disappears.

If Serevan is reduced to 0 HP during the final exposed confrontation:

> **his Last Secret resolves before he becomes unable to speak**

so the campaign cannot softlock because Darkwing hit too hard.

---

## Direct Contact

Trying to physically:

- Grapple Serevan;
- Reposition him;
- seize him;
- touch the active mnemonic shell

subjects the acting creature to the current Institutional Backlash once per turn.

Trying to physically take the Book invokes the Book's intrinsic Keeper-Bound rule:

> **:stat{#source.book-of-names key=backlash}**

and the attempted handling fails unless the Book recognizes that creature as a Keeper.

Serevan's ability to handle it remains deliberately unexplained during the raid.

---

## Book Drop

When a Backlash chain ends on Serevan:

- Serevan takes the resulting damage;
- Serevan involuntarily drops the Book;
- the Institutional Ward remains Breached;
- the Book lands open nearby;
- the Book's own Class V Source Ward remains intact;
- one Name-page is visible.

Serevan is briefly staggered by the Backlash.

But he is not suddenly vulnerable to focus fire.

That is where the Host intervenes.

---

## Succession Intercession — The Host Shields Serevan

While the Book is dropped and at least one Company remains unresolved:

> **all unresolved Companies rise into their quarter-moon formations around Serevan**

The four arcs become a rotating shield of black Keeper silhouettes.

The Captains remain at ground level and visibly distinct.

During this Book Window:

- Serevan cannot be targeted by attacks or hostile effects;
- attacks directed at him vanish into the unresolved Host and have no effect;
- Serevan cannot reclaim the Book by his own actions;
- the Institutional Ward stays Breached;
- Chorus Pressure continues at the start of each round.

The encounter is now visually saying:

> **Stop hitting Serevan.**

> **The succession itself is protecting him.**

> **Use the open Book.**

This closes the raid's dropped-Book ambiguity.

Darkwing cannot accidentally spend the Book Window deleting Serevan's HP.

The way forward is the Name mechanic.

---

## Read the Open Page ◆

**CONCENTRATE, LINGUISTIC**

**Requirements:**

- you are within 10 feet of the dropped Book;
- you can see the open page;
- you can read the Name.

No physical contact is required.

You read the visible Name aloud.

No check.

No Backlash.

Immediately:

> **one Captain turns their head toward the speaker**

The association is obvious.

No Recall Knowledge.

No silhouette-identification puzzle.

The GM asks:

> **“Who were they?”**

The player answers with the Name.

That is the dunk.

---

## Restore Named Boundary — No Roll

Once a player truthfully identifies:

> **this Name belongs to this Keeper**

the entire Company resolves.

The Captain becomes corporeal.

Then the next Keeper.

Then another.

The change races through roughly fifty people.

That Company immediately:

- stops contributing Chorus Pressure;
- stops contributing Institutional Power;
- leaves the shielding quarter-moon;
- joins the quiet Host around the battlefield.

One correct Named Boundary invalidates the Company's false premise:

> **These fifty people are interchangeable occupants of one office.**

---

## Reclaim the Book

After a Company resolves:

- its quarter-moon shield disperses into distinct people;
- the Book slams shut;
- the remaining unresolved succession recognizes Serevan again;
- the Book rises through mnemonic force into his hands;
- the next lower-Class Institutional Ward forms at full Ward HP.

Serevan does not spend an action retrieving it.

The unresolved institution is reclaiming the Book through him.

After Company IV resolves:

> the Book returns one last time

because Serevan himself remains the final institutional claim.

After the Class I Ward breaks:

> **there is no unresolved Company left to shield or reclaim it**

## Final Class I Breach

Break the Class I Ward.

Resolve the Backlash Duel.

- Serevan keeps the Book;
- Ward remains Breached at 0;

- he drops the Book;

## Serevan's Last Secret

At some earlier point in his stewardship, Serevan violated the deepest boundary of his office.

He opened the Book.

He did not complete a Reading.

He read:

> **Aletheia's page**

The Book showed him enough of her life to know why she chose what she chose.

His error was not ignorance.

His error was turning:

> **This was Aletheia's answer**

into:

> **Therefore this must remain everyone's answer.**

His ability to physically handle the Book is evidence that something about that illicit encounter caused the Book to recognize him in a way Darkwing does not yet understand.

Do not answer the entire metaphysical history during the raid.

The mystery itself is valuable.

> **Kelda**

Once:

- all four Companies are resolved;
- the final Class I Ward is gone;

the secret is no longer containable.

> **resolve the Last Secret before he becomes unable to speak**

He does not have to agree with Darkwing.

He does not have to repent.

## Why Guessing Aletheia Early Is Not Enough

Darkwing may infer:

> **Order of Theia → Aletheia**

before Serevan yields.

They may say the word.

They may be right.

But a correct guessed string is not yet a Recovered Name.

Before the Last Secret:

> **Aletheia is a hypothesis**

After Serevan truthfully connects:

- the Name;
- the Book page;
- the woman;
- his firsthand Recognition;

the Named Boundary has the evidentiary support reality needs.

The game does not tell clever players they were wrong.

It tells them:

> **you knew the answer before you possessed the truth that could make it stick.**

## Why the Final Mechanic Is Identical

The campaign does not end by proving Aletheia cosmically more important than every later Keeper.

It ends by applying the same rule to her:

> **This Name belongs to this life.**

Marek receives that dignity.

Vara can receive it.

Pépé receives it.

Willow receives it.

Portia receives it.

Two hundred ordinary Forgotten Keepers receive it.

Then:

> **Aletheia receives exactly the same thing.**

The First Keeper ceases to exist as a privileged mechanical category.

Aletheia becomes a person.

## Phase IV Thematic Resolution

Phase III showed:

> **what one woman experienced and chose**

Phase IV begins by showing:

> **what an institution did with that choice**

Serevan taking the Book is the institutionalization of Aletheia's act made visible.

Darkwing then dismantles that institution:

> **one person at a time**

The quarter-moon shields disappear.

The pressure drops.

The people remain.

At the end:

- the Companies are gone;
- the hostile Chorus is gone;
- Serevan cannot contain the Name;
- Aletheia receives the same identity boundary as everyone else.

The Book remains.

The Weight remains.

Totality remains.

The final campaign question becomes:

> **Where should all of this rest now?**

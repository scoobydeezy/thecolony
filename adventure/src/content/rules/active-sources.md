## Definition

A Source is **active** while its Incursion is manifested, even if its Reconstruction Condition has since been broken. An active object Source has two universal properties:

- **Mnemonic Weight** makes it physically difficult to move.
- A **Mnemonic Ward** protects its Vessel and deals **Mnemonic Backlash** when touched.

**Mnemonic Weight.** While an object Source is active, its effective Bulk is the greater of its normal Bulk or **10 × C Bulk** (see :ref[Class]{#rule.class}). This weight is literal. It belongs to the active pressure, not the Ward, so it remains while the Source is Breached. For creature- and location-Sources, use the encounter's movement rules instead of literal Bulk.

**Mnemonic Ward.** Every active Source is protected by a Ward generated by the current reconstruction. The Ward has AC equal to the Source DC (unless the encounter says otherwise), **10 × (Source Level + C)** HP, no Hardness and no Broken Threshold. For a location-Source, the encounter defines which boundary or **Ward focus** bears the Ward; all foci of one location share one HP pool, one Breached state, one Source DC and one Backlash. Touching an ordinary interior surface that is not Ward-bearing does not cause Backlash.

**Mnemonic Backlash.** Class-derived mental damage, **C d(2C + 2)**, with a basic Will save against the Source DC. Backlash makes no attack roll.

**Breached.** A Source whose Ward has been reduced to 0 HP is Breached for the rest of that manifestation.

## Procedure

**Touching an active Source.**

1. The first time in each turn that a creature directly touches a Source whose Ward has at least 1 HP, that creature attempts a basic Will save against the Source DC and takes the Source's Backlash damage (critical success none, success half, failure full, critical failure double).
2. Attacking the Ward without direct physical contact does not cause Backlash. A Breached or Dispersed Source causes none, because no Ward is present.
3. **Reflection.** On a critical success, the creature takes no damage and the Backlash reflects toward the opposite endpoint of the connection. Roll the Backlash damage once, when the chain begins, and reuse it.
   - If the opposite endpoint is a creature, it attempts the same basic Will save against the same DC. On a critical success it takes nothing and reflects the Backlash again; otherwise it takes the basic-save damage and the chain ends.
   - Unless the encounter defines another endpoint, direct Source-contact Backlash reflects toward the Source Vessel. A living (creature) Source uses the creature-endpoint rule above. If the Vessel is a **non-creature** Source, the reflection travels inward: the full rolled damage is dealt directly to the Vessel, bypassing the Ward. If that destroys the Vessel or leaves it unable to carry the Weight, resolve :ref[Rupture]{#rule.source-destruction}.
   - Reflection applies only to actual Mnemonic Backlash, or an effect the encounter explicitly defines as using the Backlash circuit.
   - The Book of Names is an exception: a reflection chain that reaches it ends without damage or Rupture (see :ref[Book of Names — Keeper-Bound Handling]{#rule.book.keeper-bound-handling}).

**Attacking the Source.**

1. Damage aimed at a Warded Source is applied to the Ward, not the Vessel. Damage beyond the Ward's remaining HP does not carry through to the Vessel.
2. When Ward HP reaches 0, the Ward collapses and the Source is **Breached**. While Breached:
   - the Vessel can be damaged or manipulated normally, subject to Mnemonic Weight;
   - direct contact no longer causes Backlash;
   - Mnemonic Weight remains;
   - Resonance and the current Incursion are otherwise unchanged.
3. A Breach allows damaging the Vessel, attempting a forced :ref[Transfer]{#rule.source-transfer}, attaching containment or routing equipment, and handling the Vessel without Backlash, all while the Incursion is still active. A Breach does **not** open a Stabilization Window and does not remove Mnemonic Weight.

**Moving an active object Source.**

1. To move it directly, use the PF2e **Reposition** action: an Athletics check against the Source DC, with Reposition's normal degrees of success and displacement. Doing it by hand is direct contact.
2. Indirect movement is valid if the method can actually move the Source's effective Bulk: a strong cart or platform; ropes, hooks or poles (Reposition without direct contact); machinery already supporting it; or a spell or effect able to move that Bulk.
3. However it is moved, the Source's position genuinely changes. Topology follows position: re-check its Resonance.

**When the Incursion Disperses** the Ward is absent, Backlash stops, and Mnemonic Weight is suppressed: the Vessel uses its ordinary Bulk while it remains safely exposed. If the Incursion reconstructs, a fresh Ward forms at full HP around the current Vessel and Mnemonic Weight returns at once. Transfer never refreshes a collapsed Ward.

## Examples

In :ref[Bad Maps]{#encounter.bad-maps}, the Class II cloth toy is active while the baby is manifested. Grabbing it means a basic Will save against the Source DC for 2d6 mental Backlash, once per creature per turn while the Ward stands. Attacks on the toy strike the Ward (10 × (Source Level + 2) HP, AC equal to the Source DC). If the Ward collapses the toy is Breached: it can be handled without Backlash and the toy itself can be damaged, but it still weighs 20 Bulk and the baby is still fully manifested. A character who critically succeeds against the toy's Backlash throws it back into the toy, which can destroy it and cause a Rupture.

## Interactions

- Breaking Resonance while the Incursion is active leaves the Source active, Warded and heavy (:ref[Sources and Resonance]{#rule.sources-and-resonance}).
- Dispersal is the safer way to expose the Source (:ref[Dispersal]{#rule.dispersal}).

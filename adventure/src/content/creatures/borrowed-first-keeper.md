## Composite Hand
The Composite Hand Strike remains available regardless of which Form families have been removed.
The borrowed Forms make the Composite more capable.
They do not grant it the basic ability to hurt someone.

## The Six Form Slots

The Composite has six named Form Slots:

1. **Assault**
2. **Control**
3. **Mobility**
4. **Guard**
5. **Influence**
6. **Sustain**

Each slot has:

- **Primary Form A**
- **Secondary Form B**

At initial reconstruction:

> **Primary Form A begins attached.**

The six Secondary Forms remain visibly present in the ruined chamber.

Baseline Composite therefore begins with:

> **6 active Form pieces**

and:

> **6 reserve Form pieces**

Darkwing should be able to see that the creature has been assembled from specific pieces of the room.

---

## Attached Form Pieces

Every attached Form piece is independently targetable.

**AC** 33  
**HP** 45  
**Hardness** —  
**Broken Threshold** —

An attached Form uses the Composite’s saves if an effect requires a save from the piece itself.

## Damage allocation

When an attack or damaging effect targets an attached Form:

- damage applies to that Form only;
- it does **not** reduce the Composite’s HP.

When an attack targets the Composite body:

- damage applies to Composite HP;
- it does **not** simultaneously damage attached Forms.

One AoE or multi-target effect cannot multiply damage by counting the Composite and several attached Forms as separate overlapping creatures unless the final implementation explicitly says otherwise.

This prevents body-part targeting from becoming a damage multiplier exploit.

---

## Dormant Forms

When an attached Form reaches 0 HP:

> **it is torn from the Composite and becomes Dormant.**

Represent that physically:

- remove its token;
- flip its card face-down;
- leave the relic as inert debris if desired.

A Dormant Form:

- grants no ability;
- cannot be used;
- cannot normally reattach;
- remains part of the chamber as inert material.

The corresponding Form Slot becomes empty.

If that family’s Secondary Form remains unused, the Composite can replace the lost Primary.

---

## Recompose Form ◆

**CONCENTRATE**

**Requirements:** one Form Slot is empty and that family’s unused Secondary Form remains available.

The Composite rips that Secondary relic from the chamber and incorporates it.

The Secondary attaches at:

> **45 HP**

The Form Slot becomes active again.

Once both Forms belonging to a family are Dormant:

> **that Form family is permanently unavailable for the remainder of Phase II.**

The normal lifecycle of one family is therefore:

> **Primary active → removed → slot empty → Recompose → Secondary active → removed → family gone**

This gives Darkwing a finite Form supply to dismantle.

---

## Main-body damage strips Form too

Darkwing is never required to target individual Forms.

The Composite’s body HP also forces false Form away.

At:

- **330 HP**
- **220 HP**
- **110 HP**

the Composite crosses a threshold.

Whenever a threshold is crossed:

> **Darkwing chooses one currently attached Form.**

That Form is immediately torn away and becomes Dormant.

If one Strike or effect crosses multiple thresholds, resolve every crossed threshold.

If the removed Form was the Primary and its Secondary remains unused:

> the Composite may still spend **Recompose Form** later.

This is the Phase II expression of the finale rule:

> **Martials are literally beating lies off the boss.**

At 0 HP:

> **all remaining Forms immediately become Dormant.**

---

## Alternate early phase completion

If Darkwing permanently exhausts **all available Form families** before reducing the Composite to 0 HP:

> **the Composite collapses immediately.**

It has no remaining supplied Form with which to maintain a coherent person.

This is a valid alternate victory path.

A Party may therefore:

- simply burn down the Composite;
- surgically remove dangerous Forms;
- fully exhaust the Form inventory;
- or use a hybrid strategy.

All are correct.

**Material:** Stone / Carved Masonry

> **DC 36 Reflex**

## At the Table

**Objective:** follow the Echo backward through remembered crises to the original riverbank choice. **Board:** one open Memory page at a time; page locks, chronological order, rescue targets and Echo damage thresholds. **Faction state:** Dawn’s people and EGRESS’s Frames can assist or interfere. **Finish:** Memory and Choice become coherent at the origin.

Use the **contribution table in :ref[The Burden]{#encounter.the-burden}** to select full, damaged or absent capability before applying a faction’s phase procedure. The phase’s full-strength descriptions do not override a Damaged front.

## Identity
**Memory + Choice**

## Faction sockets
- **EGRESS Institute → Memory**
- **Dawn Assembly → Choice**

## Encounter Parameters

- **Incursion:** Creature 15 / Party Level +3
- **Source:** same Level 15, Class V Book of Names
- **Primary target:** one fleeing human-scale Echo carrying the closed Book
- **Echo HP:** 360
- **Three full combat Memories**
- **Page Locks:** 270 / 180 / 90 HP
- **At 90 HP:** damage stops mattering and the Memory Cascade begins
- **Core problem:** pursue and damage the Echo while surviving historical catastrophe and deciding how many actions to spend saving people caught in it

The central rule is:

> **Damage turns pages.**

## Interphase Pause — The Echo Wants to Be Followed

When the Composite collapses:

- the enormous false body falls apart;
- one human-scale black silhouette remains;
- the Book is in its hands.

The Book is still **closed**.

The Echo looks toward the reopened Dawn tunnel.

Then it runs into the tunnel.

It does not open the Book yet.

The ruined Ritual Chamber becomes quiet.

There is no Phase III timer forcing immediate pursuit.

Darkwing may take ordinary exploration-mode recovery:

- Treat Wounds;
- Refocus;
- repairs;
- consumables;
- condition recovery;
- equipment preparation;
- discussion.

The Colony remains under Totality, but the Echo itself is not pressuring them to move immediately.

Narratively, the intent should be readable:

> **It wants Darkwing to follow.**

---

## The Far End Cannot Be Used as an Ambush Route

Darkwing may know where the Dawn tunnel exits on Crescent Island.

They may attempt to:

- leave the Ritual complex by another route;
- fly or teleport outside;
- race to Crescent Island;
- wait at the far tunnel mouth;
- cut the Echo off.

That does not work.

## The Crescent-Island Mouth Is Wound-Sealed

From outside, the Crescent Island end of the Dawn tunnel is blocked by an active mnemonic Wound.

It is not:

- a door;
- masonry;
- a wall with HP;
- a barrier that can be Dispelled through ordinary damage.

It is the boundary of the active reconstruction.

From the outside it is:

> **impenetrable**

Darkwing cannot:

- damage through it;
- excavate around it;
- teleport through it;
- force entry.

The Wound opens from the inside only when the Memory progression reaches its origin.

Thus there is no valid ambush route at the far end.

Eventually, the only meaningful route is:

> **follow the Echo into the tunnel.**

## Dawn and EGRESS Arrive Before Entry

Dawn and EGRESS do **not** need to have been present during Phase II.

During Darkwing's recovery pause:

> **the relevant faction participants descend into the ruined Ritual Chamber**

according to current faction state.

This beat should make their upcoming behavior obvious before anyone crosses the threshold.

Possible states:

- assisting;
- interfering;
- absent.

Then Darkwing chooses when to enter.

## Phase III Trigger — The Book Opens Only After Darkwing Follows

The first Darkwing PC crosses the tunnel threshold.

Ahead, the Echo stops.

It turns around.

For the first time:

> **it opens the Book.**

The first crowded memorial page becomes visible.

Reality tears sideways.

The Memory projection expands across the entire Recognition circuit.

Immediately:

> **all Darkwing PCs are pulled into the same projected experience**

even if someone remained in the Ritual Chamber.

No save.

The Party has not physically time-traveled.

Their bodies are moving through the tunnel.

Their experienced:

- terrain;
- distance;
- battlefield;
- sky;
- historical surroundings

are now being supplied by the Book.

Relevant Dawn and EGRESS participants are incorporated according to faction state.

## Dawn Interaction

## Assist — Hold the Route

Dawn does not enter the Memory.

They take position at the tunnel entrance in the Ritual Chamber.

Their promise is:

> **“Go. We hold this end.”**

No additional outside faction complication follows Darkwing into Phase III.

## Interfere — Follow Them In

Three Dawn operatives insist on accompanying Darkwing.

One of them is always:

> **Julian Arrett**

Of course Julian believes he is personally capable of preventing Darkwing from making the wrong decision.

If the story has already produced recognizable Dawn loyalists, use them for the other two.

Otherwise they are simply:

> **two nameless loyalists to Julian Arrett**

No additional backstory is required before the encounter.

Their reasoning may be sincere:

> **They will not leave this woman's fate entirely in Darkwing's hands.**

The Book immediately incorporates **all three** into every primary Memory as historical victims.

No matter how many times Darkwing rescues them:

> **the next page recasts all three into a new disaster.**

This is both mechanically costly and darkly comic.

They wanted to guarantee agency.

The Memory keeps making them hostages.

## Dream Pace

The chase uses memory / dream logic rather than ordinary physical distance.

Within the projected Memory:

> **no creature can exploit extreme movement to trivially collapse the chase.**

A creature with any Speed above 30 feet, or an effect that would voluntarily move or teleport it more than 30 feet with one action, may attempt:

> **Fortitude DC 33**

as a free action at the start of its turn.

### Critical Success
For that turn:

- use normal Speeds;
- voluntary teleportation and movement can exceed 30 feet normally.

### Any Other Result
For that turn:

- each Speed is treated as a maximum of 30 feet;
- no single voluntary movement or teleportation action can move the creature more than 30 feet through the Memory.

This never reduces a Speed already below 30 feet.

It is not difficult terrain.

It is the remembered world refusing to let distance behave normally.

A speedster can occasionally break the dream logic.

They cannot simply solve the entire phase by being faster than history.

## Run the Page ◆◆

The Echo Strides up to twice.

It:

- ignores difficult terrain created by the current Memory;
- can move through other creatures' spaces as difficult terrain;
- cannot end in another creature's space;
- triggers reactions normally.

If:

- grabbed;
- restrained;
- immobilized;

it must resolve those conditions normally.

Darkwing can:

- Grapple;
- Trip;
- Slow;
- body-block;
- use reactions;
- use battlefield control;
- close to melee when the chase allows it.

Melee combat is therefore possible.

It simply requires actually winning distance inside the Memory.

A Page Turn resets the pursuit geometry.

---

## The Scrolling Battlefield

The Memories have no meaningful physical boundary.

Darkwing can run through:

- an apparently endless Chelish-controlled Colony;
- an invasion extending beyond the map;
- a flood with no visible edge.

Represent only the current visible section.

Use approximately:

> **60 feet long × 40 feet wide**

## Scroll the Memory

When the pursuit approaches the front edge:

1. shift all participants backward the same distance;
2. preserve relative distances;
3. remove terrain left behind;
4. place more current-Memory terrain ahead.

No one encountered a wall.

The Memory simply continued.

---

## Page Locks

The three primary historical Memories must all occur.

The Echo has Page Locks at:

- **270 HP**
- **180 HP**
- **90 HP**

The Echo cannot be reduced below the current Page Lock by one effect.

Excess damage from that effect is lost.

After the triggering action:

> **Turn the Page.**

High DPR advances the phase rapidly.

It cannot delete an entire historical Memory.

---

## Turn the Page ◇

**Trigger:** the Echo reaches the current Page Lock.

1. End the current Memory scene.
2. Remove its terrain and historical projections.
3. End grabs / restraints between the Echo and other creatures as the reconstruction changes.
4. Preserve Darkwing HP and ordinary conditions unless they depended entirely on vanished scenery.
5. Recast **all three Dawn operatives** into the next Memory if Dawn interfered.
6. Place the Echo roughly **20–30 feet ahead of the lead PC**.
7. Turn the physical Book page.
8. Replace the battlefield with the next Memory.

EGRESS Frames remain through all three combat Memories.

## The Book Chronology During Phase III

Every page shown during Phase III belongs to the **early memorial portion** of the Book.

The pages are crowded.

They contain:

- many names;
- margin entries;
- names squeezed between lines;
- crossed-out names that remain legible.

The three major Memories and the later rapid cascade all come from these early crowded memorial pages.

Darkwing does **not** reach the one-name-per-page Keeper succession during Phase III.

That transition happens only after Aletheia writes her own Name.

## Dawn Victims — Duskwood Memory

All three Dawn operatives are recast as early defenders trapped inside a collapsing defensive position.

Each begins:

- prone;
- immobilized beneath rubble or fortification debris.

A suitable rescue can use:

> **Athletics DC 32**

or:

- destroy the obstruction;
- move it;
- teleport them;
- use appropriate magic.

When freed, they can move normally for the remainder of the Memory.

At the next Page Turn:

> **all three are recast again.**

## Dawn Victims — Flood Memory

All three Dawn operatives are recast into separate flood emergencies.

Examples:

- one clings to floating debris;
- one is trapped on a collapsing roof;
- one is being swept sideways toward wreckage.

Each rescue is straightforward.

Possible solutions:

- reach and Grapple;
- Athletics DC 32;
- fly;
- teleport;
- throw a rope;
- use appropriate magic.

If ignored:

> the flood continues hurting them while Darkwing chases the Echo.

## The Fourth Threshold — Memory Cascade

At:

> **90 HP**

the chase stops being a damage encounter.

After the triggering action resolves:

> **stop tracking Echo HP**

Damage no longer matters.

Initiative ends.

The Book begins turning its crowded memorial pages by itself.

Faster.

Then faster.

Then faster still.

---

## Faction Participants Leave the Memory

At the beginning of the Cascade:

> **all non-Darkwing faction participants are expelled from the projection**

This includes:

- all three Dawn operatives;
- both EGRESS Frames.

They appear at the **Ritual Chamber-side entrance to the tunnel** in whatever condition the Party last left them.

They retain:

- HP;
- dying state;
- persistent damage;
- fatigue;
- sickened;
- other ordinary conditions that still make sense.

Scene-only historical restraints vanish.

Thus if Darkwing ignored Dawn and simply poured damage into the Echo:

> **all three operatives are waiting back at the tunnel entrance in whatever wrecked condition the last Memory produced.**

If hostile EGRESS Frames were protecting the Echo:

> their objective ends immediately.

There are no longer stable combat Memories to record.

They do not continue into Phase IV.

---

## The Memory Cascade

The GM turns perhaps ten or more **crowded memorial pages**.

Each gets one fragment.

Not an encounter.

Not exposition.

Examples:

- a hand disappearing beneath floodwater;
- a prison door slamming;
- someone digging a grave;
- a child hiding while a building burns;
- a body wrapped for burial;
- someone running through rain;
- a soldier looking back once;
- someone screaming a name;
- hands carrying an injured stranger;
- a condemned person seeing sunrise;
- a family being separated;
- someone dying alone.

The images arrive faster than Darkwing can process them as stories.

The point is:

> **understand how much one crowded memorial register was actually carrying.**

---

## The Blank Page

The rapid page-turning stops.

For the first time in Phase III:

> **Darkwing sees a page with no Name on it.**

The crowded memorial pages have reached their end.

The Memory becomes quiet.

Riverbank.

Crescent Island.

The same physical place the tunnel is leading toward.

There is:

- no Order;
- no Monument;
- no Ritual Chamber;
- no formal Reading;
- no Keeper office;
- no Founder’s Way;
- no ceremonial procession.

Only:

- the memorial register;
- the river;
- one black nameless woman;
- one blank page.

## The Origin Memory

Aletheia remains:

> **a completely black, nameless Veiled silhouette**

Darkwing has recovered Memory.

Darkwing has not recovered Name.

They see behavior belonging to **her** rather than to the office created later:

- exhaustion;
- fear;
- empathy;
- hesitation;
- responsibility.

She reaches the blank page.

Then:

> **she writes her own Name.**

Darkwing witnesses the action.

The Name itself remains unreadable.

Possible presentation:

- her hand obscures it;
- the ink will not remain in conscious perception;
- the letters refuse to stabilize.

Darkwing experiences:

> **the act of naming herself**

without yet learning:

> **Aletheia**

---

## Memory Restoration

The chase has demonstrated:

> **These are the lives she experienced.**

The crowded memorial was not abstraction to her.

Those names became human lives.

That is:

> **Memory**

---

## Choice Restoration

The origin scene also establishes:

- nobody ordered her;
- no institution instructed her;
- no ritual existed;
- there was no Keeper office;
- no doctrine promised an outcome.

She encountered the inheritance.

Then she chose.

The false inherited imperative:

> **Carry them forever**

collapses into:

> **One woman chose to carry them then.**

That is:

> **Choice**

No final check.

Once the truth is unavoidable:

> **Memory + Choice are Restored.**

---

## Phase III Transition

The origin Memory fades.

But the river does not.

The grass does not.

Darkwing realizes:

> **they have exited the tunnel.**

They are standing on present-day Crescent Island in the exact location where the remembered event occurred.

The nameless black woman stands where she stood in the Memory.

She holds the real Book of Names.

Her own page now contains the unreadable Name she just wrote.

Then:

> **she turns to the next page.**

That page contains a **single Name**.

A black Keeper appears.

She turns another.

Another single Name.

Another Keeper.

This is the first time Darkwing sees the Book's later one-Keeper-per-page structure.

Phase IV begins.

## 8. Crescent Island as the Origin

Current working canon to lock:

> **The convergence point on Crescent Island is the place where Aletheia’s act transformed the ordinary memorial register into the first true mnemonic anchor of the later Book of Names.**

This was not previously locked in the source documents, but the final encounter now strongly benefits from making it true.

The Dawn tunnel therefore becomes an accidental historical arrow pointing backward toward the origin.

## State Handed On

Memory and Choice join the already Restored Identity. Preserve which Dawn participants survived and what they learned, surviving Frames, current resources, and the Book’s unchanged Source identity. The Party reaches present-day Crescent Island at the historical riverbank; the remaining problem is Name.

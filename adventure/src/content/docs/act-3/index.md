## The Act in Brief

The eclipse becomes Totality. Five simultaneous fronts force the Colony’s prepared people and institutions to act without Darkwing everywhere at once. Surviving capabilities and relationships enter The Burden; none reset between the emergency and the finale.

## The Act Question

Who will carry what the Colony can no longer make one forgotten person bear? Survival and restored Identity make that question answerable; they do not dictate the answer.

## Routes Through the Act

Eclipse leads to Totality. Sunny, The Breach, Milltown, The Tangled Web and The Open Door are concurrent responses with different procedures and consequences. Use :ref[Act III Front Outcomes]{#rule.act-three-fronts}. Perrik and Kelda remain independent personal contributions, not additional fronts.

## The Finale

:ref[The Burden]{#encounter.the-burden} has four phases: the Monument, Borrowed First Keeper, Echo through the Memories, and Keeper succession. It restores Context/Pattern, Form/Relationship, Memory/Choice, then Name. Local evidence prevents skipped routes from creating a softlock.

:ref[Aletheia]{#event.aletheia} asks the living to choose viable destinations and responsibilities. **The Final Reading**, the opening section of :ref[The Light Remains]{#event.the-light-remains}, enacts that answer without a new test.

## Promises and Consequences

Pay off Vara and Marek, Evin and Gressa, and Tom and Sundara without treating Sundara as a Keeper. Preserve faction losses, free departures, refused gifts and the Spider’s genuinely new object. No ending automatically repairs every Asset or harmonizes every institution.

## Suggested Pacing

The source’s final four sessions are a planning shape, not a turn limit. The city scenes and four-phase finale may each need substantial table time. Prepare boards and cards before entering a phase; pause at a clear boundary when necessary, preserving state.

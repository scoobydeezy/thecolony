## Definition

**Source Transfer** is moving mnemonic Weight from its current Vessel into another destination. The destination becomes the new Source Vessel. Transfer is **not** Resolution: moving unresolved Weight only changes the topology through which reality can reconstruct it.

Transfer happens in two ways: **deliberately**, by the party or an institution (below), or **uncontrolled**, as the Spill after a :ref[Rupture]{#rule.source-destruction}.

## Procedure

**Deliberate Transfer.**

1. **Opening.** The Source must be safely exposed: during a :ref[Stabilization Window]{#rule.stabilization-window} or another period when the Source is exposed, or by a forced Transfer while the Source is Breached (see :ref[Active Sources]{#rule.active-sources}).
2. **Destination.** Name the receiving destination: a prepared containment vessel, another resonant object, a related place, a compatible engram, a person, or another Source able to accept the load.
3. **Check, only if needed.** If compatibility and Capacity are already established, the Transfer should usually work; do not hide basic physics behind an unnecessary check. Call for a check against the Source DC, with a skill that fits the method, only when the party is:
   - uncertain whether the destination is compatible;
   - forcing an unnatural transfer;
   - exceeding safe Capacity;
   - using damaged equipment;
   - transferring while the Source is only briefly Breached;
   - performing a technically complex routing operation.
4. **Pressure.** In a Window, each action spent on the Transfer adds Reconstruction Pressure normally.

**After any Transfer** (deliberate or uncontrolled):

5. Unless Weight is deliberately split or transformed, the new Source keeps the Weight's **Source Level, Class, current Reconstruction Pressure, Restored Identity**, and stabilized or unstabilized state.
6. An active Incursion stays manifested; changing Vessel never Disperses it.
7. The Ward is not refreshed. A collapsed Ward stays collapsed; a Source transferred during a Window stays without a Ward. A fresh Ward forms only if the Incursion reconstructs.
8. **Re-evaluate Resonance immediately.** The new destination may break the existing Resonance, create a different Reconstruction Condition, change the Incursion's target priorities or Form, split one circuit into several, or combine separate fragments.

## Examples

In :ref[Bad Maps]{#encounter.bad-maps}, if the cloth toy is destroyed while Gressa is inside the Rupture area, the Weight transfers uncontrolled into Gressa herself. She becomes the new Class II Source. Because the Weight and its strongest Recognition now share one living Vessel, separating her from the broken toy can no longer break the circuit.

## Interactions

- Changing the Source can change what reality thinks it is reconstructing; a Transfer is not like moving a phylactery.
- Transfer into a person is physically possible without Consent; that does not make it legitimate. A living Source keeps their agency.
- Capacity is rated against Class (see :ref[Containment]{#rule.containment}).

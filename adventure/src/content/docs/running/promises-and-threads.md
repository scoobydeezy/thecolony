A promise is something play has made meaningful enough that the campaign owes it an answer, a consequence, or a deliberate reason to remain open. The thread entries connect scenes; this page helps the GM preserve the people and questions those connections carry.

## People the Campaign Must Remember

| Promise | Establish early | Payoff and fallback |
|---|---|---|
| :ref[Vara and the Keeper]{#thread.the-keeper} | Give Vara interests, intentions, and relationships beyond being Called. | If alive, preserve her agency after rescue. If Forgotten, reconstruct her through surviving relationships and confront what reopening her sacrifice means. |
| :ref[Marek, the missing father]{#thread.the-missing-father} | The compass, missing household adult, mapmaking habits, and unfamiliar hand on the Manor map. | EGRESS can reveal Marek without requiring him to be the Vault's hidden subject. | :ref[The cloth toy]{#thread.the-cloth-toy} | A mother's bond produces an infant-shaped false reconstruction. | A Life can reveal Evin's adult life. If missed, the culminating Keeper material must still make clear that he grew up and later became a Keeper. |
| :ref[Old Town and Sunny]{#thread.old-town} | Tom's nickname and the damaged history of Old Town. | Old Town teaches six other lives. Sunny later reveals Sundara's grave and whistle. If skipped, provide an earned relationship-based payoff elsewhere; Sundara was not a Keeper. |
| Uldren and Celia | Care for the living Keeper and preservation of the belongings that remain. | Once Identity becomes coherent, let them acknowledge the person their separate institutions preserved in pieces. |

The later scene must actually supply the payoff; a note that it could do so is not enough. Track which promises play has already paid and which it still owes.

## Institutions That Preserve an Incomplete Answer

The Monument honors Aletheia's act while losing the woman. The Order's name preserves Theia within Aletheia. The Book's crowded memorial pages precede the single-name Keeper succession. Bring these concrete details back when their meaning becomes clear.

The :ref[official Duskwood story]{#thread.the-official-duskwood-story} is another incomplete representation. The forest is dangerous, but it is not the Veiled's origin. Act I challenges the external-threat account; Old Town undermines it through evidence of the Colony's own wounds.

:ref[Bad maps]{#thread.bad-maps} and :ref[the Manor]{#thread.the-manor} join the same idea: an incomplete account can contain truth and still lead someone badly astray. The Observer studies that problem; he did not create the Colony's crisis to illustrate it.

## Promises That Stay Complicated

The Cult's two lessons concern receiving others without claiming authority to define or keep them. The Shape of Someone challenges supplied Form; The Open Door tests hospitality that decides a guest's desires in advance. Maelyn's release of what she treasures is an intended possibility, not a protected script.

The Counterfeit Weaver's fourth object can carry genuinely new remembered experience without already proving independent Anchoring. Its future remains an open question, separate from whether the Ledger survives.

These threads can stay unresolved because their consequences continue. Do not mistake an open future for an accidentally abandoned clue.

## Ordinary Details at the End

Revisit the Open Place, Second Portion, circulating possessions, unfinished portraits, Spare Hand, and Resting Stroke. Their payoff is the Party recognizing how the Colony made absence livable. They need no single secret origin.

“The light remains” acquires meaning through what the living Colony chooses. Do not decode it into an authoritative slogan at the conclusion.

## Keep a Small Promise Record

After a session, note what the players noticed, whom it matters to, what evidence is established, and where it can next become relevant. Distinguish an unresolved person from a missing mechanic: local evidence must make the finale playable even if a personal thread remains unpaid.

The Colony's former name, founding purpose, the Calling's selection mechanism, and the long-term wisdom of the final institution can remain unanswered. Do not add mandatory scenes merely to close every question.

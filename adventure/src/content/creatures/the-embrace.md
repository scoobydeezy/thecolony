### Gather Them In ◆◆
**ATTACK** — The Embrace Strides up to half its Speed, then attempts **up to two Grapples against different targets** in reach, each Athletics +26 versus Fortitude DC. These are ordinary Grapples, with MAP applied normally; each successful hold occupies one branch of its arms. Maximum four held creatures, subject to reach.

### Never Let Go ◆
**Requirements:** One target is grabbed by the Embrace. The target attempts **DC 32 basic Fortitude** against **3d8 bludgeoning**. On a failure, it also becomes **clumsy 1** until the end of its next turn. This does not automatically extend the Grapple.

### Closer ↻
**Trigger:** A held target successfully Escapes or another creature Strides away from within the Embrace's reach; **Frequency:** once per round.  
The Embrace attempts **Reposition** against the triggering creature if it remains in reach, or Strides 10 feet toward the nearest target if not. A successful Reposition moves the target 5 feet toward the Embrace. Use the normal MAP-independent reaction timing for the check.

**Tactics:** Grab, gather, and prevent separation, rather than selecting victims by malice. Its held creatures are still part of the ordinary encounter and can Escape normally. Its Source remains in Cell 05; Dispersal away from home follows Cell-Bound Reconstruction.

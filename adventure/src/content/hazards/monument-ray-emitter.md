There are eight identical emitters around the Monument base.

**AC** 33  
**HP** 30  
**Hardness** —  
**Broken Threshold** —

A ray emitter is a Small fixed object occupying part of the pedestal edge.

At 0 HP:

- the emitter is destroyed;
- the corresponding sector can no longer discharge either Ground or Air energy;
- the Monument's Ray-Fed Resistance decreases by 2.

A destroyed ray never returns during Phase I.

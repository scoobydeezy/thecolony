**Fragment attack modifier:** +22 unless a Fragment specifies otherwise.

### Corpus Shell
Each Corpus has one fixed Shell numbered **1–6**. The Shell never migrates and remains the same through Dispersal and reconstruction.

Each Shell carries **one fixed pre-action behavioral tell**. Before taking its three actions on every turn while manifested, describe its same physical motion; this is **not an action or reaction** and never changes when Fragments migrate. Borrowed pieces can make a practiced motion look like something entirely different. Play the motion, not an explicit occupational explanation. The six tell scripts live in the life entries below and the Recognition rule immediately following. A player can recall a tell already observed when attempting Recognition; new fragments provide new context without forcing another wait for the Shell's turn.

### Migratory Fragments
Each unnamed Corpus carries exactly one Head, one Arms set, and one Back Fragment.

A Fragment's mechanical ability belongs to the Fragment, not the Shell wearing it. If the Fragment moves, its entire rules package moves with it.

This is intentionally capable of producing highly uneven combinations. Some assemblies will be unusually effective; others will be tactically awkward or absurd. Do **not** rebalance a combination after migration. The instability is part of the Chorus.

### Refrain
An unnamed Dispersed Corpus reconstructs at full HP at the **start of its second turn after Dispersal**, emerging from the shadow of an unoccupied grave marker or another legal space within the active cemetery field.

A Dispersed Corpus reconstructs wearing whatever Head, Arms, and Back Fragments currently occupy its numbered slot.

**Refrain replaces the normal individual Class-based Stabilization Window for the Corpora themselves.** The Old Town cemetery remains the distributed Source supporting the Chorus. Naming, not repeated damage, permanently removes a Corpus from this encounter.

## Fragment Packages

These are fixed historical life kernels. Their personal names are GM working labels, replaced by the Names authored in play. :ref[Old Town]{#encounter.old-town} owns Recognition, swaps, and permanent Named Boundaries.

## **1. Mara Venn — Catfolk Rescuer**

**Shell:** Medium catfolk woman  
**Fixed Tell (before three actions):** Makes two tight wrist-circles, reaches sharply outward, then rocks backward as though taking a sudden load.  
**GM interpretation (not narrated automatically):** With the wrong equipment, it could be a hammer windup, an errant spell, or a panicked grab. With Rescue Hook and Lifeline Coil restored, it is unmistakably winding, casting, and bracing a rescue rope. The persistent movement is the same regardless of the worn arms.  
**Grave Motif:** Waves and a coiled rope.  
**Epitaph:** *“She brought them home.”*

### True Head — Mara's Head
A feline head with prominent ears, short muzzle, tied-back mane or braids, and one visibly torn ear.

No combat ability. The exaggerated feline silhouette is an identity cue.

### True Arms — Rescue Hook
One hand carries a long boat hook; the other controls a knotted rescue line.

**Rescue Hook** ◆  
**Melee** +22, reach 10 feet  
**Damage** 2d8+10 bludgeoning

On a hit, the wearer can pull the target **5 feet toward itself** if the target is no more than one size larger than the wearer.

### Cast the Line ◆◆
The wearer lashes the rescue line at one creature within 30 feet. Make a Fragment attack against the target's AC.

* **Critical Success:** 2d8+10 bludgeoning damage and pull the target 20 feet toward the wearer.
* **Success:** 2d8+10 bludgeoning damage and pull the target 10 feet toward the wearer.
* **Failure:** No effect.

Forced movement must end in legal terrain.

### True Back — Lifeline Coil
A huge coil of rope and remembered flotation gear dominates the wearer's back silhouette.

### Lifeline ↻
**Trigger:** A creature within 20 feet is forcibly moved, knocked prone, or would fall.  
**Frequency:** once per round.

The wearer throws a line and pulls the triggering creature up to **10 feet toward itself**. An unwilling creature can attempt a **DC 29 Reflex save** to negate the pull.

If this movement would prevent a fall, it does.

## **2. Dorrik Vane — Dwarf Mason**

**Shell:** Medium dwarf man; broad and low-set  
**Fixed Tell (before three actions):** Plants one palm squarely against an invisible vertical plane; the other hand gives two short, precisely spaced taps, then both hands hold still.  
**GM interpretation (not narrated automatically):** With an apothecary kit, it resembles examining a patient; with a shield, checking a defense. With Mason's Maul and Frame, it becomes the practiced check-and-set motion of aligning a stone before striking.  
**Grave Motif:** Hammer beneath a stone arch.  
**Epitaph:** *“What he set stood.”*

### True Head — Dorrik's Head
A square dwarven head with an enormous braided beard and flattened workman's nose.

No combat ability. The beard should remain one of the encounter's most immediately recognizable silhouettes when attached to the wrong Shell.

### True Arms — Mason's Maul
An oversized stoneworking hammer and heavy chisel.

**Mason's Maul** ◆  
**Melee** +23  
**Damage** 2d12+10 bludgeoning

### Set the Stone ◆◆
Make a Mason's Maul Strike.

If the Strike succeeds, the target attempts a **DC 29 Fortitude save**.

* **Critical Success:** No additional effect.
* **Success:** Pushed 5 feet.
* **Failure:** Knocked prone.
* **Critical Failure:** Knocked prone and takes an additional 2d8 bludgeoning damage.

Whether or not the Strike hits, one 5-foot square adjacent to the target or wearer becomes difficult terrain until the start of the wearer's next turn as remembered masonry briefly pushes through the ground.

### True Back — Mason's Frame
A heavy timber-and-stone carrying rig loaded with blocks, braces, pegs, and tools.

### Load-Bearing
The wearer:

* gains **resistance 5 to physical damage**;
* gains a **+2 circumstance bonus to checks and DCs against forced movement**;
* takes a **–5-foot status penalty to Speed**.

## **3. Seraith Vennel — Elf Apothecary**

**Shell:** Medium elf woman; extremely tall and slender  
**Fixed Tell (before three actions):** Makes three small circles with one hand, pauses, then raises its other hand to eye level and tilts its head to inspect it.  
**GM interpretation (not narrated automatically):** With a weapon it looks like blade maintenance or a spell preparation; with Apothecary Hands and Rack it becomes stirring a dose and checking its concentration.  
**Grave Motif:** Mortar and leaf.  
**Epitaph:** *“None went unattended.”*

### True Head — Seraith's Head
A narrow elven face with dramatically elongated ears and hair pulled high or backward so the ear silhouette remains unmistakable.

No combat ability.

### True Arms — Apothecary Hands
One arm carries clustered vials and stoppered bottles; the other wields a long pestle, spoon, or dosing instrument.

### Caustic Flask ◆
**Ranged** +22, range increment 30 feet  
**Damage** 2d6+8 acid plus 2 acid splash damage

### Wrong Dose ◆◆
One creature within 20 feet attempts a **DC 29 Fortitude save** against a remembered treatment applied without sufficient Context.

* **Critical Success:** Unaffected.
* **Success:** Sickened 1.
* **Failure:** Sickened 2.
* **Critical Failure:** Sickened 2 and slowed 1 until the end of its next turn.

A creature currently sickened by Wrong Dose is temporarily immune to Wrong Dose until the end of the wearer's next turn.

### True Back — Apothecary Rack
A tall scaffold of bottles, herb bundles, tubing, pouches, and dangling tools.

### Automatic Triage ↻
**Trigger:** The wearer or another Corpus within 10 feet takes damage.  
**Frequency:** once per round.

If the triggering creature is Veiled, it regains **12 HP**.

If the triggering creature is not Veiled, the rack instead attempts to administer an inappropriate remembered treatment. The target attempts a **DC 29 Fortitude save**. On a failure, it becomes **sickened 1**; on a success, it is unaffected.

## **4. Koru Blackfeather — Tengu Warning Courier**

**Shell:** Medium tengu man  
**Fixed Tell (before three actions):** Lifts one arm high, covers and uncovers it twice with the other, then sharply points into the distance.  
**GM interpretation (not narrated automatically):** With the wrong Arms it reads as signaling an attack or shading the eyes. With the Signal Lantern and Courier Rig it is two precisely shuttered warning flashes and a route indication.  
**Grave Motif:** A road beneath a rising star.  
**Epitaph:** *“Ahead of the fear.”*

### True Head — Koru's Head
An unmistakable tengu beak with a swept-back feather crest.

No combat ability.

### True Arms — Signal Lantern
One hand carries a large hooded signal lantern on a long handle. The other manipulates remembered shutters or signaling panes.

**Sweep the Road** ◆  
**Melee** +22  
**Damage** 2d8+10 bludgeoning

### Beacon Sweep ◆◆
The wearer throws open the remembered signal shutters. Choose a **30-foot cone**. Impossible routes, warning marks, fleeing silhouettes, and evacuation paths momentarily overwrite the battlefield.

Creatures in the area attempt a **DC 29 Reflex save** against **5d6 mental damage**.

* **Critical Success:** Unaffected.
* **Success:** Half damage.
* **Failure:** Full damage and **off-guard until the start of the wearer's next turn**.
* **Critical Failure:** Double damage, off-guard until the start of the wearer's next turn, and the creature must use its reaction, if available, to **Stride up to half its Speed away from the wearer** by the safest available route.

This movement has the mental trait and does not cause the creature to enter obviously lethal terrain.

### True Back — Courier Rig
A tall message frame, satchel, route markers, and strips of cloth form a conspicuous back silhouette.

### Never Stop
The wearer has **Speed 35 feet** instead of its normal Speed.

After completing a **two-action Arms ability**, the wearer can **Step** as a free action.

## **5. Ketha Vorr — Orc Defender**

**Shell:** Medium orc woman; tall and heavily built  
**Fixed Tell (before three actions):** Sidesteps half a pace, turns one shoulder outward, plants both feet, and sweeps one arm across a space beside it before lowering its center of gravity.  
**GM interpretation (not narrated automatically):** With a bow or vial it resembles a threatening draw or flourish; with Spear and Broad Shield it resolves as a practiced gap-covering defensive formation leaving safe passage behind.  
**Grave Motif:** Spear crossing a broken chain.  
**Epitaph:** *“She stood where others could pass.”*

### True Head — Ketha's Head
A broad orc head with prominent tusks and a tall braided crest.

No combat ability.

### True Arms — Defender's Spear
A long spear or polearm with an exaggerated silhouette.

**Long Spear** ◆  
**Melee** +23, reach 10 feet  
**Damage** 2d10+10 piercing

### Remembered Guard ↻
**Trigger:** A creature within the wearer's reach uses a move action, makes a ranged attack, or uses a manipulate action.  

The wearer makes a **Long Spear** Strike against the triggering creature.

On a critical hit against a creature using a move action, the triggering movement is disrupted if that action normally permits disruption.

### True Back — Broad Shield
A huge oval shield is strapped across the wearer's back and shoulders.

### Sheltering Bulk
The wearer gains a **+1 circumstance bonus to AC**.

### Interpose ↻
**Trigger:** Another Corpus within 10 feet would take damage.  
**Frequency:** once per round.

The wearer can Step up to 5 feet toward the triggering Corpus if needed to bring it within 10 feet. Reduce the triggering damage by **12**.

The wearer then takes **6 damage**. This damage cannot be reduced or redirected.

## **6. Szik Ember-Eye — Kobold Occult Cartographer**

**Shell:** Small kobold man  
**Fixed Tell (before three actions):** Touches three separate points in the air, joins two with a straight sweep, pauses at the third, and sharply turns its head as though checking a bearing.  
**GM interpretation (not narrated automatically):** With borrowed implements it might be a ritual, a three-point stabbing pattern, or abstract counting. With Star-Staff and Mnemonic Lattice it is plotting a measured alignment and checking the unseen bearing.  
**Grave Motif:** A star inside a flame.  
**Epitaph:** *“He mapped what others could not see.”*

### True Head — Szik's Head
A sharply draconic kobold head with oversized swept horns.

No combat ability. The head is deliberately exaggerated so that a "dragon head" attached to another ancestry reads instantly in silhouette.

### True Arms — Star-Staff
One hand holds a crooked staff while the other remains frozen in an exaggerated casting gesture.

### Gravefire Bolt ◆
**Ranged spell attack** +22, range 60 feet  
**Damage** 3d8+8 force

### False Constellation ◆◆
Choose a point within 60 feet. A remembered constellation detonates in a **10-foot burst**.

Creatures in the area attempt a **DC 29 Reflex save** against **6d6 force damage**.

On a failure, the creature is also **dazzled until the end of its next turn**.

### True Back — Mnemonic Lattice
A fan of geometric rods, crystalline vanes, scroll cases, and ring-like instruments rises behind the wearer.

### Overchannel
The first time each round the wearer uses an **Arms ability that deals damage**:

* add **one additional weapon or damage die** of the same type to that ability's damage;
* if the ability requires a saving throw, increase that ability's DC by **1**.

If the ability damages multiple creatures, only one shared damage roll gains the additional die.

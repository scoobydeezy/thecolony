The Weaver is not a deity, avatar or divine herald. Its sense detects recognized Weight and compatible activity, not infallible historical truth. Its 20-foot space and reaching legs are real; true Coherence overrides the false divine Form as described in the encounter.

**UNIQUE, VEILED, COMPOSITE, OCCULT** (encounter traits; **not** an actual deity, avatar, or divine herald)
**Str** +8, **Dex** +9, **Con** +6, **Int** +10, **Wis** +8, **Cha** +9
**Melee** ◆ *Artisan's Leg* +38 (magical, reach 20 ft), **Damage** 4d10+16 bludgeoning (average 38); on a critical hit, the Spider may Reposition the target 10 feet into a space it can reach, following the normal forced movement restrictions.
**Ranged** ◆ *Looming Strand* +38 (magical, range increment 100 ft), **Damage** 4d8+14 bludgeoning (average 32) and a tethered target on a hit, as below.
**Occult ability DC** 42 (high level-20 DC). The Spider's field-note clues are **site-evidence**, not checks against this number: known inconsistencies such as persistent web and controlled timing can always be observed.

### Web-Cast ◆◆ — Occult, Conjuration, Incursion

The Spider fires a fully corporeal veil of microscopic strands at one creature within 100 feet that it can perceive. **Fortitude DC 42**.

- **Critical success:** Target avoids the web.
- **Success:** Restrained only until the end of the target's next turn by partial entanglement (**immobilized** instead of fully restrained); the Spider may release it earlier.
- **Failure:** **Restrained** and **unconscious** while attached to an anchored wall or ceiling. The web causes no automatic HP loss; the Spider is choosing nonlethal incapacitation.
- **Critical failure:** As failure, and the target takes 6d10 mental damage from forced mnemonic association; it remains restrained until released or cut with risk.

For the four Witnesses and Celia, use **scripted failure on her chosen staged performances** rather than demanding dozens of level-20 save rolls that the NPCs cannot plausibly succeed at. If the Spider targets PCs, adjudicate normally; it prefers barriers, warnings, and bargaining to player deaths. Its web can be touched but is extraordinarily dangerous to destroy (see below).

### Persistent Loom — Trait

Webs the Spider has created are part of her **still-active** Incursion. They do not vanish or cease applying effects when she uses Counterfeit Dispersal. A creature touching a live strand without adequate insulation is exposed to :stat{#source.spider-focus key=backlash}. The backlash is once per discrete deliberate contact, not automatic repeated damage every turn.

**Breaking an anchored strand** (by physical damage, magical severance, or equivalent direct destruction) transfers the binding's tension to its victim. The restrained creature takes **12d10 mental** (basic Will against DC 42), then ceases to be bound by that strand. This can drop or kill a wounded NPC. The visible transfer begins before severance: observers see the web's black lines threading into the captive's flesh and memory. Warn the players of likely severe backlash; no “cut rope, free Celia with no risk” trick.

These mechanics apply to this Incursion's engineered bindings only; do **not** infer a universal rule that severing any Veiled web or restraint transfers all its Weight. Direct manipulation that safely reconfigures genuine Resonance through properly Restored Meaning may free captives without this penalty, when logically supported by play.

### Counterfeit Dispersal ◇ — Concentrate, Occult, Deception

**Trigger:** The Spider chooses to end an apparent Witness counterattack, to expose the testing Focus, or to imitate a normal Dispersal. **Frequency:** At will, but bounded by encounter fiction; not an ordinary reaction used to negate a player victory after genuine Coherence.

She folds away her visible body, feigning the 0-HP Veiled disappearance. **No HP are lost, no Ward collapses, no real Stabilization Window opens, and the current Reconstruction Pressure does not reset.** Her Focus is briefly placed within the existing 30-meter-radius test field on its plinth and she withdraws from physical obstruction. All existing webs remain.

**Tell:** The Focus continues to resonate, the web persists, and the Spider does not return until the next hypothesis is formally submitted. A character can notice all these facts without checks; interpreting them as deception is a deduction, not an obstacle hidden behind the Spider's level-20 DC.

### Counterfeit Reconstruction ◇ — Occult, Deception

**Trigger:** A completed, valid test with exactly three candidate objects that **does not** produce genuine Coherence, or a later theatrical conclusion once the fourth object is complete.

The Spider appears to reconstruct, selects a capable Witness, uses Web-Cast to incapacitate that Witness, silently advances **Independent Weave** one step if incomplete, and allows the other defenders to stage a new apparent Dispersal. This sequence is an **encounter operation / performance**, not a standard six-second combat turn or four free attacks available during ordinary PF2e initiative. One valid wrong submission triggers **at most one** performance and one Witness loss. GM narration of a fake countdown is flexible; the hidden progress is not.

### Independent Weave — Special, Craft

**Progress:** 0/4 at opening, **+1 for each counterfeit performance**. The Spider braids and enchants an actual new magical item, entirely consistent with a future unique-item design tailored to its eventual holder. The material is real manifested silk and a real newly made magical creation. The speed is a **unique level-20 Incursion crafting capability**, not the general PF2e Craft downtime activity and not an unlimited free-item technique.

At 4/4, complete the object; place it as a purported undocumented Source among the tests. At her staged final withdrawal the Spider may safely unweave the anchored bindings, releasing Celia and surviving defenders without severance backlash; this makes Celia available to issue the order to remove the unregistered object. Its newly supplied Weight is derived from the Spider's **authentic** experience of making it; it remains too new to prove it is an independently sufficient Anchor. If genuine Coherence occurs before 4/4, interrupt creation and do not secretly finish it. If genuine Coherence happens after 4/4, the object remains as an independently made magical object even if the original false divine Form collapses.

### Identity Outruns Power — Passive

**Trigger:** The original Focus and all three genuine heirlooms (02, 03, 04) join within the legitimate isolated test field with no contaminating candidates.

This establishes **genuine Coherence automatically**, not an attack and not a saving throw. The level-20 creature's divine-scale Form contracts into an ordinary craftsperson-shaped reconstruction. Remove its level-20 combat actions and oppressive bindings as that false Form stops being viable. The existing coherent figure can have an ordinary conversation and permit truthful Restoration (Relationship/Pattern/Context) and subsequently valid Anchoring; these are separate from mere Coherence. The Spider cannot use Counterfeit Dispersal or a conventional resistance roll to negate this Source truth.

**After the item is finished:** the original divine Form can still be unmasked by Coherence. The fourth object does not retroactively become a historical fourth possession or prevent the original life from being Restored; it is its own separate future-memory question.

### Canny Weaver ↻ — Occult, Mental

**Trigger:** A creature within 60 feet attempts to remove one of the Spider's three genuine Source heirlooms, examine the devotional Focus, or significantly disturb Independent Weave while she is physically manifest.

**Effect:** She may Step up to 10 feet, redirect one loose non-held web-thread or object up to 10 feet into a legal space, and speak a truthful but misleading sentence about provenance. She does not alter an archived record, rewrite ownership, or counterfeit the true objects' Weight. If the movement would forcibly move a creature, attempt a standard Reposition with +38 Athletics instead; use appropriate degrees of success and limits.

### Threaded Escape ◆◆ — Occult, Teleportation

The Spider travels along a continuous manifested web to another visible point of her web within 100 feet. The web's end points must exist; this does not teleport across unknown Source boundaries or through the Scar. It may leave a trailing line until it chooses to sever it.

### If Darkwing Forces Actual Combat

The creature is genuinely level 20, but the intended encounter is **not** to fight level-20 HP. Its stats make the bluff defensible against direct attack, and its priority is **complete the fourth object, keep the Ledger crisis believable, and leave**. It uses threats, binds, movement, and negotiation, not indiscriminate area damage. An actual hit changes its HP normally. If reduced to 0 HP through legitimate combat, apply normal Dispersal/Source rules; **Counterfeit Dispersal must not override a genuine 0-HP result**. Don't treat “I hit for 300 damage” as a narrative tickle if the combat rules actually apply.

### Witness Defender Reference (No Full NPC Combat Sim Required)

Four capable defenders hold the apparent Dispersal loop while Celia is webbed. Track four upright minis. On each valid wrong test, one becomes webbed and incapacitated. Their observed coordinated attacks are real attacks against voluntary extensions of the Spider's Form and need no repeated attack rolls while the Spider consents. If direct combat breaks out, scale individual Witnesses to the party's level; do not pretend the four NPCs can normally overpower a level-20 creature. Freeing a defender by cutting the web risks the above backlash and doesn't automatically restore the apparent Dispersal loop.

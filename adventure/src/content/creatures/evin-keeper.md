### Full Coherence
When the fourth Evin fragment Source joins the active Resonance network, the Infant, Child, and Adult reconstructions immediately cease to exist and **Keeper Evin manifests as one ordinary goblin man**. Every active Child game/countdown, carried-baby penalty, and Returning Arrow token ends. This is **not Dispersal** and does not begin Stabilization Windows for the fragment Incursions; reality is revising several incomplete conclusions into one coherent reconstruction.

While full Coherence persists, the four Evin fragment Sources jointly support Keeper Evin. For a rule that requires a single Source statistic or Reconstruction Pressure track, use the **Cloth Toy (Source 15, Class II)** as the lead Source. The other three objects remain Sources and retain their own Ward, Backlash, Weight, and Rupture statistics.

If Keeper Evin is Dispersed before Relationship is Restored, it uses the lead Source's normal Stabilization Window and can reconstruct while all four unresolved Evin fragment Sources remain connected to the Resonance network.

### Caretaker's Concern
Once full Coherence is achieved, Keeper Evin's immediate priority is Gressa's welfare rather than combat. Unless attacked or forcibly obstructed, he attempts to reach Celia and ask who is caring for Gressa.

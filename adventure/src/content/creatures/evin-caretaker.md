**Open Hand ◆** +26, reach 15 feet; 3d8+12 bludgeoning, nonlethal. A successful Strike does not automatically Grab; Grapple requires its own action. While carrying the active Bed, including an occupied Bed, the Nurse gains a +2 circumstance bonus to AC (35 total).

### Appearance and Six-Hand Inventory
The reconstruction does not resemble Evin. It embodies the *function* of caretaking: an impossibly tall featureless black nurse with an exaggerated cap and apron, six hands, instruments, medications, pillows, and blankets. Its medical costume is a false conclusion imposed by incomplete Pattern evidence; Evin was not necessarily a nurse.

* **Upper two hands:** carry the **Bed** (a distinct allied satellite creature) upright as a shield, with or without a patient.
* **Lower four hands:** can each hold one grabbed patient, or remain available for medication, attacks, and object interactions. Maximum **four hand-held patients**, subject to size and reach; one additional patient can be inside the Bed.
* Hands occupied holding patients cannot perform tasks requiring a free hand; other hands remain free. Each Grapple must be earned through the listed actions; multiple hands do not grant extra unrestricted turns.
* A hand-held creature must remain within reach; if a holding arm becomes unable to maintain contact, its Grapple ends. The Bed's restraint is independent of hand-held Grapples.
* The Nurse and Bed are **one Echo / Pattern Incursion supported by Gressa's single Source**, not two Sources, two separate mnemonic puzzles, or independent stabilization windows. The Bed is an encounter-specific satellite body with its own HP and reaction.

### Remembered Care
The Caretaker knows recurring details of Gressa's care that Gressa does not consciously remember: medications and dosages, ordinary routines, provisioning, appointments, habits, and similar repeated responsibilities preserved in the Weight. This knowledge is evidence of Pattern, not proof that the reconstruction looks like Evin or even that Evin was a nurse.

### All Hands on Deck ◆◆
**ATTACK**  
**Requirements:** At least one of the Nurse's lower hands is free.  
**Effect:** The Nurse attempts **up to two Grapple checks**, each against a **different** creature within its 15-foot reach, using Athletics +25 against the target's Fortitude DC. It needs a separate free lower hand per successful Grapple. Apply normal Grapple degrees of success and normal size restrictions for a Large creature. **Do not increase MAP between these two checks**; after both resolve, count the activity as two attack actions for subsequent MAP. A creature held in a lower hand is grabbed or restrained according to the resulting Grapple check. Both successful holds remain independent.

### Maintain Care ◇
**FREE ACTION**  
**Trigger:** Start of the Nurse's turn.  
**Effect:** Extend the duration of **all existing hand-held Grapples** until the end of the Nurse's next turn, without checks and without spending its normal actions. The Nurse must still have a hand on each target and retain reach; a successful **Escape** ends that target's hold normally. An affected creature is **not forced to make an Escape check each round**; it may attempt the ordinary PF2e Escape action on its turn, using Athletics, Acrobatics, or unarmed attack against the Nurse's **Athletics DC 35** (Athletics +25 +10), as normal. The Bed's independent restraint does not require Maintain Care.

### You'll Feel Better After This ◆
**MANIPULATE, MENTAL**  
**Requirements:** The Nurse has a free lower hand; target is within reach and either grabbed/restrained by the Nurse or secured in the Bed.  
**Frequency:** Once per target per round.  
**Effect:** The Nurse administers an impossible mnemonic sedative. The target attempts a **DC 33 Fortitude save**:

* **Critical Success:** No effect.
* **Success:** **slowed 1** until the end of its next turn.
* **Failure:** **slowed 1** for 1 minute.
* **Critical Failure:** **slowed 2** until the end of its next turn, then **slowed 1** for the remainder of 1 minute.

The sedation is supernatural medical Pattern, **not an ordinary drug, toxin, or poison**. The same slowed value does not stack with itself; use PF2e's normal rules for overlapping conditions and durations.

### Visiting Hours Are Over! ◇
**FREE ACTION, AUDITORY**  
**Frequency:** Once per turn, on the Nurse's turn only.  
**Effect:** The Nurse delivers an overwhelming *SHHH!* and sweeps its free limbs, blankets, and equipment outward. **Every other creature in a 15-foot emanation that is not hand-held by the Nurse and is not secured in the Bed** attempts a **DC 33 Fortitude save**. Gressa is not inherently exempt unless she is held or secured; the Nurse may avoid using this ability when it would endanger her.

* **Critical Success:** Unaffected.
* **Success:** Pushed 5 feet directly away from the Nurse.
* **Failure:** Pushed 10 feet directly away.
* **Critical Failure:** Pushed 15 feet directly away and knocked **prone**.

This forced movement does no damage and must end in a legal space; if blocked, move only as far as possible. The effect does not automatically break Grapples on creatures excluded from it. This is a special action, not an extra general-purpose action.

### Protective Intercession ↻
**Trigger:** A creature within 30 feet targets Gressa with a hostile effect, attempts to forcibly move her, or performs an act the Nurse interprets as an immediate threat to her safety.  
**Effect:** The Nurse Strides up to half its Speed toward the triggering creature or Gressa. If it ends within its hand reach of the triggering creature and has a free lower hand, it can make one **Open Hand** Strike against that creature. This is the **Nurse's reaction**; the Bed has a separate reaction budget.

### Back to Bed ◆◆
**MOVE**  
**Effect:** The Nurse Strides up to its Speed toward Gressa, its Bed, or a creature it believes is under its care. During this movement it attempts **one** Grapple or Reposition using Athletics +25, if an available lower hand and legal target permit. On a successful Reposition, it may move the target **up to 10 feet instead of 5** toward an appropriate bed, chair, or safe place. If a creature already grabbed in a lower hand is brought within reach of an **empty** Bed, the Nurse may instead deposit that patient into the Bed as part of this activity; the Bed immediately uses **Patient Intake** (below). Transferring a patient this way releases the Nurse's hand-held Grapple after the Bed's restraint is established. One transfer maximum per use.

### Incomplete Care / Restoration
The Nurse is not initially hostile. It performs tasks with absolute gentleness until someone interrupts care, attempts to remove Gressa, or violates its remembered routine. It cannot distinguish care from coercion. If sufficient truthful **Pattern** is Restored—recognizing the sustained responsibilities a grown child carried for Gressa—the Nurse and the Bed **both cease hostile activity immediately**, regardless of remaining HP. The reconstruction need not acquire Evin's true anatomy. Merely classifying Pattern well enough for routing is not Restoration.

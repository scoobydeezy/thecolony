## At the Table

**Objective:** protect the Ledger and establish true provenance. **Puzzle:** the original Focus plus exactly three historical heirlooms; four staged performances advance the Spider’s new craft. **Truth:** 02 teacup, 03 thimble, 04 key. **Counterfeit:** apparent Dispersal is voluntary withdrawal; persistent webs betray it. **Actual foe:** a level-20 entity, not a routine fight.

### Narrative Container — The Archive Has Become a Stage

During Totality, increased effective Resonance allows Fractured Objects and other Sources that the Witnesses previously kept in stable separate custody to activate Incursions. The Hall of Remembrance and its associated warehouses are full of manifestations. Most are neutral, misread as hostile only because their Forms are terrible. **One** dominates the Hall: an enormous, absolutely black spider resembling a false reconstruction of **Grandmother Spider / Nana Anadi**.

The Incursion is not the deity. It draws on a forgotten Cult craftsperson's genuine devotion, lived craft, and three surviving personal possessions. It has assembled the goddess's outward Form and cunning from that sincere but incomplete remembrance. Uniquely, it understands that it is a Veiled reconstruction. It wants to persist beyond Totality by making a genuinely new, powerful magical object that begins an independent history *belonging to the Spider herself*.

The Hall crisis remains an **optional Act III operation**, not another compulsory combat. Its immediate outcome governs whether the Witnesses retain the **Ledger** for *The Burden*. The Spider's independent escape attempt is a separate hidden consequence.

### Foreshadowing — The Twelve Objects Were Always Here

On an earlier visit, Celia can lead Darkwing past twelve apparently mundane objects in enchanted display cases: red mitten, blue teacup, brass thimble, iron key, spinning top, leather glove, pewter spoon, spectacles, wooden door hinge, embroidered handkerchief, lunch tin, and flower bottle. Their outward descriptions are in *:ref[the Ledger records]{#handout.ledger}*, section *First-Visit Display Labels*. **Do not** announce which three are relevant, and do not expose the GM-only reference dossier.

At Totality these twelve aren't a random assortment: **Celia has gathered them as twelve plausible candidates** after comparing the Spider's apparent Source, prior Resonance incidents, intake records, and shared provenance. Her research is a competent, unfinished investigation. Only three are the original craftsperson's relevant possessions; none of the other nine needs to be part of a complete three-item Source group.

Prior visitors know Celia, the Hall's layout, the ordinary exhibits, and the difference between Coherence and merely bringing Sources close. If Darkwing previously completed *A Life*, Celia recognizes its usefulness, invites help quickly, and the group already knows that deliberately reuniting Sources can force Coherence in an active Veiled without reducing it to 0 HP. Without that history, Celia's protocol and the scene teach the procedure.

### Long Hall — Opening State

The building is a long, monumental corridor, with exhibit alcoves and access to side archives along its length. Four armed Witness defenders face inward from two positions on either side of the Spider at the center. Celia stands at a desk at the far end, paging through the actual Ledger. The Spider spans much of the central floor and physically holds a small devotional Focus between its forelegs. Four smaller neutral manifestations continue their ordinary, occasionally frightening routines in distant alcoves.

**Physical testing geography:** Celia has marked an exclusion field of **30 meters (approximately 100 feet, or twenty 5-foot squares) in radius**, centered on a testing plinth in the central gallery. The Focus must be placed on the plinth during a test. All nonselected suspected objects must be **outside** that radius when the test occurs. The outer display/holding areas are beyond that boundary or connect through side archives far enough away; do not imply ordinary enchanted preservation cases suppress Totality Resonance. A centerline and radius ring may be drawn on the table map. This is a tuned *testing and exclusion protocol*, not a universal rule that 30 meters defeats Resonance. The hall may be lengthened on the battle map to accommodate its true radius.

As Darkwing arrives, the Witnesses coordinate an apparent defeat of the Spider. The great black Form folds in upon itself. Celia calls the team over and begins explaining that the preserved objects may belong to a single forgotten life. Before she finishes, a black web snaps from the vacant center, fixes her to the far wall, and leaves her unconscious. **The web remains when the Spider is absent.** A leg reappears, then her voice, addressing Darkwing. When she finishes speaking, she withdraws again and leaves the Focus on its plinth for the first test. The Spider is conversational, amused, exceptionally observant, and far more dangerous than the supposed fight suggests.

**Starting state:** Celia webbed, four defenders capable, the Spider between its theatrical appearances, Focus accessible only during the apparent Window, twelve candidates in outer holdings, Ledger and Celia's field note at her desk, no submitted hypotheses yet, the Spider's fourth-object craft progress **0/4**. Celia's incapacitation is the opening attack, **not** the first of the four defender losses.

### The Uncatalogued Reference Source

The original active Source is a **small worn Nana Anadi devotional shrine/focus**, visually unmistakable as a spider-and-eight-strand weaving motif. It was in the Spider's possession when Celia located the large Incursion. Its catalogue entry is extremely sparse and is reproduced in *:ref[the Ledger records]{#handout.ledger}*; it is **not** item 01–12 and is **not** one of the three missing personal possessions.

When manifested, the Spider physically guards the Focus at the center of its body; removing it by combat would require engaging the genuine level-20 entity or persuading it to yield. During each feigned Dispersal, she sets it on the central plinth to allow the next test. That is why Celia's process explicitly requires a Stabilization Window and why the party can interact with the Focus despite the Spider's overwhelming power.

### Celia's Desk — The Actual Puzzle

Celia has left **Controlled Relational Resonance — Field Procedure 4**, a legitimate experimental protocol and a short archival cross-reference index. The contents are in *:ref[the Ledger records]{#handout.ledger}*, ready to read as props. Consult *:ref[the Material Concordance]{#handout.material-concordance}* to decipher field codes; `X` references are leads, not automatic assertions of ownership. The twelve complete entries and referenced supporting documents are authored in the Ledger. The GM has a fixed solution: **02 blue teacup + 03 brass thimble + 04 iron key**.

The answer is **not** “the three items with the same code.” Three independently corroborated histories trace different routes to a *personal needlework station and locked personal cupboard* in the Pilgrim Quarter's eastern craft annex. Other items share legitimate repairs, Cult provenance, a worksite, intake batches, and devotional marks. The other nine are believable because they truly have relationships to one another or to the same region—just not this particular Forgotten life.

**Investigation is not a Mastermind-style guessing game.** Parties follow records, examine physical objects, and assemble a defensible account. An ordinary Recall Knowledge action (Society, Occultism, Crafting, or appropriately relevant lore) can decode an archival convention, locate a relevant reference, or identify corroborating observations. A check never invents false ownership or automatically chooses the correct three. Once a convention is decoded, it stays decoded. Physical evidence can be understood without a successful roll. The quality-of-information table, DC selection, and once-per-topic limit are in the mechanical reference entries linked on this page.

**The Spider's false web is untrusted testimony.** Its strands represent what she wants observers to believe, not necessarily authentic Source relationships; a Dispersal-like collapse does not remove her web. She may offer technically true but strategically irrelevant interpretations of the Ledger. None of the documentary records is secretly falsified by her.

### The Experiment — Exactly Three Candidates

During each apparent Window:

1. The Focus sits exposed on the central plinth; its original Source remains active.
2. The party selects **exactly three** of the twelve candidates. It moves the chosen items into the marked test zone using existing PF2e movement, Interact, and carrying rules.
3. **All nine other candidates must remain outside the 30-meter exclusion radius.** If additional suspect objects remain inside, the protocol is contaminated: the Witnesses' instruments say *NO VALID READING* and the players may withdraw the extras without consuming a submitted test. No brute-force pile of all twelve items can pass.
4. The party activates the existing test frame with an **Interact** action, or requests a Witness do so. This commits its hypothesis. The hypothesis remains whatever the players selected, regardless of whether they are attempting controlled containment or full Restoration.
5. If those three are **02 + 03 + 04**, the genuine Source network achieves **Coherence immediately**, as in *A Life*: the false divine Form is forced toward the ordinary forgotten craftsperson, regardless of level or current HP. If crafting is not yet complete, the Spider's fourth-object plan is interrupted. This is not an HP Dispersal and not automatically full Restoration or Anchoring; restoring authentic Relationship/Context and securing a viable Anchor remain possible follow-through.
6. Otherwise the arrangement does not produce the required Coherence. The Spider uses it as the cue for her next **Counterfeit Reconstruction**, webs one defender, theatrically falls to a coordinated Witness counterattack, and exposes the Focus again for another test. This fake cycle does **not** change the true Source topology or mechanically open a real Stabilization Window.

The party may separate and properly isolate the correct three instead of reuniting them. Genuine engineered containment can suppress their Resonance without Restoring the Forgotten person. But **only actual Coherence** forces an early end to the Spider's divine Form; simply announcing “we've separated the Sources” isn't a universal instant cure while she continues actively manifesting. The Spider can stage apparent compliance with either method after she has completed her own objective.

### Four Performances — A Clock She Invented

**This is an apparent countdown controlled by the Spider, not a real pressure-based Reconstruction clock.** A visible row of four Witness tokens represents her planned performances. The GM lets the Witnesses insist that another reconstruction is imminent, uses faux urgency and occasional six-second exchanges, and keeps the time between formal tests **flexible**. Four performances are the Spider's planned time to complete her magical creation; not a rule that a four-part archival investigation must literally be solved in twenty-four seconds. The party can reasonably take time to inspect records, but the GM should play the defenders' fear honestly. If they refuse to test indefinitely, she pressures, bargains, or changes tactics; don't secretly advance an unrelated unavoidable timer.

| Submission | Performance if genuine Coherence has **not** occurred | Witnesses still available | Secret fourth-object progress |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Opening | Celia bound; four defenders apparently disperse the Spider | 4 | 0/4 |
| First wrong test | Spider returns, webs one defender, pretends to be dispersed | 3 | 1/4 |
| Second wrong test | Repeats the play, with her weaving visibly more intricate | 2 | 2/4 |
| Third wrong test | Repeats; only one defender remains | 1 | 3/4 |
| Fourth wrong test | Last defender webbed; magical item complete; Spider stages final apparent defeat | 0 | **4/4** |

**Early correct test (before completion):** real Coherence interrupts the fabrication, and a level-20 bluff is exposed. The party does *not* need to defeat level 20 HP to change her Source truth. A truthful Restoration can still be completed with appropriate Context/Relationship and Anchor, if the party chooses to pursue it.

**After completion:** the Spider can feign the desired conclusion on **whatever three items the party next submits**, including the fourth test itself. If they're not the real trio, the three original objects remain unresolved: her theatrics are not genuine containment or Coherence. A group that notices this contradiction can still perform the true test, refuse the apparent victory, preserve or quarantine the fourth object, or negotiate. Her escape isn't guaranteed by GM fiat.

### The Webbed Witnesses — Evidence and Stakes

Celia and subsequently four defenders are pinned to walls in intensely fine, pitch-black web. They are visibly unconscious or incapacitated, apparently at mortal risk. The web **persists during the false Windows**, unlike an ended Incursion's manifested accessories. Ordinary handling causes mnemonic backlash. Slicing or otherwise destroying the strands **transfers a dangerous concentrated backlash onto the person restrained**; rescuing a defender does not simply restore an extra cycle. A player can attempt it with fair warning, but it is not a cost-free reset button. Exact action costs, consequences, and saving throws are in the mechanical reference entries linked on this page.

The Witnesses are *not* being killed automatically for an incorrect answer; the Spider has chosen a nonlethal-looking theatrical removal. It can still become lethal if the characters use reckless methods or the situation deteriorates. If she withdraws after the crisis, surviving captives are released unless another established circumstance prevents that.

### Clues the Spider Is Not Dispersing

These clues are freely observable; checks offer interpretation, not permission to notice them.

- **Impossible timing:** despite the supposed Stabilization Window, the Spider never reconstructs until a theory is formally tested. Her return is triggered by **the party's behavior**, not by accumulated Resonance Pressure.
- **Persistent webs:** Celia and other defenders remain bound through each “Dispersal.” Her creation's reality has not been interrupted.
- **The Focus never becomes truly quiet:** source-level Resonance continues through a supposed Window; successful Occultism or direct appropriate source-sensing can verify the mismatch.
- **Performance errors:** devastating blows barely concern her; she jokes between apparent deaths; her weaving is precise and continuous; and after her fourth act she suddenly becomes eager for the party to conclude.

Do not require a successful check or exact dialogue to recognize an obvious contradiction. If Darkwing challenges her, she can admit some truth, bargain, threaten, or abandon her play. **She is self-aware and cunning, not obliged to follow a script that can no longer succeed.**

### Background Incursions — The False Threats

The four most intimidating neutral manifestations are the **Cobbler** (#06 glove; gigantic shoe cutter), **Feeder** (#07 spoon; huge serving implement), **Examiner** (#08 spectacles; invasive eye exam), and **Doorkeeper** (#09 hinge; gate-like figure that opens doors). They are genuinely supported by their own Sources or wider networks not fully present in the Hall. They are not controlled by the Spider and do not become hostile merely because their Forms look threatening. Smaller benign manifestations can come from other holdings; some objects have no visible manifestation.

The party may talk, avoid, or interact with these people-shaped errors; **none is an extra answer to the Spider's three-object puzzle**. Their visual misdirection is one way the Spider can direct investigators away from her original Source network. See the mechanical reference entries linked on this page for simple optional reactions without creating four separate combat encounters.

### The Fourth Object — Her Actual Objective

During her four staged cycles the Spider weaves and enchants **a completely new, genuinely magical object**. Its final form and specific activation are deliberately left **unselected** until tailored to an actual Darkwing PC: PF2e items can grant invested benefits and special activated actions/reactions, but its power must be appropriate to the recipient. Do **not** substitute a weak decorative charm. It is not an ordinary gift or an item the party has volunteered to carry.

She contrives to leave it among the original test apparatus after her final counterfeit defeat, apparently as a **fourth undocumented Source** that resonates with the devotional Focus and the three historical possessions. The signal is **real**. The inferred antiquity/ownership is **false**: the object did not exist before the encounter. As part of her staged final defeat, the Spider voluntarily withdraws the webbing: **Celia awakens**, examines the suddenly unregistered object, and **demands that Darkwing take it out of the Hall** rather than leave such a powerful undocumented Source among thousands of others. If an earlier player action genuinely prevented Celia's recovery, surviving staff follow her written protocol and reach the same conclusion. Their concern is legitimate; the inferred age and ownership are wrong. Destruction risks Rupture and is not the default response.

The item's newness is legible: no historic wear, no accession or treatment history, and no pre-Totality handling record. The Spider is counting on the same genuine Recognition for herself that authenticated her maker's existence: the item is something **she actually created**. Its retention and use could build a new, independent mnemonic history. **It does not automatically become a sufficient Anchor for her level-20 Form or confer a conventional soul**. Continued existence after Totality remains a future question, not a free loophole.

The party can refuse to carry it, quarantine it, confront the Spider, or devise another response. Saving the Hall does not mandate carrying the Spider into a subsequent campaign.

### Resolution and Act III → Finale Handoff

The immediate operation is **protecting the Ledger and a functioning Witnesses response to use it**, not earning a combat victory over a level-20 foe. The Spider's own fourth-object outcome is recorded **separately**.

| Darkwing's commitment | Witnesses front / Ledger state / Finale Pressure | Witnesses' finale contribution (direction by Insight + Relationship) |
|---|---|---|
| **Participates meaningfully in The Tangled Web** | **Stable / +2** | Full: when assisting, make one attached Form Dormant per round; if interfering, every Form piece gains *Preserved Once* |
| **Viably delegates the crisis** | **Damaged / +1** | Half: alternate-round Dormancy assist, or *Preserved Once* on Primary Forms only |
| **Ignores it** | **Lost / +0 (functionally unavailable)** | No organized Witnesses contribution |

These are prepared consequences, subject to actual play (including a decision to destroy the Ledger). **The Witnesses front, Ledger condition, and Finale Pressure contribution are one Stable/+2, Damaged/+1, or Lost/+0 result.** That same result supplies full, half, or no *The Burden* Form intervention; **Insight + Relationship** decide whether it helps or opposes Darkwing. There is no additional Operational Integrity gate or inherited-pressure meter. Competent delegation consumes real available capacity.

**GM outcome flags:** (1) Witnesses front = Ledger finale capability = +2/+1/+0 Finale Pressure; (2) key Witness survivors and Insight/Relationship for stance; (3) correct original three identified? (4) genuine Coherence vs counterfeit defeat? (5) Spider's fourth object absent / incomplete / complete / removed; (6) Spider's future independent Source status *unresolved*. An early genuine Coherence and a post-fourth counterfeit victory should not be narrated as mechanically identical to a party capable of detecting the difference.

**True provenance versus inference; archival decoding; deliberately induced Resonance; Coherence without HP Dispersal; counterfeit Dispersal that violates learned timing; persistent manifestation webs; level-20 trickster agency; distinct original Source and newly created memory; the Witnesses' capacity vs alignment; prior A Life and Pilgrim's Rest investigations as optional leverage.**

## Ledger Consultation

## Ledger Consultation — PF2e Procedure (Encounter-Specific)

The usual action is **Recall Knowledge ◆** using Society, Occultism, Crafting, or appropriate Lore. Use a **moderate level-based DC for the actual party level** for ordinary code reading, adjusted reasonably for highly technical references or a character with the exact Lore; the exact number is deliberately party-sensitive, not the Spider's DC 42. Reading visible text, using the supplied codebook, comparing two physical objects, or identifying an obvious reference does **not** require a check.

| Degree of success | Result for a chosen entry or referenced document |
|---|---|
| **Critical success** | Extract the relevant truthful observation and an independent corroborating lead; distinguish original possession from repair, donation, or workplace contact where the document supports it. |
| **Success** | Correctly decode one meaningful technical convention and identify one relevant documented fact or reference. |
| **Failure** | Find the appropriate register/document heading or recover a plain observation, but no relationship inference. |
| **Critical failure** | No additional information from this approach on this topic; never insert false ownership or forged Ledger evidence. |

**Non-repeat rule:** A check may be attempted once per investigator per genuinely new topic/record; repeating the same skill on the same fully known line doesn't create new clues. A new document, physical comparison, different skill approach, or later independent corroboration permits a meaningful new investigation. With unlimited theatrical time between tests, don't allow mechanical roll-farming. The party can solve the puzzle by working through the actual handouts without rolling.

## Background Neutral Incursions — Behavior Only

- **Cobbler (06):** A frightening huge black artisan raises a crescent knife, then cuts soles for oversized footwear. No attacks unless meaningfully provoked; it may provide safe footwear aid.
- **Feeder (07):** A hunched immense bearer of a long hooked utensil ladles food into bowls. Its Form resembles a weapon-bearer; it is feeding people, not hunting them.
- **Examiner (08):** An enormous lens-eyed silhouette leans close and asks “One or two?”; it performs an optical examination, not an attack.
- **Doorkeeper (09):** An angular gate-like figure fills a passage, then moves politely to hold a doorway. It can accidentally obstruct traffic but is not hostile.

Other holdings may sustain smaller manifestations; their Sources do not have to be among the twelve tested objects or possess complete three-object networks.

## Sealed GM Evidence Key

### Actual four-object Source topology

1. **Original devotional Focus** (`F-00`): oldest visible reference Source held by the Spider. Its old owner is not recorded, but its genuine devotional Weight is part of the incursion's active locus. It is **not** one of the twelve candidate heirlooms.
2. **#02 blue teacup:** repeated personal use at Station W-3 (`DOC.217`), later moved through House of Abiding **Post-Reading** (`HAB.PR`).
3. **#03 brass thimble:** hand-fitted personal tool used/kept at Station W-3 (`DOC.086`), later moved with a large kit through the Cult's regular donation channel (`CULT.DON`).
4. **#04 iron key:** fitted private lock under Station W-3 (`DOC.142`) but physically recovered during **Pre-Reading** House of Abiding preparation (`HAB.Pre`), without a fully established custody route.

Three different possession types—daily use, craft practice, and private access—support **one original personal life** more convincingly than intake, manufacture, or devotional symbols alone. **This is the only trio that causes genuine Coherence with the Focus**. The original person's full name is still unknown; for this encounter reconstructing their genuine Pattern/Relationship and recognizing their ordinary identity is sufficient to break the false divine Form, while full Anchoring requires normal proof and a viable destination. The Spider knows the answer and has been delaying its discovery.

### True but misleading cross-links

- **01 mitten + 05 top** genuinely belong to a child; both have `ACC.058`.
- **01 mitten + 10 handkerchief + 02 teacup** share eight-return repair features under `PAT.017`; this is a common craft technique, not a single personal Source.
- **03 thimble + 12 bottle** shared an `ACC.113` delivery, not a donor or owner.
- **02 teacup + 11 tin** shared `ACC.041`, but came from different rooms and purposes.
- **04 key + 09 hinge** reference `LOC.021` but belong to distinct private and communal fixtures.
- **Devotional imagery and eight-loop ornaments** appear on #03, #06, #10, #12 and elsewhere; Nana Anadi-looking imagery is not a one-off answer marker.

All these leads are *true*. Their limitations are available in the actual dossier. None of the original twelve records is forged by the Spider.

### Five GM questions with fixed answers

**Why can Celia not simply identify the trio?** She selected twelve plausible exhibits and was about to compare their referenced documents. Her injury interrupts the only fluent specialist; the other Witness defenders understand fighting/containment but not the Concordance's evidentiary distinctions. The party can follow the written trail rather than receiving an answer.

**Why does the Spider make the Focus available?** She needs formal testing to continue her believable “Dispersal” routine. A correct early test is the genuine danger she accepts to buy a fourth-object history; she doesn't know the party's specific selection before it crosses the exclusion ring, but can sense the increased Resonance as it nears.

**Does an incorrect trio really stabilize her?** No. After finishing the fourth object she can counterfeit a successful ending, but the real historic Source physics are unchanged. This is a discoverable contradiction.

**Why is the fourth magical item unaccessioned?** The Spider made it **today**. Its magnetic/Resonant relation to the old trio comes from her own authentic experience crafting it while reconstructing from that Weight, not from old ownership.

**What if the party catches the trick?** Let them refuse removal, quarantine the item, attempt authentic Coherence, bargain with the Spider, or pursue other plausible options. Do not automatically rescue the Spider's plan by fiat. She remains a level-20 entity with independent agency, but prefers deception to killing everyone.

## Tuning and Safety

This is a level-20 foe regardless of party level. If the party is well below level 16, any straightforward hostile use of its high-level DCs and damage can be lethal; telegraph superiority and provide noncombat alternatives as established. The four faux cycles are **narrative pressure**, not a balanced level-20 combat encounter. Before tabletop play, tailor the fourth item to its actual recipient, verify the actual party composition and level, and test web backlashes as very dangerous *optional* interventions with clear warnings. Do not accidentally make touching Celia's web an unavoidable deathtrap or demand level-20 checks to solve a paper-provenance puzzle.
The fourth object remains intentionally tailored to an actual PC. Do not guess a recipient or finalize its powers without a party roster. It can be quarantined as an unactivated new creation in this draft; completing the Hall encounter does not force adoption.

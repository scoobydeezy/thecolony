## Definition

A **Keystone** provides a reusable way to recover a particular component of Identity. It is not a general label for an important or strategically valuable Asset.

| Component | Keystone | What it supports |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | :ref[Book of Names]{#asset.book-of-names} | Identifying which life the available evidence belongs to. |
| **Relationship** | :ref[The Ledger]{#asset.ledger} | Recovering who and what mattered through objects and their connections. |
| **Pattern** | :ref[Claims Registry]{#asset.claims-registry} | Tracing obligations, ownership, dependencies, debts, and material consequences. |
| **Memory** | :ref[Engram Vault]{#asset.engram-vault} and authentic Engrams | Recovering what a person experienced, felt, feared, or intended. |
| **Form** | :ref[Face of the Unknown]{#asset.face-of-the-unknown} | Testing truthful outward identity and boundaries against candidate representations. |
| **Choice** | :ref[Dawn Charter]{#asset.dawn-charter} | Supporting recovery of what a person chose, refused, accepted, resisted, or had imposed upon them. |

**Context has no Keystone.** Recover the circumstances that made a fact true through the encounter's local evidence.

## Procedure

### Access, Not Automatic Answers

A Keystone needs a meaningful lead. Possessing the Book does not identify every silhouette; consulting the Charter does not automatically establish someone's consent. The evidence must support the particular life and question being investigated.

Use :ref[Identity and Stabilization]{#rule.identity-and-stabilization} for evidence-dependent recovery, appropriate checks, and Restoration. Owning a Keystone neither Restores Identity automatically nor bypasses the encounter's required opening. An authentic Memory remains evidence of experience, not independent proof of every claim within it.

Control of a Keystone capability can make the Party independent of faction permission. Where a faction retains control, access follows the actual relationship and terms established in play. Apply the :ref[Book's handling rule]{#rule.book.keeper-bound-handling} when relevant; institutional access does not cancel its physical restrictions.

Every Identity component required by an encounter remains recoverable locally without a Keystone. Tools and expertise improve preparation, access, and independence; they are never mandatory completion keys.

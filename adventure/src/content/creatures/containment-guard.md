### No One Passes ◆
The Guard chooses the hole's inner or outer edge and **Readies** a Shove or Halberd Strike against the next creature entering across it. This uses the ordinary Ready rules and its reaction. A Guard is not obliged to attack a creature it recognizes as authorized; its problem is that authorization is contradictory.

### Whose Orders? ◇
**Frequency:** once per round, at the start of the Guards' shared turn. The two guards swap their stated priorities (*keep people in* ↔ *keep people out*). This changes their targeting/roleplay, not their statistics, Source, or ability to use common Pathfinder actions. If the Party demonstrates an immediate concrete post to defend, they may agree to occupy that post without a check when consistent with their behavior.

**Joint Reconstruction:** Each body can be Dispersed independently. They both use **Cell 03's** one shared Source and re-form by its normal Class II Window while their collective watch remains unresolved. A body reduced to 0 HP does not cause a Source Rupture or destroy the other Guard. A complete truth-based Name puzzle is not required to bypass these guards during The Breach.

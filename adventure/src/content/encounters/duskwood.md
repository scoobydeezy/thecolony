## At the Table

**Objective:** survive or bypass a deliberately engineered predator ambush, then determine what the forest actually explains. **Opposition:** one Vemerak initially, four ridge harriers, two gatewrights; a second Vemerak waits behind a visible gate. **Trigger:** the first reaches 150 HP; engineers then attempt the release on their normal turns. **Counterplay:** intercept engineers, reach the lever, jam the apparatus, sever the lifting cable, negotiate or retreat.

## Availability and Purpose

The Cult offers this route through Tarn. It is available alongside the Source-dependent Hall and Institute investigations. The Party enters expecting to find where the Veiled come from. What it finds has physical causes: tracks, caves, predators, engineering and people with motives. The monsters’ occult powers do not make them Veiled.

Give Vara her opportunity to ask to accompany the Party if that Trust beat remains available. She need not join a dangerous expedition to keep the campaign moving.

## Setup and Map

Use a winding ravine about 30 feet wide, ridges 30 feet above it, accessible climbing routes and two cave mouths. Show the second gate’s cable, counterweight and lever from the floor. Provide cover that can break the Vemerak’s long attack lines. Put the gatewrights far enough from the lever that they must spend a whole turn moving before a subsequent turn can operate it; check this against their actual Speed when drawing the map. They cannot release it on the threshold turn from an unseen adjacent position.

The route out remains physically usable. Do not seal every escape simply because the ambush has begun. Exact map dimensions and climb surfaces are provisional staging.

## Opening State

Kobolds occupy the upper positions and begin firing as the first gate opens. Tarn recognizes a diversion system and warns the Party. He remains a sincere roadwarden and protector. He can identify the apparatus and help someone reach safety, but he does not solve the tactical decisions for the Party.

## The Second Gate

When the first Vemerak reaches half HP, describe a shouted order and the engineers abandoning their firing positions. On their next turns they move toward the lever. The release requires reaching it and using one Interact; killing, delaying or redirecting them matters. A surviving harrier can substitute if it can actually reach the apparatus.

Use :ref[the counterweight gate]{#hazard.black-claw-gate}. Destroying its latch **opens** it; severing the lifting cable **prevents** normal opening. Explain that distinction visibly. If the second Vemerak emerges, the kobolds retreat. The predators are not trained allies and may threaten the kobolds or one another when positioning makes that natural. Do not rely on a scripted monster duel to rescue the Party.

## Opposition

- Two :ref[Vemeraks]{#creature.vemerak}, staged rather than arriving together.
- Four :ref[Black Claw Ridge Harriers]{#creature.black-claw-ridge-harrier}.
- Two :ref[Black Claw Gatewrights]{#creature.black-claw-gatewright}.

The full roster is deliberately dangerous. The fight’s practical difficulty depends on preventing the second release and on the kobolds withdrawing. No extra high-damage rockfall is included.

## Resolution

Dead creatures remain dead. Disabled machinery stays disabled until someone repairs it. Tracks lead to actual caves and bodies; nothing reconstructs from missing Identity. Captured kobolds know their own engineering and immediate orders. They do not supply proof of a Serevan conspiracy that has not been established.

The official explanation remains plausible because the Duskwood really is dangerous. It fails to explain the Colony’s mnemonic phenomena, which follow people and objects outward. Return with that distinction, not a newly learned Veiled technique. Dawn can then make its approach through :ref[An Opportunity]{#event.an-opportunity}.

## If the Party Does Not Intervene

The ambush is not a mandatory milestone. Tarn can report the dangerous diversion, but the Party has not personally established the full negative evidence. Keep the forest available for investigation; advance the Reading through its actual preparations, not an arbitrary forest deadline.

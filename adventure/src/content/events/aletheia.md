## Opening State

The last Named Boundary has made Aletheia coherent. The hostile reconstruction is gone; the burden remains.

## Aletheia's Revelation
Once coherent, Aletheia can deliver a short, deliberate revelation rather than a lore dump.

This moment should explicitly pay off:

### Theia
Aletheia gives her name.

Someone recognizes the surviving fragment inside **The Order of Theia**.

The institution has been saying part of her name for centuries without remembering the woman.

### The Book
Aletheia makes the Book's history legible.

It was a memorial before it was a ritual instrument.

The crowded early pages were not a primitive version of the Reading. They belonged to a different moral structure: the community recorded the dead because those lives remained everyone's responsibility.

Aletheia's solitary act was an answer she improvised, not a system she designed for eternity.

## The Host of Forgotten Keepers
When Aletheia becomes coherent, the other Forgotten Keepers become present around her: roughly two hundred **fully corporeal Forgotten people** whom the Party can speak with and touch (see :ref[The Burden]{#encounter.the-burden}).

They are people, not a parade of exposition scenes.

This host provides a final place to pay off open promises that the Party's route choices may have left unresolved.

Possible guaranteed or conditional recognitions include:

- **Marek Cairn**, Vara's father and the forgotten hand on the Manor map.
- **Evin Peakwalker**, the adult Keeper whose infant relationship with Gressa produced the cloth-toy Veiled in Session 2, if this promise has not already been paid through **A Life**.
- **Sundara Cobb**, as a separate Old Town relationship or nearby recovered presence if her grave was never resolved—not as a Keeper among the succession. Her final fallback must be deliberately authored, not silently assumed from the four canonical Keeper companies.
- The Keeper from Dawn's historical rescue.
- The Keeper associated with the Veiled House.

Route completion determines how much the Party already understands when these people appear.

The host should reward prior investigation without making optional routes mandatory for closure.

## Closing Unvisited Promises

Marek and Evin belong among the Keeper history where appropriate. Their recognition answers relationships actually pursued; it need not become five speeches. Sundara is **not a Keeper Company**. If Sunny was skipped, use Tom, surviving recognition and the now-legible Old Town record to offer a brief separate encounter with her history. It offers the missing relationship and a chance to choose Rest; it does not award a Stable front or undo losses. If Tom is unavailable, the grave and surviving object remain material evidence rather than an invented resurrection.

## Existing Characters React
Act III dialogue can also cash out personal institutional promises:

- Vara and Marek, depending on path and Vara's state.
- Uldren recognizing the person whose living possessions he once understood.
- Celia recognizing the life she preserved only after it had been divided into objects and records.
- Miriel recognizing what her family inherited from a Keeper no one remembered.
- Perrik seeing that his impossible records were always the material outlines of real people.

These moments should be brief and relational rather than explanatory.

## Aletheia Does Not Give the Answer
Aletheia may explain:

- What she remembers.
- Why she wrote her name.
- What she feared would happen to the names if no one accepted responsibility.
- How different the modern Reading is from the act she actually chose.
- Her horror, gratitude, uncertainty, or willingness regarding the present crisis.

She does **not** tell the Party what institution to build.

She restores the forgotten question.

## The Final Problem — Anchoring
Aletheia remains connected to:

- Every original memorial name.
- Every later Keeper.
- Every relationship carried through the inheritance.
- Every portion of that burden now distributed through the Colony.

The easiest available destination may be Aletheia herself.

Reality already knows she can carry it.

She may even be willing.

That option is not secretly evil.

It is the campaign's inherited temptation:

> **Someone can carry this. Therefore someone should.**

The Party must decide what to ask of Aletheia and what the living are willing to carry themselves.

## Crafting the Solution
The Party and the factions now construct the system that will be enacted in the Final Reading.

The solution should be assembled from capabilities that actually survived the campaign.

Useful dimensions include:

- **Recognition** — How are the lives identified truthfully?
- **Relationship** — How is meaning preserved rather than reduced to data?
- **Routing** — How does memory move toward viable destinations?
- **Destination / Anchor** — Who or what actually carries weight?
- **Capacity** — How much can each destination bear safely?
- **Consent** — Who has agreed, and what can they refuse?
- **Protection** — What prevents the transfer from destroying participants?
- **Continuity** — How will the system survive after the Party leaves?
- **Revision** — How will future generations know what question the system was built to answer and when it should change?

Faction relationships now determine what is actually available.

A hostile faction may refuse its Asset, demand terms, pursue its own answer, or require the Party to design around it.

A trusted faction may place its people, infrastructure, expertise, or authority inside a system it does not completely control.

This is where politics becomes the ending mechanic.

### Answer

> **Remembering Aletheia correctly does not settle the burden. The living must decide how responsibility will be distributed now that the person beneath the ritual is no longer forgotten.**

### Question Created

> **How will the living put their agreed answer into practice?**

## Agreeing a Viable Answer

Use :ref[Anchoring and Resolution]{#rule.anchoring-and-resolution}. Identify real destinations, Capacity, truthful Recognition and consent before implementation. Discuss any hazard or missing capacity while choices remain open. Once a viable answer is agreed, the Final Reading enacts it; no surprise test or retracted commitment follows.

:::design
The separate Sundara fallback is connective material. It preserves her non-Keeper identity without retroactively resolving her front.
:::

## Opening State

The map marks an ordinary tavern with THE RECKONING IS COMING. The Shattered can offer evidence while still having to answer for the cloth-toy operation.

## The Scene

The Broken Bell first presents as an ordinary tavern. The Observer's map supplies the password **THE RECKONING IS COMING**. Kivren admits the cloth-toy operation and explains that the Party was a useful witness, not its target. The Shattered challenge Darkwing to examine Old Town instead of accepting the official explanation that its devastation arrived from the Duskwood. Damaris insists on evidence and provenance; Tavia is willing to pursue a much more radical conclusion. Neither faction confidence nor exposure alone supplies truthful Restoration.

## Questions Worth Asking

Kivren acknowledges using the Party as witnesses. Let Darkwing confront the actual harm, including Gressa’s current state. Damaris wants verifiable evidence; Tavia is more willing to act on a radical conclusion. None should be flattened into a single faction speech.

The claim they can substantiate is that Old Town deserves direct examination. Do not give away Tom’s whole history or Sundara’s grave before the investigation. The later bell alarm remains a separate physical event; an unresolved map question about the original bell’s location is not evidence of a second named bell.

## Possible Next Developments

:ref[Old Town]{#encounter.old-town} can follow now or later. Refusal leaves the evidence available; it does not force the Party into an ambush or settle the Shattered’s methods as correct.

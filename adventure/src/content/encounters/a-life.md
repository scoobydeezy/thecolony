## At the Table

**Objective:** recognize one continuing life and Restore its mother–son Relationship. **Trigger:** the intact toy reaches the Hall. **Track:** baby carrier and comfort cooldowns, Child’s Hide/Count state, two returning arrows, and four physical Sources. **Finish:** Coherence reveals an ordinary adult; recognizing his responsibility for Gressa ends hostility and permits Anchoring.

## Availability

Run this route if the toy remains a Source and the Party brings it to the Witnesses. It is an alternative to the living-Gressa route, not a required second version of the same lesson. Give Vara the third Trust opportunity if she is present: let her ask to continue with Darkwing without making her attendance compulsory.

### Narrative Container
The intact cloth toy reaches Celia Harthwright and the Hall of Remembrance. The Hall contains thousands of Fractured Objects whose provenance has been forgotten. No label says that any object belonged to Evin. Twelve otherwise ordinary, numbered display objects are already visible on a first Hall visit; their repeatable first-visit labels live in :ref[the Ledger records]{#handout.ledger}. Do **not** pre-expose the later Spider's three objects or interpret those displays as an encounter clue yet. The Ledger records relationships and circumstances around holdings without preserving the missing Name.

Four objects ultimately participate:
- **Cloth toy** — infancy; the Source that produced the enormous infant in *Bad Maps*.
- **Carved hobby horse / stick horse** — childhood; the letters **E.P.** are carved into the wood.
- **Bow** — young adulthood; the letters **E.P.** are cut discreetly into its grip.
- **Shaving case** — recovered from the House of Abiding after its owner's Reading; likewise associated with **E.P.** through Ledger description.

**Encounter signature:** A tiny but deafening infant must be passed from caretaker to caretaker; a goblin-horse child forces an involuntary game of hide-and-seek; and a four-armed adult archer fires two bows whose misses return once for a second attempt. These are not three accurate likenesses. Each is reality's extravagantly literal conclusion about a true slice of a single life.

### Opening State and Readable Props
- The **Infant** is recognizably the same baby as the enormous *Bad Maps* reconstruction, now Tiny rather than Huge. Its cry, dependence, and gestures provide the continuity.
- The **Child** is an uncanny goblin-centaur hybrid, as though the child who played at being a horse has become the horse. It giggles and treats everything as a game. The hobby horse is a physical archival Source, not an abstract clue.
- The **Adult** is an absurdly capable archer, with four arms and two longbows. The historical fact was skill with a bow; the false conclusion is that no normal body could have been so skilled.
- Place real, separately identifiable **E.P.** markings on the hobby horse, bow, and shaving case props or clue cards. The repeated initials are evidence, not a required skill check or the answer by themselves.
- Use a **Baby carrier marker**, **You're Not Mommy** condition markers, a **Child game-state/countdown** marker, and up to **two Returning Arrow** tokens. These make the encounter legible without tracking hidden locations.

### General Flow
1. **Ordinary processing creates the accident.** The newly arrived cloth toy is placed near the childhood object during normal Hall procedure. Their compatible Relationship fragments recognize the same absent center.
2. **First Resonance: infant + child.** Two fragmentary Echoes manifest. Infant Evin is physically dependent and intermittently screams, imposing Deafened/Stupefied. A character can hold and Comfort the baby, but a successful Comfort grants that creature **You're Not Mommy**, preventing it from comforting again for one minute. Holding the baby imposes Clumsy, Off-Guard, and Encumbered; passing it around becomes an actual tactical choice.
3. **Child's first game: Hide.** The goblin-horse hides with audible giggling. Creatures that hear the giggle become Fascinated, with normal PF2e ways to end that condition; do not artificially prolong it through later damage. The GM rolls the Child's current hiding difficulty and the party uses Seek. If the Child is not found before its next turn, the missed-search consequence threatens Slowed, Sickened, and Stunned.
4. **First discovery produces the bow.** When Darkwing first finds the Child, the creature bounds out and collides with or retrieves the archived bow. This brings the actual **E.P. bow Source** into range of the already-active fragment network. **Adult Evin manifests, hostile.** This is the existing third-Source trigger made into a visible payoff of play rather than a separate archival coincidence.
5. **Second game: Seek.** After being found, the Child vanishes from ordinary view and begins counting down from ten. It counts two numbers for each of its own actions, completing the count after five actions. Darkwing must become **Hidden or Concealed** before the count finishes; those who have neither status when the count completes take the encounter's mental damage. The Child then returns to its Hide game. The party must use ordinary cover, concealment, movement, spells, and stealth tools; the GM need not manage a catalogue of Child hiding spots.
6. **Adult pressure.** The four-armed Adult uses paired bows against different targets. An arrow that misses or critically misses remains circling its original target, makes exactly one return Strike at the **start of the Adult's next turn**, then vanishes whether it hits or misses. Returning arrows are visible tokenized threats, not an unlimited projectile swarm.
7. **Celia recognizes the pattern.** The matching **E.P.** initials on the hobby horse and bow, together with the same absent relational center, suggest one continuous life rather than unrelated apparitions. The clue is available from the objects; coordinated performances by the three Echoes are not required.
8. **The Ledger is taught as a tool.** Celia directs an assistant or Darkwing to the Ledger entry describing an unidentified personal shaving case recovered from the House of Abiding. The description and initials **E.P.** corroborate the connection without supplying a Name.
9. **The fourth Source is deliberate.** Celia instructs the group to bring the shaving case into the active resonance field. Darkwing deliberately *increases* Resonance to test the shared-life hypothesis.
10. **Third Resonance: Coherence.** The exaggerated Infant, Child, and Adult collapse into **one ordinary goblin man**, Keeper Evin. This is neither the dispersal of the three nor automatic Restoration. It is reality revising several partial, absurdly literal conclusions into one coherent person-shaped reconstruction. Child game markers and returning arrows end with that reconstruction.
11. **Coherence is not Restoration.** Keeper Evin's first concern is Gressa. He asks Celia who is looking after his mother. Darkwing or Celia recognizes that **the baby, horse-child, archer, and Keeper are Gressa's one son**, whose adult life included taking responsibility for the mother who raised him.
12. **Relationship is Restored.** The false reconstruction becomes untenable and hostility ends, without requiring Keeper Evin to reach 0 HP. The coherent Veiled may remain briefly as a stable, non-hostile reconstruction for a handful of questions.
13. **Anchoring.** The Weight settles into a valid connected vessel, preferably the cloth toy. Preserved in truthful Relationship with the other objects, the same Source that once generated an impossible baby becomes an Anchor for a whole life.

### Player Leverage
- Pick up, hold, Comfort, and pass the infant among available caregivers, weighing its carrier penalties against its Scream.
- Spend ordinary Seek actions to find the Child against a GM-rolled Stealth difficulty; ending the infant's Scream indirectly removes its condition pressure from those checks.
- Find ways to become Hidden or Concealed before the Child counts to zero; attack, reposition, or control the Adult despite the returning arrows.
- Identify the repeated **E.P.** marks; use Celia and the Ledger to connect the shaving case; move the final Source into the Resonance network.
- Disperse fragment Echoes for temporary relief, break Reconstruction Conditions by separating Sources, or reach full Coherence and truthfully Restore the Relationship.

### Restoration / Truth Condition
> **Gressa's child did not remain an infant. These apparently incompatible reconstructions are parts of one life. Her grown son eventually cared for her, then became a Keeper.**

Do not require a character to recite this wording. What matters is recognizing the actual continuing mother–son Relationship. The initials and archery skill alone are not sufficient, but they are meaningful evidence.

### Resolution
The Hall can preserve the four objects together without reproducing the false four-life reconstruction. Darkwing learns that **separation is containment; truthful relationship is resolution**. The three aggressively bizarre Echoes resolving into one ordinary goblin anticipates, at small scale, the eventual distinction between a false reconstruction and a recovered person.

### Systems Established
**Coherence, distributed/related Sources, deliberate Resonance, Identity Restoration during an active Incursion, hostility ending through Restoration, Source → Anchor transition, Ledger as Relationship evidence.**

### Tuning and Playtest Watch
The Child's status conditions, the Infant's Scream, and the Adult's returning arrows are intentionally oppressive when they overlap. The concrete DCs/damage/action costs are specified in the mechanical reference entries linked on this page. Test whether the combined action and status pressure creates meaningful choices without making an unlucky opening round functionally unwinnable. Do not dilute the condition-driven identity merely to smooth expected damage.

## If the Party Does Not Intervene

The Witnesses separate compatible objects and retain them under quarantine. This prevents further reconstruction while the condition is broken; it does not discover Evin’s Name or restore the whole life offscreen. Keep the records and objects available for a later visit. Celia can describe what happened without granting the Party an unearned personal relationship or Keystone.

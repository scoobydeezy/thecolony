## At the Table

**Objective:** strip borrowed Form and reveal the Relationships used to build the supposed First Keeper. **Board:** six Form slots with Primary/Secondary pieces, active and Dormant states, restoration threshold and visible replacement supply. **Damage:** opens the truth; no final recitation check.

Use the **contribution table in :ref[The Burden]{#encounter.the-burden}** to select full, damaged or absent capability before applying a faction’s phase procedure. The phase’s full-strength descriptions do not override a Damaged front.

## Identity
**Form + Relationship**

## Faction sockets
- **Cult of the Unknown → Form**
- **Witnesses → Relationship**

## Encounter Parameters

Phase II remains:

- **Incursion:** Creature 15 / Party Level +3
- **Source:** same Level 15, Class V Book of Names
- **Arena:** ruined Ritual Chamber
- **Core combat problem:** destroy the Composite while stripping finite borrowed Forms from it
- **Final-exam escalation from Shape of Someone:** the earlier Shape could embody three active Forms; this Composite can sustain **all six Claim families at once**

The six Form families are:

1. Assault
2. Control
3. Mobility
4. Guard
5. Influence
6. Sustain

Each family has:

> **two candidate relics**

for a total of **twelve possible Forms** in the chamber.

The Cult’s Assist removes **one entire Form family**, meaning both relics belonging to that family are unavailable.

## Interphase Stabilization Window

When the Monument reaches 0 HP and collapses through the Ritual Chamber dome, Phase I’s Wound Incursion is Dispersed.

The Book of Names lands exposed on or near the shattered dais.

Because the Source is Class V:

> **Stability Threshold: :stat{#source.book-of-names key=stability-threshold}**

Reconstruction Pressure begins at 0.

Every non-movement action taken during the Window adds the normal **1d2 Reconstruction Pressure** after it resolves.

This creates a legitimate short recovery opportunity.

Darkwing may choose to spend actions on:

- Battle Medicine;
- Heal;
- Lay on Hands;
- potions;
- condition removal;
- reloading;
- stance / preparation actions;
- other recovery or setup.

The cost is obvious:

> **Healing accelerates the next reconstruction.**

That is acceptable and intentional.

The Party can decide how much recovery is worth how quickly reality rebuilds.

## Keeper-Bound Book During the Window

The ordinary Ward is absent, but :ref[Keeper-bound Handling]{#rule.book.keeper-bound-handling} still applies. Looking or reading an exposed page is permitted; unauthorized handling fails and triggers the Book’s derived Backlash.

## Phase II Reconstruction

When Reconstruction Pressure exceeds :stat{#source.book-of-names key=stability-threshold}:

1. the Book rises into the air;
2. its Ward reforms;
3. a black void blooms around the closed Book;
4. pieces of the ruined chamber begin tearing free and flying toward it;
5. the Composite assembles around the void.

Possible pieces include:

- a face from a dome fresco;
- the broken stone face of the Monument;
- the Order sigil torn from the floor;
- the carved arm of a cherub or saint;
- collapsed column sections;
- a long torn ceremonial banner;
- a sword hanging on the chamber wall;
- a ceremonial shield;
- the broken arm of the Monument;
- processional reliefs;
- wings from a carving;
- other later Keeper imagery.

The resulting body is not anatomically coherent.

It is:

> **just coherent enough to fight.**

The closed Book remains visibly suspended in the black central void.

## Cult Interaction

## Assist — Deny a Form Family

Before the Composite finishes assembling:

> **Darkwing chooses one of the six Form families.**

The Cult’s learned practice prevents the reconstruction from receiving that category of Form.

Both candidate relics belonging to that family remain inert.

That entire slot is unavailable for the phase.

Examples:

- remove Assault entirely;
- remove Guard entirely;
- remove Sustain entirely;
- etc.

This is intentionally stronger than removing one specific piece.

The Cult has learned how **not** to supply a category of false Form.

## Interfere — Feed the Manifestation

The Cult receives the reconstruction as revelation and makes manifestation easier.

Effect:

> **The Composite has 4 actions per turn instead of 3.**

That fourth action may be used normally, including for **Recompose Form**.

This is intentionally powerful.

The Composite has **4 actions per turn** while Cult interference is active.

---

## Witness Interaction

## Assist — Restore Provenance

Once per round, the Witnesses identify one active relic as belonging to another Keeper / another relationship.

Darkwing chooses one attached Form.

> **That Form immediately becomes Dormant.**

No attack roll.

No damage requirement.

This does not damage Composite body HP.

If a Secondary remains for that family, the Composite may still spend an action to Recompose.

The Witnesses therefore accelerate the consumption of the Composite’s finite Form inventory.

## Interfere — Preserve the Relic

The Witnesses refuse to let meaningful remnants be destroyed.

The first time **each individual Form piece** would become Dormant:

> **it immediately reattaches after the triggering action resolves at full 45 HP.**

This costs the Composite no action.

Mark that specific relic:

> **Preserved Once**

The next time that same relic is removed:

> it becomes Dormant normally.

Thus every relic effectively gets **two lives** under Witness interference.

The failure remains sympathetic:

> “Don’t destroy that. It mattered.”

They are correct.

It mattered.

It simply belonged to somebody else.

## Form Materials

The Composite body itself is a black mnemonic void and has **no general material resistance or weakness**.

Its attached Forms are actual borrowed relics.

Every Form therefore carries a material profile.

Use the following shared profiles rather than inventing a separate resistance line for every relic.

## Stone / Carved Masonry

**Resistance** 5 fire, piercing, slashing  
**Weakness** 10 bludgeoning, electricity, sonic

## Metal

**Resistance** 5 fire, piercing, slashing  
**Weakness** 10 electricity, 5 sonic

## Painted Plaster / Fresco

**Resistance** 5 piercing  
**Weakness** 10 bludgeoning, sonic

## Textile

**Resistance** 5 bludgeoning  
**Weakness** 10 fire, slashing

These modifiers apply only to the targeted Form piece.

They do not transfer to the Composite body.

A Form's special ability and its physical material are independent: the material determines how the relic is easiest to destroy; the Form family determines what capability it grants while attached.

The table sees:

## Damage Thresholds

The Composite begins at:

> **440 HP**

Damage should both reveal the void beneath and forcibly strip false Form.

---

## 330 HP — 75%

Darkwing chooses one attached Form.

It tears loose and becomes Dormant.

The first unmistakable section of the black void becomes visible.

The Party sees:

> **there is no body hidden underneath the relics.**

The relics **are** the body.

---

## 220 HP — 50%

Darkwing chooses another attached Form.

It tears loose and becomes Dormant.

The creature becomes visibly less capable of resembling any coherent individual.

The detached relic briefly exposes a truthful relationship belonging to somebody else.

---

## 110 HP — 25%

Darkwing chooses another attached Form.

It tears loose and becomes Dormant.

The closed Book is now plainly visible through the gaps.

The black void is holding the remaining borrowed material together by mnemonic force rather than resemblance.

The institutional “First Keeper” image is nearly gone.

## Relationship Restoration Through Form Removal

Every removed relic should receive a **brief deterministic narrative beat**.

Not an investigation.

Not a skill roll.

Just enough truth to establish:

> **This belonged to somebody else.**

Possible beats:

- the cherub arm briefly overlays a later Keeper comforting someone;
- the shield reveals the actual person its imagery memorialized;
- the fresco face resolves as a later Keeper beside a family member;
- the ceremonial banner becomes a later civic event;
- the Order sigil reveals a later office rather than an original act;
- the candelabrum belongs to later ritual display, not to the nameless woman.

Darkwing does not need to catalogue all twelve.

The repeated evidence establishes the governing truth:

> **The relationships are genuine. The ownership is wrong.**

Thus one mechanical act handles both Identity components:

**Form**
> This piece does not belong to Aletheia.

**Relationship**
> It belongs to another life.

## Phase-end conditions

Phase II ends under either condition.

## A — Composite body reaches 0 HP

All remaining attached Forms are immediately torn away and become Dormant.

Unused reserve Forms become inert.

or:

## B — All available Form families are exhausted

No candidate Form remains capable of describing the figure.

The Composite immediately collapses even if body HP remains.

Either route is valid.

---

## Phase II Restoration

Once the Composite collapses:

- Monument stone falls inert;
- fresco fragments fall away;
- ceremonial relationships detach;
- civic iconography stops adhering;
- all unused relics become inert;
- the enormous assembled “First Keeper” disappears.

The truths become unavoidable:

**Form**
> There was one woman here, not this assembled saint.

**Relationship**
> These borrowed relationships belong to later lives, not to her.

No final check is required.

> **Form + Relationship are Restored.**

## Phase II → Phase III Transition

The black void contracts.

The closed Book remains.

One human-scale black Veiled silhouette remains with it.

For the first time, the reconstruction has:

- one body;
- one continuous boundary;
- no borrowed monumental anatomy;
- no accumulated civic costume.

The Book falls into / is taken by the figure's hands.

The Book remains:

> **closed**

The Echo looks toward Darkwing, turns toward the old Dawn breach, and runs into the tunnel.

It does not open the Book until Darkwing follows.

The Party may recover in the ruined chamber before beginning Phase III.

## Phase II Table State

Track:

- Composite HP;
- six labeled Form-family slots;
- one active relic token/card in each slot;
- one visible reserve relic per family;
- Dormant markers / face-down cards;
- **Preserved Once** markers if Witnesses interfere;
- Book token visibly inside the Composite;
- Cult-disabled family / Form marker if applicable.

The table should always be able to answer:

> Which six capabilities are active?

> Which families still have a reserve?

> Which Forms are gone forever?

No hidden Form bookkeeping.

## State Handed On

Retain Form and Relationship Restoration, all earlier Identity, actual damage and resources, removed Form families and the stance/capacity of contributing factions. The opening after this phase permits recovery at the pace explicitly described in Phase III; it does not restore expended daily resources by default.

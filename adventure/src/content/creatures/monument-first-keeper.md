The Monument's resistances and weaknesses above are its **Stone Resistance** and **Stone Weakness**.

### Stationary Actuator

The Monument cannot:

- Stride;
- Step;
- Fly;
- Burrow;
- be forcibly moved.

It can otherwise interact with ordinary PF2E conditions unless the condition logically requires bodily movement.

It can still be:

- off-guard;
- frightened;
- stupefied;
- slowed;
- stunned;
- debuffed;
- critically hit;
- subjected to precision damage when otherwise legal.

---

## Ray-Fed Resistance

Each intact ray emitter supplies:

> **resistance 2 to all damage**

to the Monument.

The eight emitters therefore begin by supplying:

> **resistance 16 to all damage**

Destroying one ray immediately reduces Ray-Fed Resistance by 2.

Ray-Fed Resistance does **not** stack with the Monument's Stone Resistance. Use the higher applicable resistance normally.

The Monument's Stone Weakness still applies normally. This means its material vulnerabilities become progressively more pronounced as the institutional ray network is dismantled.

The ray emitters themselves do **not** benefit from Ray-Fed Resistance.

This creates the Phase I version of the final encounter's recurring lesson:

> **Deal with the supporting structure and the boss becomes easier to hurt.**

## The Eclipse

At encounter start, the outer glass globe of the eclipse sculpture shatters.

The Monument raises its arms.

The enormous stone sphere tears free through mnemonic force and flies into its upstretched hands.

The forced-perspective artwork becomes physically true:

> **The First Keeper now appears to hold the eclipse.**

The stone sphere is not a separate creature and does not have its own HP pool.

It is the Monument's primary direct weapon.

## Processional Actions

The Monument has two **PROCESSIONAL** actions:

- **Charge the Eightfold Pattern**
- **Make Way**

A Processional action does **not** resolve immediately.

It visibly marks the battlefield, then resolves at the **start of the Monument's next turn** before the Monument takes actions.

Every PC gets one full turn to react.

> **The Monument never resolves an untelegraphed Processional Pattern.**

The Monument can use only one Processional action per turn and cannot use the same Processional action on consecutive turns.

Default cadence:

> **Eightfold Pattern → Make Way → Eightfold Pattern → Make Way**

The stone-ball attack remains immediate.

## Charge the Eightfold Pattern ◆

**PROCESSIONAL, CONCENTRATE**

Every intact ray enters one of four visible states.

The triangular ray itself supplies the iconography:

| Glyph | State | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **▲** | **Air Only** | flying creatures in this sector are threatened |
| **△** | **Whole Sector** | both flying and grounded creatures are threatened |
| **▼** | **Ground Only** | grounded creatures in this sector are threatened |
| **▽** | **Disabled** | this intact ray does not discharge this cycle |

**Disabled** is not the same thing as **broken**.

A Disabled ray:

- still physically exists;
- still supplies its 2 points of Ray-Fed Resistance;
- may activate normally in a later Pattern.

A broken ray:

- supplies no resistance;
- cannot discharge;
- has no state at all.

When the Monument Charges the Pattern, place or reveal the eight state glyphs directly on the eight physical ray emitters.

The battlefield itself is not numbered.

Darkwing must read the Monument's actual geometry.

At the start of the Monument's next turn, resolve **Eightfold Discharge**.

### Eightfold Discharge

A creature is threatened when its current movement state matches the ray state of the sector it occupies:

- **▲ Air Only:** flying creatures only;
- **△ Whole Sector:** all creatures;
- **▼ Ground Only:** non-flying creatures only;
- **▽ Disabled:** no creature.

A threatened creature attempts:

> **DC 36 basic Reflex**

**Damage:** 8d8 force

**Failure:** full damage and pushed 10 feet horizontally away from the Monument.  
**Critical Failure:** double damage, pushed 20 feet horizontally away from the Monument, and knocked prone; a flying creature instead descends 20 feet if possible and falls prone if it reaches the ground.

A broken ray makes that sector completely safe for the cycle regardless of the Pattern card.

### Pattern cards

Prepare eight Pattern cards.

Each card represents the entire eight-ray ring, oriented to Founder’s Way rather than to board numbers.

In text form, read the glyphs clockwise beginning with the ray aligned to the processional axis. At the table, render them as a circular ring so the Party must map the predicted geometry onto the physical pedestal.

| Pattern | Clockwise ray states |

| 3 | ▼ △ ▽ ▲ △ ▲ ▽ ▼ |

| 8 | ▽ △ ▲ ▼ △ ▼ ▽ ▲ |

Every Pattern contains:

- two Air Only rays;
- two Whole Sector rays;
- two Ground Only rays;
- two Disabled rays.

This prevents flight from becoming a universal escape hatch.

Changing altitude may solve one ray state and make another worse.

The Party must actually read the Pattern.

### Altitude changes

A grounded creature can become airborne before the discharge.

A flying creature can land before the discharge.

That is useful, but it is no longer sufficient by itself because:

- Whole Sector rays threaten both;
- Disabled rays are safe for both;
- neighboring sectors may have different states.

Movement remains the primary answer.

## Make Way ◆

**PROCESSIONAL, CONCENTRATE**

The processional axis activates.

The battlefield telegraphs two simultaneous constraints:

1. two long side bands illuminate along Founder’s Way, leaving a **20-foot-wide central lane**;
2. the ground within **15 feet of the Monument pedestal** begins pulsing with outward processional pressure.

At the start of the Monument's next turn, resolve **The Procession**.

### The Procession

Centuries of remembered:

- citizens;
- guards;
- banners;
- ropes;
- attendants;
- ceremonial barriers

appear simultaneously.

Every creature that is either:

- outside the 20-foot central lane; or
- within 15 feet of the Monument pedestal

is swept into the ceremonial geometry.

Move each affected creature by forced movement to the nearest legal unoccupied square that is:

- inside the central lane; and
- at least **20 feet from the Monument pedestal**.

Then each affected creature attempts:

> **DC 36 basic Reflex**

**Damage:** 6d8 bludgeoning

**Failure:** full damage and knocked prone.  
**Critical Failure:** double damage and knocked prone.

The remembered side crowds remain impassable until the **end of the Monument's current turn**.

Then they vanish.

The attack therefore does two jobs:

> **compress the Party into a narrower lane**

and:

> **flush PCs away from camping at the Monument's base.**

This creates the setup for Eclipsefall.

## Eclipsefall ◆◆

**ATTACK**

The Monument hurls the giant stone eclipse into the battlefield and recalls it through mnemonic force after impact.

Choose a point within 60 feet.

Creatures in a:

> **15-foot burst**

attempt:

> **DC 36 basic Reflex**

**Damage:** 10d8 bludgeoning

**Critical Success:** no damage.  
**Success:** half damage.  
**Failure:** full damage.  
**Critical Failure:** double damage and knocked prone.

No additional supernatural rider is needed.

> **It is a giant rock.**

After Make Way, the tactical answer is:

> **Get inside the lane, get away from the pedestal, and spread out along the lane's length.**

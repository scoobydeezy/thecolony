## Operate or Disable

One Interact at the lever releases the counterweight and lifts the stone gate. Anyone can do this. The arrangement is visible: identify which rope lifts the gate and which latch holds the counterweight without a secret check.

**Jam ◆◆** (manipulate): adjacent to the apparatus, attempt Crafting or Thievery DC 28. Success disables normal operation until cleared. Failure leaves it working and deals no damage. A gatewright can attempt to clear a jam as described in its profile.

| Component | AC | Hardness | HP | At 0 HP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Release latch | 24 | 5 | 35 | Releases the counterweight; gate opens if lifting path works |
| Lifting cable | 24 | 0 | 20 | Gate cannot be lifted normally |
| Stone gate | 20 | 10 | 120 | Opens a passage for the second Vemerak |
| Counterweight rigging | 22 | 5 | 50 | Normal opening mechanism disabled |

Damage affects the named component; it does not subtract from every component. Explain the foreseeable consequence before a character attacks it. The mechanism has no damaging trap routine. Broken cables or rigging cannot be restored with a single Interact.

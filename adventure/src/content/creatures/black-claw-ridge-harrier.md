## Ridge Withdrawal ↻

**Trigger:** a hostile creature finishes a move adjacent to the harrier. **Effect:** Step 5 feet along the ridge into a legal space. This spends its reaction; it grants no protection from falling or other reactions.

## Prepared Venom

The first bolt fired is coated with a local spider-derived venom: on a damaging hit, DC 26 Fortitude; failure deals 2d6 poison damage, critical failure 4d6. No ongoing stages. Subsequent bolts are unpoisoned. This is a custom poison, not a published item.

## Objective

Keep intruders on the ravine floor while the gates open. Once the second predator is released, use prepared paths to escape. These kobolds can surrender; they have no loyalty to the Vemeraks.

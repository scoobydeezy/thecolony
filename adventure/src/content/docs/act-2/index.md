## The Act in Brief

The Reading has changed what the Party can recognize, not settled what the Colony should do. Presence leads the stopped path toward the Manor; absence leads the successful path there. Five investigations expose useful institutional methods and the mistakes hidden inside them.

## The Act Question

:::branch{when=path.the-reading-is-stopped}
> **What did the Party save, and what did it leave unresolved?**
:::

:::branch{when=path.the-reading-succeeds}
> **Who did the Party agree to forget, and what remains after the system works?**
:::

On both paths, the shared deeper question is:

> **What information is required before a forgotten life can be reconstructed truthfully?**

## Emotional Progression

:::branch{when=path.the-reading-is-stopped}
> **Relief → opportunity → doubt → responsibility**
:::

:::branch{when=path.the-reading-succeeds}
> **Absence → pursuit → recognition → responsibility**
:::

## Routes Through the Act

The routes are optional and independent: Name at Old Town, Form at Pilgrim’s Rest, Memory in the Vault, Choice in the tunnel, and Context in the Veiled House. About two substantial investigations is the pacing expectation; no completion count or full Keystone collection gates the endgame. The Clearing House remains a support resource throughout.

## Pressure and Convergence

Use faction operations as a soft clock: seals, recalls, relocation, preparation and independent NPC choices. The Confluence begins when investigation becomes implementation. The Observer’s complete reveal ends the act.

On the stopped path, the eclipse begins during the Confluence and Vara’s completed second attempt is rejected. On the successful path, the Observer’s question helps restore Relationship and the eclipse then begins without a new Reading. Act III opens with the eclipse already underway on both paths.

## What Changes by the End

Record recovered Identity, Keystone access, people and Assets preserved, faction stance, open personal promises and what the Party asked Vara to carry. No completed route makes its institution wholly right. Missing lessons remain locally available when needed later.

## Resources: the Clearing House

:ref[The Clearing House]{#location.clearing-house} is circled on the Observer's map. :ref[Perrik]{#npc.perrik-tallow} and the :ref[Claims Registry]{#asset.claims-registry} are Act II's **cross-reference engine**: the Party can revisit them between substantial routes and bring whatever it has recovered.

The Claims Registry traces the material wake a life leaves: property, debt, dependents, payments, maintenance, inheritance, contracts, civic obligations and supply chains. For example:

- Broken Bell evidence can expose Old Town ownership discontinuities.
- An EGRESS subject number can be connected to a vanished household.
- Dawn excavation evidence can reveal historical purchases or false property claims.
- A Veiled House object can be traced through taxes, repairs or transfers after its owner was forgotten.

> **Forgetting a person does not erase what their life changed.**

Bring a specific record, object, obligation or absence. Perrik traces a material connection and explains its limits; he does not conjure an absent Name from accounting. The Party does not need to own the Claims Registry: reliable access to Perrik and the Clearing House can be enough to make Pattern available later. Record any bargain, personal trust or access restriction established here for his finale contribution.

The local administrator cares about the Colony's functioning; the wider Consortium also sees commercial value in contracts that remain reliable here. Keep those motives distinct when negotiating.

## Suggested Pacing

Allow each investigation its full procedure and aftermath. Session numbers are planning estimates. Communicate urgency through visible consequences rather than cutting off a third route by fiat.

## At the Table

**Objective:** dismantle the ritual architecture and expose the mismatch with Aletheia’s original act. **Board:** eight sectors with Ground/Air layers, ray emitters, visible pattern cards, Monument and any Graspers. **Damage:** every emitter and threshold matters; 0 HP earns Restoration, with no repeated full-health phase.

Use the **contribution table in :ref[The Burden]{#encounter.the-burden}** to select full, damaged or absent capability before applying a faction’s phase procedure. The phase’s full-strength descriptions do not override a Damaged front.

## Identity
**Context + Pattern**

## Faction sockets
- **Shattered → Context**
- **Aspis → Pattern**

## Encounter Parameters

Phase I is tuned for a **six-character, level-12 Darkwing party** (Darkwing is expected to be level 12 or 13, possibly higher).

- **Incursion:** Creature 15 / Party Level +3
- **Source:** Level 15, Class V
- **Baseline encounter:** Monument plus its eight destructible ray emitters
- **Shattered interference:** adds three Creature 10 control manifestations
- **Arena:** approximately **60 × 60 feet / 12 × 12 five-foot squares**

The Monument is intentionally hittable for a final boss.

Its durability comes from:

- large HP;
- ray-supplied resistance;
- positional pressure;
- optional Grasper adds;

rather than inflated AC.

## The Source Beneath the Monument

Use :ref[the Book of Names]{#source.book-of-names}. It remains on the Ritual Chamber dais beneath the battlefield and cannot ordinarily be reached in Phase I. The Monument is the Wound’s Pattern-resonant actuator, not the Source. Attacking it damages the Incursion normally, not the Book’s Ward.

## Arena

The full Founder’s Way mall is much larger than the encounter area. Phase I uses only the Monument end of it.

Use:

> **60 feet × 60 feet — 12 × 12 five-foot squares**

The Monument stands on a large round pedestal whose design mirrors the Order of Theia symbol:

- a round center;
- **eight triangular rays** around it.

Founder’s Way passes through the arena along the processional axis.

Farther down the mall stands the eclipse sculpture:

- a large stone sphere;
- suspended inside a glass sphere;
- positioned so that, from the intended viewing angle, the distant First Keeper appears to hold an impossibly large eclipse between raised hands.

At encounter start, that artistic illusion becomes literal.

### Sector map

Number the eight 45-degree sectors around the pedestal **1–8 clockwise**.

Every battlefield square belongs to one sector.

A creature checks the sector containing the center of its space.

The eight ray emitters at the pedestal are physical battlefield objects and correspond one-to-one with these sectors.

## What Darkwing fights

The **Monument itself comes to life**.

This is not a stone golem pretending to be Aletheia.

> **The Monument is the Wound using the Colony's inherited answer as a body.**

The Monument remains architectural:

- it does not walk;
- the pedestal and ceremonial geometry remain part of the Incursion;
- the battlefield is its primary weapon.

The larger Wound includes:

- Monument;
- pedestal;
- Founder’s Way;
- processional geometry;
- ritual architecture;
- repeated civic alterations;
- Ritual Chamber beneath;
- the accumulated Pattern built around Aletheia’s act.

The fantasy is:

> **The city’s answer stands up.**

## Shattered Interaction

## Assist — Broken Rays

Before or as initiative begins, Kivren and the surviving Shattered operation detonate prepared charges at the Monument base.

Darkwing chooses:

> **two adjacent ray emitters**

Those rays are destroyed before the fight begins.

Effects:

- those two sectors are safe at both Ground and Air layers;
- the Monument begins with Ray-Fed Resistance 12 instead of 16.

## Interfere — Intentional Incursions

The Shattered deliberately seed additional mnemonic exposure around the Wound.

Three **Processional Graspers** begin the fight on the battlefield.

They are not separate Source puzzles.

They are satellite manifestations created by deliberately exposing compatible Fractured Objects to the Class V Wound.

The Book remains the Source.

Their entire purpose is to drag Darkwing into bad geometry.

## Aspis Interaction

## Assist — Perrik's Forecast

If Perrik remains in the Colony, trusts Darkwing enough to help, **and has access to the Claims Registry**:

> **a player chooses every d8 Eightfold Pattern result instead of rolling it**

If Perrik remains and helps **without** functional Registry access:

> **a player chooses every other Pattern, beginning with the first**

Perrik's contribution is **relationship-led**, not a sixth front or a separately scored Asset tier.

Perrik is not controlling the Monument.

Player choice represents perfect prediction.

This remains useful even though the attack is visibly telegraphed, because Darkwing gets to determine **which Pattern will be the one telegraphed**.

That creates powerful interaction with destroyed rays.

Example:

If rays 2 and 6 are destroyed, selecting Pattern 2 makes:

- Ground 2 / 4 / 6 / 8
- Air 1 / 3 / 5 / 7

but sectors 2 and 6 are completely inert.

Grounded PCs therefore only need to avoid 4 and 8.

## Interfere / Absent

Perrik does not need to become an enemy.

He may simply have evacuated the Colony.

If he is gone:

> **no Forecast Pattern ability exists.**

## Damage Thresholds

The Monument begins at:

> **420 HP**

## 315 HP — The Facade Breaks

Later commemorative facing, inscriptions, and decorative masonry shear away.

The Party sees repeated rebuilding and alteration.

The Monument is not singularly ancient in the way its civic story implies.

No additional numerical effect.

## 210 HP — The Pattern Cracks

Deep seams split through the pedestal.

The eight-ray geometry is visibly a later construction imposed over older masonry.

Randomly select one currently intact ray emitter.

That ray is destroyed immediately.

This:

- makes its sector safe at both vertical layers;
- reduces Ray-Fed Resistance by 2.

## 105 HP — It Cannot Hold the Eclipse

One raised arm breaks away.

The stone sphere remains suspended by mnemonic force, but the iconic image is failing.

**Eclipsefall** shrinks from:

> **15-foot burst → 10-foot burst**

Damage remains 10d8.

## 0 HP — Phase Restoration

The Monument does **not** enter an ordinary repeat-the-phase Stabilization loop.

Reducing it to 0 HP has physically exposed enough truth that the current Wound reconstruction becomes untenable.

**Pattern**

> The processional system, ray geometry, and ritual architecture accumulated afterward.

**Context**

> Aletheia's original act did not occur here and was not this ceremony.

The Party does not need to recite those exact sentences.

Once the phase has made the distinction unavoidable:

> **Context + Pattern are Restored.**

No check.

No reset.

All surviving Graspers immediately Disperse.

## Phase I → Phase II Transition

The pedestal gives way.

The plaza ruptures.

The Monument, shattered ray pieces, stone eclipse, surrounding masonry, and Darkwing crash through the Ritual Chamber dome below.

The chamber is:

> **ruptured, not obliterated**

The fall creates the physical inventory for Phase II:

- Monument stone;
- broken eclipse imagery;
- dome frescoes;
- ritual furnishings;
- Keeper depictions;
- inscriptions;
- ceremonial fragments.

The Wound has been physically dismantled.

Its remains begin trying to build a person.

## Phase I Encounter Budget

For six level-12 PCs:

- **Monument — Creature 15:** 120 XP
- **Three Graspers — Creature 10:** 20 XP each / 60 XP total

With Shattered interference:

> **180 XP — Severe**

Without the Graspers, the raw creature budget is lower, but the Monument applies persistent battlefield-wide positional pressure and is only the first phase of the larger encounter.

Context and Pattern remain Restored. Preserve actual wounds, resources, destroyed emitters and faction commitments. Graspers Disperse when the Monument falls. The physical Book remains the same Source; transition into the exposed reconstruction rather than reset the encounter.

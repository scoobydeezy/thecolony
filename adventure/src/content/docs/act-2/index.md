## The Act in Brief

The Reading has changed what the Party can recognize, not settled what the Colony should do. Presence leads the stopped path toward the Manor; absence leads the successful path there. Five investigations expose useful institutional methods and the mistakes hidden inside them.

## Routes Through the Act

The routes are optional and independent: Name at Old Town, Form at Pilgrim’s Rest, Memory in the Vault, Choice in the tunnel, and Context in the Veiled House. About two substantial investigations is the pacing expectation; no completion count or full Keystone collection gates the endgame. The Clearing House remains a support resource throughout.

## Pressure and Convergence

Use faction operations as a soft clock: seals, recalls, relocation, preparation and independent NPC choices. The Confluence begins when investigation becomes implementation. The Observer’s complete reveal ends the act.

On the stopped path, the eclipse begins during the Confluence and Vara’s completed second attempt is rejected. On the successful path, the Observer’s question helps restore Relationship and the eclipse then begins without a new Reading. Act III opens with the eclipse already underway on both paths.

## What Changes by the End

Record recovered Identity, Keystone access, people and Assets preserved, faction stance, open personal promises and what the Party asked Vara to carry. No completed route makes its institution wholly right. Missing lessons remain locally available when needed later.

## Suggested Pacing

Allow each investigation its full procedure and aftermath. Session numbers are planning estimates. Communicate urgency through visible consequences rather than cutting off a third route by fiat.

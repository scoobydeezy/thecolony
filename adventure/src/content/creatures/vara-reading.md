Vara is not an enemy and does not make hostile Strikes during this encounter.

### Read a Name-Page ◆
**AUDITORY, CONCENTRATE, LINGUISTIC, MANIPULATE**

**Requirements:** Vara is in the Keeper's ritual position, can see the current page, can speak, has access to the Book, and still chooses to continue the Reading.

Vara reads one ritual page and turns it. Advance the physical Book by **one page**.

This action is subject to all normal restrictions of its traits. In particular:

- **grabbed** can interfere with the Manipulate action through the normal flat check;
- **restrained** prevents it;
- effects that prevent speech or reading prevent it;
- stunned or slowed reduce the number of actions Vara can spend Reading.

Vara normally uses Read a Name-Page up to three times on her turn.

### Keeper's Ward
Once Vara reads the first page, the Reading fixes her to the Keeper's ritual position for as long as she willingly continues.

While the Ward is active:

- Vara cannot be forcibly moved by Shove, Reposition, forced movement, carrying, teleportation, or similar effects;
- another creature cannot transport Vara unless she willingly accepts the movement;
- Vara may voluntarily leave the ritual position using her own movement;
- voluntary departure immediately ends the Reading and the Ward;
- the Ward ends immediately if Vara chooses to stop or becomes unconscious.

Keeper's Ward grants **no bonus to AC or saves** and does not prevent Grapple, Restrained, silence, blindness, stunned, or other effects that can interrupt the Reading.

### My Choice
Vara cannot be Coerced into beginning or ending the Reading during this encounter. Her decision is changed only through the **Appeal to Vara** procedure or through her own response to events.

### Vara's Turn
While she intends to continue, Vara's default priority is to spend as many actions as possible using **Read a Name-Page**. She may instead use actions to Escape, Step, speak, or interact with the Book when the current battlefield state requires it.

If Resolve reaches 0, she immediately stops and will not resume this Reading.

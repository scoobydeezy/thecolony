The Colony is a prosperous independent city-state whose people have learned to live around absences they cannot explain. Meals are cooked, debts settled, streets repaired, and children taught. The institutions that make that continuity possible also conceal its cost.

Use this part to give the players a place worth caring about before asking them to change it. Its descriptions are for the GM: a citizen's explanation of a custom, an institution's public purpose, and the truth behind it are not always the same.

## A Place That Responds

Begin with work, relationships, and things people use. Let a familiar face introduce an institution; let an institution's response reveal its priorities. A repaired bridge, an inherited tool, or an extra portion of supper can establish the Colony more effectively than a speech about its history.

The directories show the campaign's starting state. Injuries, changed loyalties, damaged holdings, and new obligations established in play belong to the continuing Colony. Use those changes when revisiting a place.

For the underlying secret, read [GM Truths](/docs/running/gm-truths/). For how to keep the city moving between sessions, read [The Colony Turn](/docs/running/colony-turn/).

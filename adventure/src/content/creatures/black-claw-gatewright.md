## Operate the Gate

A gatewright uses ordinary movement and one Interact action at the lever to release the counterweight. Its profession grants no remote trigger, extra turn, or automatic repair. A physically severed cable requires replacement and time; a jam can be cleared with two actions and a DC 28 Crafting check. On failure the jam remains.

## Objective

On the first Vemerak falling to half HP, move toward the second lever on the next normal turn. Release it if possible, then retreat. If isolated or offered a viable escape, surrender or bargain.

The same machine is used whether EGRESS assists or interferes.
**Ref** +19  
**Will** +20
## Clamp Arm
**Melee** +22
**Damage:** 2d8+9 bludgeoning
**Improved Grab**
## Protective Intercept ↻
**Trigger:** a protected creature within 15 feet would take damage.
The Frame Steps up to 5 feet toward the protected creature if necessary.
Reduce the triggering damage by:
> **10**
The Frame takes:
> **10 damage**
This damage cannot itself be reduced by Protective Intercept.

### If EGRESS assists

A protected creature may be any Darkwing PC.

The Frame prioritizes:

1. preventing a PC from being dropped;
2. helping rescue Dawn operatives;
3. obstructing remembered threats where fiction permits;
4. keeping pace.

### If EGRESS interferes

The protected creature is:

> **the Echo**

The Frames prioritize:

1. Protective Intercept against attacks targeting the Echo;
2. Grappling / Repositioning PCs away from the Echo;
3. body-blocking pursuit;
4. preserving the Memory long enough for Engram acquisition.

They do **not** heal the Echo.

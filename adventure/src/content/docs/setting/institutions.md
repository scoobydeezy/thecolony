Institutions are where the Colony's philosophies become daily work. Introduce them through a practical need: resolving a dispute, moving goods, caring for someone, tracing an object, or making a damaged street usable again.

## Government and Protection

The :ref[Hall of the Keepers]{#asset.hall-of-the-keepers} is the Order's administrative center. :ref[Serevan Ilvar]{#npc.serevan-ilvar} represents its authority, while :ref[Kelda Varruk]{#npc.kelda-varruk} and the :ref[Order Watch Garrison]{#asset.order-watch-garrison} make protection and enforcement visible. The :ref[Bell of the Sun]{#asset.bell-of-the-sun} gives civic decisions a public voice.

The Order's ability to protect people should be tangible. So should the point at which its protection becomes control. Its response to an Incursion belongs to the encounter and :ref[Containment]{#rule.containment}, not a blanket assumption that every guard knows every secret.

## Representation and Public Works

The :ref[Assembly Hall]{#asset.assembly-hall} hosts debate, hearings, and reform. The Order retains ultimate authority, but Dawn's role in civic life is real. :ref[Julian Arrett]{#npc.julian-arrett} and :ref[Miriel Stoutfoot]{#npc.miriel-stoutfoot} give the Party people to approach when a private problem needs public action.

The :ref[Civic Improvement Fund]{#asset.civic-improvement-fund} finances improvements, the :ref[Public Works Yard]{#asset.public-works-yard} supplies practical means, and the :ref[Dawn Charter]{#asset.dawn-charter} preserves proposals and amendments. Show a repair or service people depend on before reducing the Assembly to its failure to end the Reading.

## Commerce and Continuing Obligations

The :ref[River Exchange]{#asset.river-exchange} and :ref[Docks]{#asset.docks} connect the Colony to outside trade. The :ref[Clearing House]{#location.clearing-house} reconciles contracts, property, and debts. Its :ref[Claims Registry]{#asset.claims-registry} preserves evidence of obligations even when their human participants cannot be remembered.

:ref[Perrik Tallow]{#npc.perrik-tallow} can make this work legible to the Party. A record of a repair, payment, boundary, or dependency can reveal a missing life without functioning as a complete biography. Access is a relationship and an institutional decision, not a reward that grants ownership of every record.

## Care and Remembrance

At the :ref[House of Abiding]{#location.house-of-abiding}, :ref[Uldren Harthwright]{#npc.uldren-harthwright} cares for the living Keeper. At the :ref[Hall of Remembrance]{#location.hall-of-remembrance}, :ref[Celia Harthwright]{#npc.celia-harthwright} preserves objects and their relationships after a person is forgotten. Together, their work spans the institutional division between preparing a life for the Reading and caring for what remains.

The :ref[Ledger]{#asset.ledger} records material evidence; the :ref[Empty Lectern]{#asset.empty-lectern} belongs to the public ceremony in which selected passages are read. Distinguish the Hall's preservation work, the record itself, and its ceremonial use. The two linked assets retain their individual starting records.

## Research and Hospitality

The :ref[EGRESS Institute]{#location.egress-institute} brings laboratories, archives, scholars, and technicians together. The :ref[Engram Vault]{#asset.engram-vault} preserves experienced Memory; :ref[Occlusion Containment]{#location.occlusion-containment} studies and contains dangerous manifestations. A preserved recollection supplies evidence, not a complete person or an automatic solution.

:ref[Pilgrim's Rest]{#location.pilgrim-s-rest}, hosted by :ref[Maelyn]{#npc.maelyn}, offers shelter and hospitality. The Cult's reception of people and experiences others reject is valuable in its own right, even when its explanation of those experiences is wrong.

## Using Institutional Access

Give a request a human recipient and a concrete object: permission to inspect a record, transport for a dangerous Source, space for a meeting, or help protecting a person. Establish what the institution can actually provide and what its representative needs in return. Record agreements and transfers in the continuing campaign state; the starting directory does not grant permanent access.

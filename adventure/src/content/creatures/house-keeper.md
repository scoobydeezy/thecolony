**Keeper's Staff** ◆ **+21**, **Damage** 2d8+9 bludgeoning plus 1d6 mental, nonlethal

### Custodial, Not Hostile
The House Keeper does not use ordinary Strikes against creatures merely because they are present.

It begins each manifestation attempting to control the site rather than injure intruders:

- shut and secure thresholds;
- classify movable objects as evidence;
- confiscate evidence;
- prevent further handling.

The House Keeper may use **Keeper's Staff** offensively against a creature that has attacked or damaged it, deliberately interfered with its custody of evidence, or otherwise escalated the confrontation.

It never attacks a dying or unconscious creature and does not pursue fleeing creatures merely to deal damage.

Its coercion is procedural rather than murderous.

### Secure the Evidence ◆◆
The House Keeper Strides up to its Speed.

At any point during or immediately after that movement, it chooses one movable object of **2 Bulk or less** within reach. The House Keeper considers essentially any movable possession, tool, weapon, document, potion, Context object, or household item to be potential evidence.

It cannot use this action to remove armor currently being worn or an object that cannot reasonably be seized without a longer procedure.

If the object is unattended, the House Keeper picks it up or moves it into an adjacent space it designates as secured evidence.

If another creature is holding the object, the House Keeper attempts **Athletics +20 against that creature's Reflex DC**.

- **Critical Success:** The House Keeper takes the object if it has a free hand; otherwise it places the object in an adjacent space of its choice.
- **Success:** The creature releases the object and the House Keeper places it in an adjacent space of its choice.
- **Failure:** The creature retains the object.
- **Critical Failure:** The creature retains the object and the House Keeper is **off-guard** until the start of its next turn.

The House Keeper generally gathers secured evidence on the Hearth table or lines it neatly against a wall rather than carrying everything indefinitely.

### Leave It Alone ↻
**Trigger:** A creature within the House Keeper's reach uses an **Interact** action to pick up, move, activate, open, close, or otherwise manipulate an object, fixture, door, hatch, or piece of secured evidence.

The House Keeper makes a **Keeper's Staff** Strike against the triggering creature.

On a **critical hit**, the triggering Interact is disrupted if that action can be disrupted.

### Shut the Doors ◆◆
**Requirement:** The House Keeper is currently inside a Hearth Room.

All six thresholds of that Hearth—North, East, South, West, Up, and Down—slam shut simultaneously and become **barred** until the start of the House Keeper's next turn.

A creature can open a barred threshold early by succeeding at a **DC 28 Athletics check to Force Open**.

A creature currently crossing one of those thresholds when the doors slam attempts a **DC 28 Reflex save**:

- **Critical Success:** The creature chooses which side of the threshold it ends on.
- **Success:** The creature completes the crossing and ends on the destination side.
- **Failure:** The creature is forced back to the side it came from and falls **prone**.
- **Critical Failure:** As failure, and the creature takes **3d6 bludgeoning damage**.

Shut the Doors never creates, destroys, rerolls, or changes an established maze connection.

An unresolved threshold remains unresolved after it reopens.

### Tactical Priority
The House Keeper should feel like a furious custodian rather than a conventional predator.

A useful priority is:

1. **Shut the Doors** when doing so isolates intruders or divides people already spread through the maze.
2. **Secure the Evidence** whenever a desirable movable object is available.
3. Use **Keeper's Staff** proactively only after a creature has escalated the confrontation.
4. Remain near the Hearth and the gathered evidence rather than chasing isolated PCs through the maze.

The encounter cost of a wrong answer is lost time, separation, confiscated gear, and a short Veiled fight—not another boss-length damage race.

### Dispersal
At 0 HP, the House Keeper Disperses normally.

Because the slate immediately rejects the failed Context assertion when the Incursion manifests, that rejected arrangement is no longer a valid Reconstruction Condition after Dispersal.

The Ward disappears normally.

The slate clears its **STOP** state and can later accept another Set the Hearth attempt.

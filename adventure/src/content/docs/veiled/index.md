This part gathers everything about the campaign's supernatural danger in one place. Read [A Practical Primer](/docs/veiled/primer/) for what happens at the table when an Incursion begins, and [The Seven Components of Identity](/docs/veiled/seven-components/) for what each investigation teaches. The rule entries below own the reusable procedures; the Veiled Sources are the encounters' Source entries, the vessels carrying unresolved mnemonic Weight.

Individual Veiled and their stat blocks stay in the [Bestiary](/docs/bestiary/), and each encounter supplies its own evidence, opposition and stakes.

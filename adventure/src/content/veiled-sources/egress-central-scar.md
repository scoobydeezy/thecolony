*Environmental Source, **not** a creature, Wound, or independently Dispersible Incursion*

**Origin:** An old EGRESS Source Rupture whose individual origin/biography has not been recovered. Uncontrolled Spill settled into the central floor. **Source Level :stat{#source.egress-central-scar key=source-level}, :stat{#source.egress-central-scar key=class}; Source DC :stat{#source.egress-central-scar key=source-dc}** for risky deliberate interface work. There is **no ordinary active Incursion Ward or Ward HP** to attack while it remains raw Scar. The original Weight is neither destroyed nor automatically growing whenever another Incursion approaches it.

### Regions and Geometry
- **Influence:** Within roughly **30 feet** of the jagged central rupture; foreign impressions and visual distortion clearly warn of danger, but no automatic damage.
- **Boundary:** Within roughly **15 feet** of the fault; raw Weight resists coherent Incursion bodies. The EGRESS conduit ring and field interfaces run **outside** this line. An Incursion attempting to enter the Boundary makes **:stat{#source.egress-central-scar key=source-dc} Fortitude** at the moment of entry: critical success crosses normally; success is halted at the edge; failure is pushed 10 feet outward; critical failure is **Dispersed** (not Stabilized). This check occurs at most once per creature per turn. Its Source stays where it is.
- **Horizon:** The innermost unmistakable open black fissure/region, approximately **5 feet** from its visible lip. It has heavily marked rails, warning instruments, and a visible interval before full crossing. A person fully crossing **is incorporated into raw Weight**, not merely damaged or teleported. A Source vessel crossing may actually combine Weight with the Scar under the Veiled rules. A manifested body alone crossing is normally Dispersed; the underlying Source remains elsewhere.

**Counterplay:** Traversing the Influence is possible. Repositioning or Shoving toward the Horizon first confronts ordinary movement, an unmistakable physical rail/lip, and an opportunity for an adjacent ally to intervene before a second commitment crosses the actual Horizon. **Never cause Horizon incorporation from an unseen single failed save or an untelegraphed Shove.** Once someone fully crosses, no ordinary Escape/reach-in rescue exists. Effects of a genuine Source joining the Scar must record conserved Weight/provenance rather than erasing it.

### Boundary Field Regulation
A powered brood circuit can **carry the Scar's repulsive Boundary interference** to signature-specific containment barriers in the perimeter cells. This is EGRESS engineering, not a universal Scar polarity switch or permanent solution for the Scar. The field **does not transfer or absorb** the prisoners' Sources. Optional archived `-OCC` signatures can key a controlled attractive interface for capture if the Party invents and safely operates that strategy; no such keying is required to restore ordinary cell containment during this scene.

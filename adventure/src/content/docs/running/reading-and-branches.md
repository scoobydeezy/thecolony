:ref[The Reading]{#branch.the-reading} is the campaign's required persistent branch. It changes what the Party has done to Vara and what the living inherit from that decision. It does not divide the rest of the Colony into unrelated alternate adventures.

## The Choice at the End of Act I

The Reading works. Vara may sincerely choose to continue after encountering the lives the Book carries. Let the Party understand both the sacrifice and her agency before it acts. :ref[The Reading encounter]{#encounter.the-reading} owns the ritual's timing, opposition, and interruption procedures.

:::branch{when=path.the-reading-is-stopped}
Vara survives. The eclipse recoils, and no immediate supernatural catastrophe follows. Political certainty collapses first. The factions begin acting on possibilities previously kept theoretical, unresolved Weight persists, and the living Keeper becomes someone institutions want to protect, study, recruit, or control.

Keep her intentions present. Saving Vara grants neither the Party nor a faction authority over her future.
:::

:::branch{when=path.the-reading-succeeds}
The Reading completes, the eclipse passes, and Vara is Forgotten. The Party forgets her too. Its notes, possessions, promises, habits, and feelings preserve consequences of a relationship it can no longer consciously identify.

As the Party reconstructs her, it becomes another distributed mnemonic anchor for someone already carried within the Book. The question is no longer simply whether to stop a sacrifice: remembering can reopen one that was knowingly completed.
:::

## The Same City, Different Responsibilities

Both paths enter Act II's investigations. The :ref[Manor]{#event.the-manor} opens opportunities within the same continuing world. The Party may visit a subset, in an order shaped by its choices and faction activity. An unvisited route is not a separate timeline, and the factions do not automatically advance merely because their route was skipped.

Carry the branch into conversations, risks, and evidence. On the stopped path, record Vara's experiences and intentions. On the succeeded path, record the particular residues the Party has encountered and what it has reconstructed. Do not play the successful Reading as though the Party retains an intact biography of Vara.

## Convergence and the Observer

The Observer closes Act II on both paths, with the order of revelation and eclipse changed by the Reading:

| Path | Transition |
|---|---|
| Reading stopped | The eclipse begins at :ref[The Confluence]{#event.the-confluence}; :ref[The Observer]{#event.the-observer} then delivers the reveal. |
| Reading succeeds | The Observer reveals himself, then the eclipse begins. |

:ref[Eclipse]{#event.eclipse} opens Act III with the eclipse already under way. :ref[Totality]{#event.totality} is the mechanical convergence: both paths have reopened an inheritance the old closed chain can no longer contain. The emotional consequences remain different.

## Reading Branches in This Book

Path-labelled blocks apply only to their stated Reading outcome. Shared prose applies to both unless the scene says otherwise. A route being available, a Source being contained, or an NPC being absent is a local condition, not an additional campaign-wide Reading path.

Preserve actual outcomes beyond the two labels. A death, departure, damaged Asset, or changed relationship still matters. Reassigning a narrative function does not resurrect its former bearer or automatically transfer their faction goal.

## One Shared Ending Question

Neither path is the designated right answer. Both eventually ask where the Weight should rest, on what terms, and with whose consent. Optional Identity lessons make the Party better prepared; required evidence must remain recoverable locally in the finale.

The final Reading in :ref[The Light Remains]{#event.the-light-remains} enacts the answer the table has already built. It is not a surprise new test that can invalidate that answer.

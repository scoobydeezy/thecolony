**Capture Strength** `ceil(P / 2)`; normally **3** for six PCs
**Unformed Hand** +30 to hit, reach 10 feet, **3d10+14 bludgeoning damage**

### Form Capacity

The Shape can embody at most **three active Forms** at once.

Each active Form grants **both Claims** printed for that artwork. One of those two Claims is marked **Resonant** according to the player's ritual interpretation.

Represent each active Form with its Totem or a matching token beside the Shape. Mark the Resonant Claim on the GM reference card.

Three active Forms therefore normally give the Shape:

* **six available Claim abilities**;
* **three Resonant Claims**;
* its normal three actions and one reaction with which to use them.

When the Shape Assumes a fourth Form, remove its **oldest active Form** and both Claims granted by it. The corresponding Form Station remains Available unless its Totem has been Taken or Released.

### Assume Form ◇

**Trigger:** The Shape gains control of an Available Form Station whose Form it does not currently possess.

The Shape immediately Assumes that Station's Form as a **free action**.

* Add that Form's Totem/token to the Shape.
* The Shape gains **both Claims** associated with that Form.
* If three Forms are already active, remove the oldest active Form first.
* If the Station's Totem is later Taken or Released, remove that Form and both Claims immediately.
* Resolve **Resonant Expression** for the newly Assumed Form.

### Resonant Expression

One Claim on each selected Form is marked **Resonant** by the participant's ritual interpretation.

When the Shape Assumes that Form:

* if the Resonant Claim has an action cost, the Shape may **immediately use that Claim once as a free action**;
* if the Resonant Claim is passive or reactive, its listed Resonant effect begins immediately and any explicit on-capture effect resolves immediately;
* while the Form remains active, the Claim continues to use its **Resonant rider** rather than its base version.

The immediate free use represents the projection entering reality at maximum clarity. It is not quickened and creates no duration token to track.

### Reject the Claim ◆◆

The Shape Strides up to its Speed. At any point during this movement, it can make one **Unformed Hand** Strike against a creature carrying a Form Totem.

On a hit, the Shape can choose to deal no damage and instead force the creature to **drop the carried Totem** in its space.

### Reclaim Projection ◆

**Requirement:** The Shape is adjacent to a dropped Form Totem.

The Totem immediately returns to its original Form Station. That Station becomes Available again.

If the Shape already controls that Station when the Totem returns, it immediately **Assumes that Form** and resolves Resonant Expression normally.

### Projected Reconstruction

If the Shape reaches 0 HP before three Forms have been Released, it **Disperses** rather than ending the encounter.

When it Disperses:

* remove the Shape from the battlefield;
* remove all active Form tokens from it;
* all Form Stations, Released Forms, carried Totems, and dropped Totems remain in their current state;
* the Shape reconstructs at the **end of the next round** at the Face with full HP and no active Forms.

Dispersal therefore creates a full-round objective window but does not resolve the Source.

The Cult's gallery contains twelve prepared artistic representations. Each card isolates one visually dominant **Representation** and supplies two possible mechanical expressions of that same image.

The participant's interpretation during the ritual marks **one** of those two Claims as Resonant. When the Shape later Assumes that Form, however, **both Claims become available**. Resonance determines which one gains its rider and fires immediately on capture.

## 1. The Antlered Wayfarer

**Medium:** polychrome wood sculpture.  
**Representation:** **Antlers**.  
**Gallery image:** an elk-headed traveler with branching antlers, road ribbons, bow, mud-worn boots, and an outstretched guiding hand; strongly influenced by Erastilian frontier imagery.

### Assault — Goring Charge ◆◆

Stride up to Speed, then make an **Unformed Hand** Strike as a horn Strike. If the Shape moved at least 20 feet toward the target, add **one weapon damage die** and push the target 5 feet on a hit.

**Resonant:** Push the target **10 feet** instead; on a critical hit, also knock it prone.

### Mobility — Trailbreaker ◆

Stride up to Speed, ignoring difficult terrain and moving through occupied spaces. The Shape must end in a legal space.

**Resonant:** Stride **twice** instead of once.

## 2. The Many-Handed Saint

**Medium:** glazed ceramic relief assembled from differently colored clay.  
**Representation:** **Many Arms**.  
**Gallery image:** a serene six-armed figure whose hands paint, feed, bind a wound, offer a flower, play music, and reach outward; influenced by Shelynite artistic traditions.

### Assault — Hands Everywhere ◆◆

Make three **Unformed Hand** Strikes against up to three different creatures. These attacks count toward MAP, but MAP does not increase until all three attacks are resolved.

**Resonant:** One creature may be targeted by **two** of the three Strikes.

### Sustain — Tend the Shape ◆

The Shape regains **16 HP** and reduces one persistent-damage value or its frightened or sickened condition by 1.

**Resonant:** Regain **32 HP** instead.

## 3. The Mother at the Threshold

**Medium:** layered household tapestry repaired and expanded by many hands.  
**Representation:** **Doorway**.  
**Gallery image:** a fully rendered older woman standing in a brightly woven doorway between a warm household and a dark storm, surrounded by domestic details.

### Guard — Safe Threshold

While the Shape occupies any Form Station, it gains a **+2 circumstance bonus to AC**.

**Resonant:** The +2 circumstance bonus also applies to saves and DCs against **forced movement** while the Shape occupies a Form Station.

### Mobility — Through the Threshold ◆

Teleport from the Shape's current space to an unoccupied space inside any **Available unreleased Form Station within 60 feet**.

**Resonant:** Increase the range to **120 feet**, and this teleport does not trigger reactions.

---

## 4. The Three-Eyed Hound

**Medium:** charcoal and red-chalk mural preserved on a removed plaster panel.  
**Representation:** **Three Eyes**.  
**Gallery image:** a huge three-eyed jackal or hyena beside a tiny human companion, using culturally familiar Lamashtan monster imagery without necessarily being devotional art.

### Assault — Threefold Gaze ◆◆

Make three ranged gaze Strikes, **+30**, range 60 feet, against up to three creatures. Each hit deals **3d8+12 mental damage**. These attacks count toward MAP, but MAP does not increase until all three are resolved.

**Resonant:** One creature may be targeted by **two** of the three gaze Strikes.

### Influence — Seen

The Shape ignores the **concealed** and **hidden** conditions against creatures it can sense. A creature carrying a Form Totem is also **off-guard** to the Shape.

**Resonant:** Totem carriers cannot benefit from **cover** against the Shape, and the Shape always knows their direction and distance while they remain on the encounter map.

## 5. The Star-Winged Pilgrim

**Medium:** painted silk devotional hanging.  
**Representation:** **Wings**.  
**Gallery image:** a joyful traveler with huge butterfly-like wings painted with constellations, influenced by Desnan and Varisian visual traditions.

### Mobility — Take Flight

The Shape gains a **Fly Speed equal to its land Speed**.

**Resonant:** Increase that Fly Speed by **10 feet**. When this Form is Assumed, the Shape immediately Flies up to **half its Fly Speed** as part of Resonant Expression.

### Guard — Sheltering Wings ↻

**Trigger:** The Shape takes damage.  
Reduce the triggering damage by **16**.

**Resonant:** Reduce the triggering damage by **32** instead.

## 6. The Crowned Magistrate

**Medium:** precise black-ink woodcut on cream paper.  
**Representation:** **Chains**.  
**Gallery image:** a richly dressed horned Chelish magistrate surrounded by chains, seals, keys, and contractual script; authority rendered through familiar infernal civic aesthetics.

### Control — Bind ◆

One creature within 30 feet attempts a **DC 37 Reflex save**. On a failure, it is **immobilized** by manifested chains until it Escapes against DC 37.

**Resonant:** On a failure, the creature is **restrained** instead of immobilized.

### Influence — Weight of Authority

While the Shape occupies a Form Station, reduce Darkwing's total Capture Strength in that Station by **1**, to a minimum total of 1.

**Resonant:** Reduce Darkwing's total Capture Strength by **2** instead, still to a minimum total of 1.

## 7. The Lantern Beyond the Rain

**Medium:** hanging totem made from bone, black wood, feathers, colored glass, and a tiny working lantern.  
**Representation:** **Lantern**.  
**Gallery image:** a four-winged ravenlike psychopomp wearing an elaborate funerary mask and carrying a lantern; strongly influenced by Pharasmin and psychopomp imagery.

### Influence — Follow the Light ◆◆

One creature within 60 feet attempts a **DC 37 Will save**. On a failure, it must immediately Stride once toward a Form Station chosen by the Shape. This movement is forced movement.

**Resonant:** On a failure, the creature must **Stride twice** toward that Station; on a success, it must **Step once** toward it if able.

### Sustain — Guiding Light

Whenever the Shape gains control of a Form Station, it gains **16 temporary HP** until the start of its next turn.

**Resonant:** Gain **32 temporary HP** instead.

## 8. The Patterned Stranger

**Medium:** portable tile mosaic composed from interlocking symbolic geometric shapes.  
**Representation:** **Geometric Pattern**.  
**Gallery image:** a complete humanoid portrait built from precise tessellated patterns influenced by Varisian Harrow geometry rather than literal anatomy.

### Control — Snap to Pattern ◆◆

Up to three creatures within 30 feet attempt a **DC 37 Reflex save**. Reposition each creature that fails up to **10 feet** to an unoccupied space chosen by the Shape.

**Resonant:** Reposition each failed creature up to **15 feet** instead.

### Influence — Reconfigure ◆

**Frequency:** once per round.  
Exchange one active Form for the Form of an unreleased Form Station the Shape currently controls, without moving to that Station. The new Form is Assumed normally and resolves Resonant Expression.

**Resonant:** Exchange **up to two active Forms** instead of one.

## 9. The Sleeper Under Stone

**Medium:** heavy basalt sculpture with bronze-filled cracks.  
**Representation:** **Stone**.  
**Gallery image:** a dwarven figure half-emerging from a mountain, beard becoming strata, rune-marked hammer clasped to the chest; influenced by Torag and Five Kings traditions.

### Guard — Living Rock

The Shape gains **resistance 5 to physical damage** and a **+2 circumstance bonus to checks and DCs against forced movement**.

**Resonant:** Increase the physical resistance to **10**.

### Sustain — Rest in Stone

If the Shape used no move actions during its turn, it regains **32 HP** at the end of that turn.

**Resonant:** Regain **48 HP** instead.

## 10. The River That Learned to Walk

**Medium:** long horizontal dyed-fabric scroll.  
**Representation:** **Water**.  
**Gallery image:** a river creature transitioning from fishlike tail through scaled legs into a weathered riverwoman, influenced by local River Foam and Gozren nature imagery.

### Control — Current ◆◆

Creatures in a **30-foot line** attempt a **DC 37 Reflex save**. On a failure, a creature is pushed **10 feet**. The affected line becomes difficult terrain until the start of the Shape's next turn.

**Resonant:** Failed creatures are pushed **20 feet** instead.

### Sustain — Flow Back Together

The first time each round the Shape moves at least **20 feet** during its turn, it regains **16 HP**.

**Resonant:** Regain **32 HP** instead.

## 11. The Chainbreaker

**Medium:** hammered copper relief.  
**Representation:** **Broken Chains**.  
**Gallery image:** an exhausted woman standing on broken manacles with an eagle spreading its wings behind her; influenced by Andoren liberation imagery and the Eagle Knights.

### Assault — Shatter Bonds ◆◆

Make an **Unformed Hand** Strike. On a hit, add **one weapon damage die** and ignore the target's physical resistance for that Strike.

**Resonant:** Add **two weapon damage dice** instead of one.

### Mobility — Unbound

The Shape ignores difficult terrain and the **immobilized** condition. At the start of its turn, it automatically ends **grabbed** or **restrained** on itself.

**Resonant:** The Shape's movement does **not trigger reactions**.

## 12. The Child's Sky-Friend

**Medium:** wax crayon, charcoal, and cheap blue chalk on rough paper, carefully framed by the Cult with the same respect as masterworks.  
**Representation:** **Giant Hand**.  
**Gallery image:** a child's enormous smiling friend with purple hair, yellow boots, too many fingers, stars overhead, and the child holding its gigantic hand.

### Control — Hold Hands ◆

Make an **Unformed Hand** Strike. On a hit, the target is **grabbed**. While it has that creature grabbed, the Shape can move normally and carry the target with it; the target moves with the Shape and must remain in a legal adjacent space when possible.

**Resonant:** A carried creature is **restrained** rather than grabbed for as long as the Shape continues carrying it.

### Guard — Protective Palm ↻

**Trigger:** The Shape is targeted by an attack.  
The Shape gains a **+2 circumstance bonus to AC** against the triggering attack. If the attack still hits, reduce its damage by **8**.

**Resonant:** The AC bonus becomes **+3** and the damage reduction becomes **16**.

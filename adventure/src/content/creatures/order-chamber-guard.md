**Polearm** +21, reach 10 feet, **Damage** 2d10+9 bludgeoning, nonlethal

### Hold Formation
While adjacent to another Order ally, the guard gains a **+1 circumstance bonus to AC**.

### Bar the Way ↻
**Trigger:** A creature within the guard's reach uses a move action that would bring it closer to Vara or the Book.

The guard makes a Polearm Strike against the triggering creature. On a critical hit, the triggering movement is disrupted.

### Drive Back ◆◆
The guard makes a Polearm Strike. On a hit, the target is pushed **5 feet away from Vara or the Book** without a separate Athletics check.

### Tactical Priority
One guard should normally protect each secondary approach through the pews / side aisles. They maintain lanes and consume Darkwing actions rather than leaving formation to pursue damage.

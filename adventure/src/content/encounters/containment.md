## At the Table

**Objective:** free Gressa from the Weight and distinguish classification from truthful Restoration. **Trigger:** Bad Maps transfers the toy’s Weight into living Gressa. **Track:** the Nurse’s four lower hands, the Bed’s one patient and separate reaction, and whether Pattern is classified or Restored. **Finish:** route the same Weight into a compatible cell; determine whether it is unresolved or Stabilized, Unanchored.

## Reference at the Table

Use :ref[Caretaker]{#creature.evin-caretaker}, :ref[the Bed]{#creature.caretaker-bed}, and :ref[the transferred Weight]{#source.evin-cloth-toy}. :ref[Tuned Suppression]{#rule.tuned-suppression} explains why the known Baby profile stays quiet while the Nurse emerges; :ref[Controlled Routing]{#rule.controlled-routing} owns the transfer requirements.

## Objective

Keep Gressa safe, learn whose recurring responsibilities the Nurse embodies, and decide whether to accept containment alone or pursue Rest. A successful institution procedure is not proof that the institution understands the person.

### Narrative Container
This route exists only if the cloth toy ruptures during *Bad Maps* and its full Class II Weight transfers into Gressa. The Order can Disperse the active Giant Baby and hold a perimeter, but a living Source cannot be treated like an object in a standard containment vessel. EGRESS responds with a **tuned suppression lattice** calibrated to the observed Echo / Relationship reconstruction. It suppresses Giant Baby during transport without resolving the Weight.

Living Sources are rare. EGRESS can measure Gressa's Class and suppress the *known* Incursion, but the Director requires enough classified Resonance to engineer a compatible receiver before routing the Weight out of her. Ordinary treatment becomes the dangerous experiment.

### Encounter Signature — The Nightmare Nurse
The first new manifestation does **not** look like Evin or a conventional adult humanoid. It is a twelve-foot, six-armed, absolutely black nurse silhouette with an exaggerated cap, apron, armfuls of medicines and instruments, and a gigantic folding hospital bed held upright like a shield in its upper hands. This is reality's sincere false conclusion about the Pattern **someone cared for Gressa**—not evidence that Evin was a nurse.

The **Bed** is an independently targetable allied satellite creature with its **own HP and reaction**, carried as a shield whether empty or occupied. It can secure **one Large or smaller patient**. The Nurse maintains several separate hand-held patients, sedates them, and shoves unclaimed visitors away. Nurse and Bed are **one Echo / Pattern Incursion**, supported by Gressa's **one living Source**; neither the Bed nor its captive patients are additional Sources or separate Identity puzzles.

The tableau should be immediately legible: a giant nurse holding a patient-filled bed as a shield, two or more adventurers dangling from spare hands, a syringe prepared for whoever resists, and an explosive *SHHH!* that clears the surrounding ward.

### Opening State
- Gressa is under observation in a functioning EGRESS cell with Giant Baby's Relationship profile suppressed.
- The new Pattern-resonant Nurse has **not** yet been correctly classified; the existing suppression cannot block it.
- An orderly is preparing a scheduled dose for Gressa; the recorded medication administration happens before the orderly delivers it.
- The Nurse arrives **calm and nonhostile**, already completing care. It becomes dangerous when Gressa attempts to leave or others disrupt the routine.
- The Bed arrives with the Nurse and is treated as her cooperating but separately targetable satellite, not ordinary inert equipment.

### Encounter Flow
1. **The known answer works.** EGRESS's tuned lattice keeps Giant Baby suppressed after the toy's Rupture sends its Weight into Gressa.
2. **Medication without an orderly.** The Director notes that Gressa's scheduled dose is already recorded. The orderly objects that it is still in their hand. Everyone turns to discover a towering six-armed Nurse placing a medicine bottle away, straightening Gressa's blanket, and silently shushing the room.
3. **Pattern, not resemblance.** The new Incursion expresses recurring medical and domestic responsibilities. Its nurse anatomy is a false interpretation of *caretaker*, not a likeness of Evin. It knows genuine facts about medication, provisioning, appointments, and household routines.
4. **Care becomes coercion.** Staff attempts to examine or move Gressa; the Nurse refuses to let its patient be disturbed. It reaches for intervening PCs, takes multiple patients at once, sedates captured characters, and propels nonpatients away with **Visiting Hours Are Over!**. It protects Gressa but has lost the Context and Choice that would make care humane.
5. **The Bed changes positioning.** The Nurse carries the Bed upright as a shield even while occupied. A patient may be transferred from a hand to the Bed, becoming restrained while the Nurse frees that hand for another patient. The Bed can use its **own reaction** to intercept one hit against the Nurse, independently of the Nurse's Protective Intercession reaction protecting Gressa.
6. **Friendly fire is a visible risk.** Attacks **directly against the occupied Bed**, or attacks whose damage the Bed intercepts, trigger a Reflex save for its patient: critical success no damage, success one-quarter, failure one-half, critical failure full damage actually dealt to the Bed (details in the mechanical reference entries linked on this page). The patient can attempt ordinary Escape instead. Never spring this risk on the players without clearly showing the bed's occupant and its shield position.
7. **No extra source puzzle.** If the Bed is Dispersed, it releases the patient and removes the Nurse's shield/interception until reconstruction; it does **not** cause Source Rupture. If the Nurse Disperses, the Bed Disperses too. Restoring Pattern settles both, and neither has a separate Stabilization Window.
8. **EGRESS classifies.** Instrumentation identifies the new **Echo / Pattern** profile but cannot supply the missing life or justify automatic consent to routing. The Director prefers studying the new phenomenon while it remains observable.
9. **Claims Registry supplies Pattern evidence.** Darkwing, Perrik, and EGRESS trace recurring medication purchases, household provision, rent, appointment obligations, and other records toward a single missing contributor.
10. **A date-shaped hole.** The responsibilities stop together at a Reading. This supports classification without giving the absent adult's Name.
11. **Classification versus Restoration.** Once Class and Pattern topology are sufficiently known, controlled routing becomes possible. If Darkwing also establishes the truthful Pattern—that **Gressa's grown child had been caring for her**—the Nurse stops imposing unwanted care. She does **not** have to become anatomically human or resemble Evin; her hands and bed simply stop acting as instruments of coercion.
12. **Controlled routing and consent.** EGRESS prepares a compatible **Class II** receiving cell and routes the Weight out of Gressa without Rupture. The procedure is technically possible without consent, but Gressa's permission remains a moral and narrative decision. She likely wants it removed.
13. **The wing of successful failures.** The receiving cell reveals other Veiled still acting out their mistaken imperatives behind functioning boundaries. These can later be recognizable residents of **The Breach**, but do not automatically generate extra fight encounters now.
14. **Transfer completed.** A merely classified but unresolved Nurse and Bed resume their routine behind the containment boundary. A Pattern-Restored Source may remain **Stabilized, Unanchored**. EGRESS has freed Gressa of the Weight without necessarily letting it find Rest.

### Player Leverage
- Observe the nurse's real caregiving knowledge to identify recurring responsibilities rather than seek a facial resemblance.
- Escape hand-held Grapples normally; a successful Escape ends that particular hold despite the Nurse's free maintenance of other holds.
- Escape the Bed through ordinary PF2e Escape against its defined DC, or target/disperse the Bed at the explicitly telegraphed risk of injuring its patient.
- Exploit the Nurse's occupied hands and Bed capacity; freeing a patient changes which new targets can be held or transferred.
- Decide whether to expose staff or Gressa to danger in order to classify the Pattern, and whether to push beyond classification into truthful Restoration.
- Insist on informed consent before EGRESS routes Weight out of a living Source.

### Restoration / Truth Condition
**The missing obligations belonged to a grown child caring for Gressa, not a generic nurse performing procedures.** This truthful Pattern ends the dangerous false conclusion. The Nurse's absurd medical Form need not turn into Evin's true likeness. Recognition of Class and Resonance is enough for **Containment**, but not automatically for **Stabilization**.

### Resolution
Gressa leaves the Institute without the Class II Weight. Depending on Darkwing's investigation, EGRESS either has a living unresolved Caretaker Incursion in a cell or a coherent, stabilized but unanchored burden. The final reveal remains: **Containment is not Anchoring.**

### Systems Established / Extended
**Tuned signature-specific suppression, living Source, alternate Source resonance profiles, Pattern, Echo, independent non-Source satellite body, multiple Grapples, free maintenance, independent reactions, conditional transferred damage, Claims Registry, classification vs Restoration, consent, Class/Capacity-compatible routing, Containment vs Anchoring.**

### Provisional Tuning / Open Design
The Nurse/Bed paired combat values, sedative, repulsion, interception, and patient damage fractions are specified in the mechanical reference entries linked on this page and are intentionally aggressive. Playtest the linked pair as one encounter instead of assuming the Nurse's old solo XP is unchanged. The exact round-by-round controlled-routing checks and breach consequences remain open; the established **Class + Resonance + compatible destination** gate is unchanged.

## Routing at the Table

Once Class, Pattern topology, a compatible receiving cell, and safe working conditions are established, complete routing as a supervised procedure outside initiative. Do not add an unannounced sequence of failure rolls. If the Party insists on routing during active interference, first secure the patient and apparatus; resolving that immediate obstacle is the encounter objective. Gressa’s consent remains a separate decision.

## If the Party Does Not Intervene

EGRESS prioritizes protecting its patient and classifying the new signature. It can contain a classified profile without restoring its history; carry an unresolved Nurse and Bed forward to The Breach if routing succeeds in the fiction. Do not assume Gressa dies because the Party chose another investigation.

This is the GM's book for *The Colony*. It is written for the person running the campaign and is full of spoilers.

- **[How to Use This Book](/docs/running/how-to-use/)** introduces its markers and references; [The Campaign at a Glance](/docs/running/campaign-at-a-glance/) gives the premise and shape of the adventure.
- **[The Colony](/docs/setting/)** collects the setting's history, districts, institutions and people.
- **[Act I — The Secret](/docs/act-1/)** begins with [The Calling](/docs/act-1/the-calling/), followed by [Act II — The Cost](/docs/act-2/) and [Act III — The Reckoning](/docs/act-3/).
- **Reference** collects the [Bestiary](/docs/bestiary/), [NPCs](/docs/npcs/), [Locations](/docs/gazetteer/), [Rules Reference](/docs/rules/) and [Veiled Sources](/docs/veiled-sources/). Chapters assemble these entries where needed so each rule or stat block has one home.

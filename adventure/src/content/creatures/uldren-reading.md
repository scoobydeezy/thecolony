**Walking Stick** +20, **Damage** 2d8+8 bludgeoning, nonlethal

### Her Choice
Uldren's allegiance follows Vara's expressed decision.

- While Vara voluntarily continues the Reading, Uldren acts as an Order-side defender of her ability to continue.
- The instant Vara voluntarily stops, Uldren becomes allied with Vara and with creatures defending that decision.

No check or delay is required.

If Darkwing stops the Reading against Vara's expressed choice, Uldren does **not** automatically change sides.

### Steward's Intercession ↻
**Trigger:** A creature adjacent to Vara targets her with a hostile action or physically interferes with her.

Uldren Steps up to **10 feet** toward Vara. If he ends adjacent to Vara, he may immediately **Aid** Vara's AC, Escape check, or other directly relevant defense against the triggering interference.

### I Know What She Needs ◆
**Frequency:** once per creature per encounter.

One adjacent allied creature gains **10 temporary HP** and may immediately attempt a new save against one mental or emotion effect currently affecting it.

### Make Room ◆◆
Uldren makes a Walking Stick Strike. On a hit, he pushes the target **5 feet** away from Vara without a separate Athletics check.

Uldren never treats dealing damage as the point of the encounter.

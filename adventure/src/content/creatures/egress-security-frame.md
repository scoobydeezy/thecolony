**Containment Pin ◆◆:** Stride up to Speed, then attempt a Grapple (+22 Athletics) or Reposition (+22) against an adjacent Incursion; ordinary PF2e results.  
**Emergency Escort ◆◆:** Stride up to Speed while assisting one willing adjacent Medium-or-smaller person to move with it; the pair must follow a legal path. The Frame has no special immunity to the Scar or the Sheep's observation loss.
Use these as **one or two allied units when the Party directly shares a skirmish**, not as a whole offscreen tactical simulation. Frames can be ordered, obstructed, saved, destroyed, or redirected using the established facility situation. A Proxy is an Engram-loaded Frame and may follow a remembered task, but its Memory never turns it into the original subject or another Veiled Source.

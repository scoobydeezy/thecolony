## At the Table

**Objective:** respond to the containment emergency while preserving EGRESS’s capacity. **Run:** three campus spotlights, then Mother/brood plumbing and Sheep observation as the situation permits. **Track:** two return controls, circulation settling, barriers, suppression, observers, and actual escaped Sources. **No twelve-prisoner combat queue.**

### Narrative Container
Under Totality, the Institute's Occlusion Containment network fails while its classified, unresolved Sources remain in their engineered cells. This is not twelve new mysteries to solve: EGRESS has spent years recording Occlusion-derived Engrams (`-OCC`), comparing them with authentic Memory, and learning how to **confine** reconstructions it often cannot yet **Stabilize** or **Anchor**. The crisis reveals what that success has been resting upon.

**Encounter signature:** An enormous black dragon tears through Containment and abandons the Colony; the Party fights three escaped Incursions across a campus already defended by EGRESS Frames; a pair of argumentative Veiled guards blocks the hole; inside, a central Scar, an empty reservoir, a screaming aquatic Mother, and thousands of black fry in translucent conduits explain the failing machinery. Returning the fry to their Mother restores the network and makes Dispersal a viable recapture technique. It also interrupts Sheep Duty at exactly the wrong moment.

The **Mother and her brood** supply the surprise, **the Scar** supplies the terror, and **the Sheep** supplies a lethal consequence of getting the first two problems under control. The finale is an operational rescue and containment decision, not a demand to finish twelve independent Identity investigations.

### Opening State — Inherit Faction Goals
The Breach exists under Totality whether or not the Party visits. Before play, determine from the **actual Faction Goal / champion state** who is attempting evacuation, retention of classified Sources, retrieval of Engrams, emergency routing, seizure of infrastructure, or continued experimentation. Record their concrete methods, opposition, capabilities, deadlines, and unattended outcomes. The set pieces below are a **prepared crisis environment**, not a replacement for live Act III operations; visiting EGRESS is not compulsory.

At the Party's arrival:
- The **Dragon** has just smashed through a high containment wall and roof, leaving a colossal opening facing the EGRESS campus. It flies directly away from the Colony. The Party **sees it clearly but is not required to fight it**. The broken wall, screaming alarms, and evacuating personnel make the breach unmistakable. Its Source physically accompanies it, so it is not one of the prisoners that will reconstruct in an assigned cell. EGRESS will not readily explain what escaped; its destination and the possibility of distant draconic contact remain an intentional future-campaign mystery.
- The other perimeter cells are broken open or have lost their boundaries. **Frames are already fighting Incursions throughout campus** and escorting wounded researchers. Ordinary Frames fight using their own automaton routines; Engram-loaded **Proxies** may assist but are not the people whose Memory they express.
- The **central Scar** remains inside a separately marked, vast cell. Its raw Weight is not a monster that can be killed. Its Boundary is dangerous, and its inner Horizon is catastrophic. EGRESS's conduits skirt the Boundary, never enter the Horizon.
- The enormous **reservoir behind the central hall is empty**. Fry are still rushing through the exposed glass conduits, desperately seeking a return route. The Mother can be heard screaming in labor, but is not visible from campus. This is a revelation when the Party enters.
- **Cell 04**, containing an apparently ordinary black Sheep, is still under uninterrupted observation by a Frame. The observation protocol has survived the outage even while the others have failed.
- Previously contained Sources **remain in their assigned cells** unless an earlier event actually moved, transferred, Anchored, or destroyed a particular vessel. Use the *Cell-Bound Reconstruction* rule in the mechanical reference entries linked on this page for those still housed there.

### What Prior EGRESS Visitors Could Have Seen
If the Party visited **Containment** earlier, allow it to recognize some of the facility's residents from behind working boundaries. If it visited **Playing for Keeps**, its familiarity with Frames, Proxies, corrupted Engrams, comparison, and closure machinery likewise gives actionable familiarity, but **the Vault layout and Mastermind procedure are not being replayed**.

During an earlier Containment tour, show a handful of these in passing without presenting each as a quest: the Waiting Woman on a bench, the soundproof Bellringer cell with warning lamps, two bickering guards, a normal sheep watched by a motionless Frame, the Embrace's harmless-looking folded arms, or the shifting Unfixed behind protective observation shutters. The Stitcher and Glassblower may be seen from safe galleries. A routed, unresolved Nightmare Nurse may occupy Cell 11 **only if that route actually occurred**. Keep the Mother's reproductive purpose, the fry's role as the Institute's circulation network, the central Scar's full hazard, and Cell 12's Dragon **hidden from visitors**. An earlier tour should seed recognition, not spoil the reveal.

### The Twelve Containment Cases
The following are **twelve cell designations**, not twelve mandatory fights. Every case has a genuine retained truth and a false conclusion made dangerous by missing Identity. The identifying names are GM-facing EGRESS nicknames, not necessarily historical names. Class, Source, trigger, reconstruction, and mechanics are in the mechanical reference entries linked on this page; the table below owns their recognizable presentation and encounter role.

The roster mixes mundane-looking figures (Waiting Woman, Guards, Sheep, Bellringer) with grotesquely false anatomy (Embrace, Stitcher, Unfixed, Mother) and familiar-but-impossible functions (Glassblower, Doorman, Nurse). Do **not** scale each into a boss encounter. EGRESS's research labels are operational shorthand, not answers about their true lives.

### Facility Layout — The Prison Around the Scar
Occlusion Containment has a **vast central chamber/cell** enclosing the jagged Scar, an accessible working floor and observation ring around it, and **twelve perimeter cells** facing inward like a prison around a central yard. Galleries, service access, Frame corridors, observation stations, and scar-interface controls occupy the ring. Pipes run along the **Scar's Boundary**, outside its Horizon, and feed containment interfaces on the outer cells. Behind the complex sits Cell 08's open maternity chamber and a large, diverted-river **reservoir** connected to the same conduits.

The Scar looks like a monstrous fault cut across the central floor, not a portable Source or a humanoid Veiled. Its edge is legible through structural distortions and repulsive pressure. A clearly marked **Horizon** lies farther in; no casual forced movement should cause an untelegraphed disappearance. The facility may harness Boundary interference but cannot safely feed ordinary people, Incursion bodies, or Sources into the Horizon. The site-specific distances, effects, and bypass procedures belong in the mechanical reference entries linked on this page.

### The Three Campus Spotlights
The Party should face **three identifiable escaped Incursions before reaching the dragon's opening**, but these can be staged as consecutive engagements amid fighting Frames, not one twelve-creature initiative. Fighting matters: Dispersal clears staff and civilians, buys movement, and can send a cell-bound prisoner back toward its original Source.

1. **Nurse or Embrace — coercive care or attachment.** If the earlier unresolved Nightmare Nurse / Bed was routed here, replay that pair with its existing actions and with prior player knowledge. Otherwise use the Embrace. Both present Grapple-heavy battlefield control rather than an ordinary melee slugfest.
2. **Glassblower — the walking furnace.** Frames struggle to keep campus equipment from igniting as a black artisan radiates intolerable heat. The party can Disperse it or manipulate cover, cooling, and battlefield paths to let evacuation pass.
3. **Stitcher — everything joined incorrectly.** By default, its threads stitch researchers to Frames or structures. **Bellringer** is an explicitly supported substitute if its soundproof cell has failed especially badly or the session needs a fear-and-sound fight instead. Do not run both as mandatory third fights.

After Dispersal, the three prisoners can reconstruct in their original cells **if their Sources remain there and their Reconstruction Conditions hold**. Without functioning barriers they may escape again; once containment is restored, that same return is a viable recapture. If a Source has genuinely transferred out of its cell, apply the universal transfer rules instead of teleporting it home.

### The Hole and the Two Guards
The dragon's exit has torn a high, jagged opening through the outer Containment wall and adjoining roof. **The Guards from Cell 03 stand in the hole, side by side, having finally found something they can agree to defend.** Their contradictory remembered instructions can produce comic bickering. They are an obstacle and an opportunity: the Party may reason with them, move around them, Disperse them, or exploit the fact that their post is now the **opening**, not a particular faction's property. Avoid presenting them as a surprise fourth compulsory boss battle.

Through the opening, the party sees the inner containment yard for the first time. The Scar bisects the center like a torn horizon. The Mother can be heard—and now seen—crying out from the maternity chamber beyond. The reservoir is dry. In transparent pipes across the floor and around the Scar's periphery, countless small black aquatic figures thrash against the engineered flow. Some have already spilled into the working floor. Cells stand open. At least the three campus threats are starting to reconstruct beside their old Sources. **A single Frame is still facing the Sheep. It has never looked away.**

### The Revelation — The Brood Is the Machine
EGRESS researchers insist that the apparatus needs the **fry circulating through the boundary conduits** to regulate the Scar and reestablish confinement. They are correct about the technical function and wrong about the complete relationship. The children are **not disposable fuel** and do not create new Weight. They are a finite, distributed expression of the Mother's actual mnemonic burden, kept in engineered motion by a preserved mother–child connection. The false birth never stops because the parent and offspring have never been permitted a completed return.

The operators **explicitly oppose** diverting the fry into the reservoir: they expect a total loss of conduction, and their established safety logic makes their objection reasonable. Their objection does not create an impossible mandatory persuasion check or make every technician a combatant. The controls are physically accessible. Through labels, transparent pipes, Mother–fry behavior, nursery intake markings, and Orlo's testable readings, the Party can infer the truth even if it never visited EGRESS before.

**The Return** is simple and tangible: the Party opens/reverses the relevant reservoir return and circulation routing, allowing the fry to swim home **through** the functioning conduits rather than trapping them permanently outside the system. The reservoir fills. The Mother recognizes her children. The screaming and endless labor stop. **Relationship is Restored**—not by discovering a hidden biography or solving a twelve-step Engram puzzle, but by correcting the false assumption that childbirth never reached reunion. Existing offspring persist as a **bounded, Anchored distributed network** tied to the Mother's truthful relationship and the reservoir; no new ones are continually produced. The exact source/Anchor and transition constraints are defined in the mechanical reference entries linked on this page.

This restoration brings back the regulated brood circuit and **re-energizes the Scar-Boundary containment fields**. It does not erase the Scar, merge the other Sources, move prisoners instantaneously, or justify the Institute's future use of the Mother without scrutiny. The party has rescued the facility while revealing what its infrastructure has been doing to a family.

### The Sheep — The Victory That Breaks Observation
The Sheep is harmless **only while watched**. Its Frame observer can maintain unbroken visual attention without blinking. Under the failing roof, a telegraphed beam collapse or other predictable disruption may destroy that Frame, leaving a technician or PC to take Sheep Duty. A watcher can be relieved by another observer; sole living watchers must withstand increasingly difficult **Will saves not to blink**. A critical failure or sustained failures may cause an involuntary blink. A failed solitary watch can be fatal. The sightline, risk, and handoff must all be visible before the lethal event.

When the restored network begins sealing cells, the Sheep's restored **opaque boundary cuts outside line of sight before its specific suppression cycle finishes**. If the watcher is outside and nobody has taken over **inside** or preserved a legal observation aperture, observation breaks—and the Sheep kills the last watcher without ever being seen acting. This is a consequence of restoring the apparatus, **not** an unavoidable cinematic death. PCs can protect the Frame, plan a handoff, delay the seal, preserve a view, or arrange a watcher inside while the Sheep-specific field calibrates. A player who repeatedly fails escalating Will saves without accepting help is accepting a genuinely lethal risk.

The Sheep remains a horrifyingly ordinary black sheep. Never describe what happens while no one is watching; describe only the suddenly missing interval and the aftermath. Exact observation, Will-save, death trigger, and suppression timing live in the mechanical reference entries linked on this page.

### Recapture — Dispersal Finally Means Containment
Once the return circuit works and cell boundaries have reestablished, remaining escaped Incursions with **cell-bound Sources** can be Dispersed through ordinary combat or Frame operations. Each reconstructs at its original Source inside a now closed and appropriately tuned cell, or remains suppressed according to its saved profile. These are **contained, unresolved** burdens—not defeated lives. Cells whose Sources were transferred cannot be automatically reset. The Dragon's mobile Source leaves with it. Other Incursions can escape the Colony if operations elsewhere are ignored or impossible to complete.

The Party need not personally fight every prisoner. Frame squads can handle ordinary recapture where their capabilities survive; the players decide which threats, personnel, infrastructure, and objectives deserve their scarce attention. No new mandatory twelve-fold Source investigation arises.

### Player Leverage and Fail-Forward
- Protect civilians and Frames in three campus fights; prioritize evacuation against recapture or evidence preservation.
- Inspect Sources and cell systems without treating the Scar's Horizon as ordinary floor space; use railings, seals, conduits, and the central Scar's repulsion defensively.
- Bypass, negotiate with, or Disperse the Guards; identify and use the dragon's exit as a route into the central yard.
- Recognize the brood's actual maternal relationship and contradict EGRESS's competent but incomplete instructions by restoring the return plumbing.
- Repair or improvise water routing when the normal controls are damaged; ordinary PF2e abilities, strong plans, and physical leverage matter more than a single mandatory Occultism roll.
- Save or relieve the Sheep's observer before the field blocks sight, or arrange continued observation until signature-specific suppression engages.
- After containment returns, help Frames Disperse and recapture remaining cell-bound Incursions, while accepting that several may already have escaped.
- Pursue a live faction operation when present, even if it requires allowing an individual Incursion to go uncontained.

### Consequences and Resolution
**Full emergency success:** the brood returns; the Mother's false labor ends; the Scar remains dangerous but regulated; most original prisoners can be recaptured; EGRESS keeps some valuable routing and containment capacity. Whether the Sheep's watcher survives depends on actual preparation. No blanket authorization for EGRESS's old institutional practices follows.

**Partial success:** the mother–child relation may be restored while sections of the field, personnel, Engram records, or cell boundaries remain lost; containment may work only in parts of the building. Frames can preserve some sectors while others escape.

**No return:** containment cannot be restored through the old brood network while the relation remains fractured. EGRESS may evacuate, sacrifice infrastructure, or improvise other valid technologies; Source and Scar laws still apply. A high-pressure crisis need not be resolved with this exact plumbing if players produce another materially sound topology.

**Outside consequences:** the Dragon is not automatically recovered; other Incursions can leave; new scars, damaged vessels, lost Frames, and compromised research persist according to play. Nothing about restoring the Mother's relationship retroactively resolves twelve histories. The central Scar remains a long-term unsolved danger.

### Act III → Finale Handoff — One EGRESS Front Result
The Breach is **optional**. Darkwing's allocation of attention determines how much of EGRESS's other essential work survives: **Occlusion Containment** is the immediate emergency, while the **Engram Vault** is the required EGRESS Asset for *The Burden*. They are distinct facilities/functions. EGRESS personnel who are forced to handle the Breach themselves cannot also fully protect the Vault.

| Party commitment to The Breach | One EGRESS front / Engram Vault / Finale Pressure state | EGRESS capability in *The Burden*, Phase III |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct intervention** — Darkwing meaningfully participates in the Breach response | **Stable / +2** | **2 Memory Frames** |
| **Delegated intervention** — Darkwing assigns a genuinely capable ally/team to the Breach rather than attending personally | **Damaged / +1** | **1 Memory Frame** |
| **Unattended** — Darkwing does not intervene or arrange a viable delegation | **Lost / +0** | **0 Memory Frames** |

These are the **prepared baseline triage consequences**, determined by where the Party places its own scarce time and capable allies. In direct play, honor concrete deviations: if Darkwing saves the Vault by an unexpected method, sacrifices it deliberately, or arrives after it was already lost, the actual established result supersedes the baseline. No extra faction roll overturns an onscreen outcome. Delegation is not a free order: record the available champion, personnel, and commitment, and do not assign the same people to incompatible simultaneous operations.

**Do not confuse an independent narrative consequence with another front measurement:**
- **Containment aftermath:** whether the Mother/brood circuit and prisoner cells were restored, and which Incursions remain loose. This is a real outcome, but not a second Finale Pressure or Asset-status score.
- **The single EGRESS-front result:** Stable/+2, Damaged/+1, or Lost/+0 establishes Engram Vault finale status **and** full/half/no Memory Frame capacity.
- **EGRESS stance:** Insight and Relationship decide whether the available Frames **Assist** Darkwing or **Interfere** by protecting the Echo. Do not re-gate Frames through a separate Operational Integrity field.

Write that one front result, stance, and actual surviving people and containment consequences into the Act III state before *The Burden*. Its prepared direct/delegated/unattended baselines are conditional on actual actions; other crises have their own outcome definitions.

## Cell-Bound Return

### Cell-Bound Reconstruction — Facility Exception
An unresolved Incursion that has been **Dispersed away from its assigned cell** reconstructs **in that original cell** instead of at its previous body's position **only if**:

1. its actual Source vessel remains within the cell's recorded containment focus;
2. its original, classified Recognition signature still supports that cell's Reconstruction Condition;
3. the Source has not been Stabilized, Anchored, transferred, Ruptured, or otherwise changed so those facts are false.

Use the Source's ordinary **Class-based Stabilization Window** and Reconstruction Pressure. This rule changes **where** the Incursion forms, **not when**, how much Weight it carries, or what happens at Dispersal. It does **not** automatically teleport the Source. If the corresponding signature-specific suppression field is active, reconstruction is suppressed and the Source remains unresolved in the cell. If the field is down, the Incursion reconstructs inside an open/breached cell and may leave again; if the physical/regulated boundary is operational, the body remains trapped there.

**Exceptions:** The Dragon's Source actually travels with it. The Mother's distribution and Restoration are defined separately. The Nurse/Bed remain one Incursion and use their existing joint Dispersal rules. If any Source has genuinely moved, apply the normal Source rules rather than forcing a respawn in its former cell.

### Classified Sources, Not Automatically Resolved
EGRESS maintains an authentic **Source vessel and measured profile** in each occupied cell. The facility has been using a captured Incursion's genuine subjective Memory to produce **`-OCC` Engrams**; those corrupted records can key a known signature or help compare against authentic Engrams without constituting truthful Identity Restoration themselves. Each active prisoner remains a separate Source except where a case explicitly defines a shared/distributed Source. There is no free transfer of prisoners' Weight into the central Scar merely because the bodies encounter its Boundary.

## Background Effects

## Background / Optional Containment Effects

These are **not extra compulsory fights**. They provide a small runnable consequence when the Party approaches an otherwise offscreen resident. The GM may use level-appropriate creature statistics if a player insists on fighting rather than bypassing or containing one.

- **Cell 01 — Waiting Woman, Echo / Relationship.** While within **20 feet** and able to perceive her, a creature must attempt **DC 28 Will** on first entry and at the start of each subsequent turn. On failure, **slowed 1** until the start of its next turn, and it can use its remaining actions only to remain close or take actions consistent with waiting; on critical failure, **stunned 1** and it cannot voluntarily move away until the end of its next turn. Success avoids the effect until its next turn; critical success grants 1-minute immunity. This is a custom compulsion, not ordinary movement control for the entire encounter.
- **Cell 07 — Unfixed, Composite / Form.** A creature watching its body continuously within **30 feet** attempts **DC 30 Fortitude** at the start of its turn; failure **sickened 1**, critical failure **sickened 2**. Looking away breaks this exposure without using a new Search/Hide puzzle. Re-exposure can recur but cannot increase Sickened above 2 through this effect alone.
- **Cell 10 — Doorman, Echo / Pattern.** Up to **four** temporary black thresholds appear in nearby traversable squares; entering one allows the Doorman to emerge from another as a normal Stride of up to 30 feet, consuming its normal move action. A creature that deliberately follows can attempt **DC 33 Will**; on failure it emerges at a different existing door chosen by the GM, and on critical failure is also **off-guard until the end of its next turn**. A door is part of the Incursion, not an independent Source or a new labyrinth subsystem. Doors vanish on Dispersal.
- **Cell 11 — Nightmare Nurse.** Use the existing **Evin Peakwalker Reconstruction (Caretaker / Nightmare Nurse) — Creature 13** and the **Bed — Creature 10**, their joint rules, and the original Class II Source values. If that unresolved Weight was never actually routed here, there is no Nurse to fight, and the cell is empty.
- **Cell 12 — Dragon.** A Creature 15 or higher, visually a full black dragon; **do not run combat** as the default campus scene. Its carried original scale-Source travels with it, so the cell's Reconstruction geometry cannot recall it. Its further behavior/destination is deliberately unauthored.

## Return to Service

## Reinitialization and Recapture

### Phased Return to Service
At the conclusion of the **full round of returning fry**, the motherhood Restoration and hydraulic circuit stabilize. EGRESS's **perimeter field barriers then energize and seal**, including an **opaque Sheep cell boundary**. Inform players at least one full intervening round in advance that opaque shutters and cell barriers will activate; this is visible on warning lamps and announced by staff or instruments. A successful intervention can delay or alter closure.

**After one further full round**, the stored signature-specific suppression calibrations finish coming online in the intact cells. Until then, an Incursion already present may remain active but trapped by the barrier; it is not automatically teleported, killed, or Stabilized. Cells whose physical boundary is actually destroyed need ordinary repair or Frame perimeter security before they qualify as successfully recaptured.

**Sheep exception:** Cell 04's boundary becomes **opaque when fields first seal**, but its own signature suppression does not complete until the **next round**. The intervening period **requires uninterrupted living/Frame observation** through a still-open aperture, an already-positioned inside watcher, or another actual visible line. The instantaneous loss of the last watcher is lethal; the beam hazard and the expected loss of an outside sightline make the risk a clear tactical problem, not an unannounced cinematic execution.

### Recapture Operation
After barriers and appropriate suppression are functional, Frames and PCs can **Disperse** a loose Incursion and let its Source reconstruct or remain suppressed inside the original cell under **Cell-Bound Reconstruction**. This is containment, **not** truthful Resolution. Use local or faction-established Frame capabilities to adjudicate ordinary recapture elsewhere; do not simulate a dozen independent turns when PCs are not there.

## Running the Facility

## GM Operational Reference

**Default spotlight lineup:** (1) Nightmare Nurse/Bed **if unresolved and routed here**, otherwise Embrace; (2) Glassblower; (3) Stitcher, or Bellringer as deliberate substitute.

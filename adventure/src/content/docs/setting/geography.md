The river is the Colony's practical connection to the wider world. Within the city, institutions, workplaces, old damage, and the movement of people give each district its character. Use those distinctions to place scenes; the directory below links every recorded location and groups sites under their containing district or region.

## Reading the City

The civic center brings the :ref[Keeper District]{#location.keeper-district}, the :ref[Hall of Keepers]{#location.hall-of-keepers}, and the :ref[Town Square]{#location.town-square} into the Party's early life. :ref[Abiding Way]{#location.abiding-way} connects care for the living Keeper with remembrance of the Forgotten. The Colony's slums lie within it, in the same district as the :ref[House of Abiding]{#location.house-of-abiding}. The :ref[Dawn District]{#location.dawn-district} gives reform a visible civic home.

The Colony also works for a living. The :ref[Mercantile District]{#location.mercantile-district}, :ref[Riverfront]{#location.riverfront}, :ref[Milltown]{#location.milltown}, and :ref[West District]{#location.west-district} bring trade, transport, labor, and public works into view. Their practical needs should remain present when faction arguments become abstract.

:ref[High Bank]{#location.high-bank} and :ref[Lower Bank]{#location.lower-bank} distinguish older and newer settlement. :ref[Crescent Island]{#location.crescent-island} preserves structures left behind by a changed river course. :ref[Old Town]{#location.old-town} carries the damage of an earlier Incursion, while :ref[the Duskway]{#location.duskway} lies between it and more populated districts.

The :ref[Pilgrim Quarter]{#location.pilgrim-quarter} and :ref[Pilgrim's Rest]{#location.pilgrim-s-rest} connect the city's daily life to travelers and seekers. Beyond the city, :ref[the Duskwood]{#location.duskwood} and :ref[Candlestone Caverns]{#location.candlestone-caverns} offer dangers that exist independently of the Colony's explanation of the Veiled.

:::gm-note
District membership is not a travel-time rule. Establish routes, distances, sightlines, and access from the scene's map before they become tactical stakes. A building's institutional connection does not make it physically adjacent to another institution's headquarters.
:::

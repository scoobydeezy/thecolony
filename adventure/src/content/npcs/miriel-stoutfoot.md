## Overview

The liaison is a halfling civic worker assigned to recruit, advise, and maintain contact with Darkwing.
A parent or grandparent helped found the Dawn Assembly when it was still a dangerous revolutionary movement. The liaison inherited both its ideals and the unfinished responsibility of making them real.

## Personality

Earnest, capable, and politically restrained. They are better in neighborhood meetings than on public platforms and more comfortable explaining policy than delivering slogans.
They understand compromise without becoming cynical and possess a surprising firmness when someone dismisses Dawn as merely symbolic opposition.

## Motivation

Turn Dawn’s inherited ideals into genuine civic participation, accountable institutions, and practical change for ordinary residents.

## Primary Protection

**The possibility that ordinary citizens can meaningfully shape the institutions governing them.**
They want more than the Order’s removal. They want participation, consent, and the ability to challenge whatever system replaces it.

## Fears

That Dawn will gain power while losing its purpose, turning participation into another performance controlled from above.

## Blind Spot

They believe legitimate participation can compensate for inadequate knowledge or capacity.
They know how to make a decision belong to the public. They are less prepared for a choice whose consequences no voter fully understands or whose burden willing citizens may be unable to carry.

## Narrative Role

The liaison gives Dawn its sincere human face and ensures the faction remains more than its leader’s ambition.
Their arc tests whether reform can survive its own political success. When Dawn gains real authority, they must decide whether continued access to power is helping them change the system—or merely teaching them to excuse the person controlling it.

## Portrayal Notes

Earnest, capable, and more comfortable with a petition than a platform. Speak in concrete processes, responsibilities, and consequences. Their idealism is durable because it has learned how much paperwork meaningful participation requires.

## Pressure Response

Restraint becomes firmness. They stop mediating personalities and name the promise, procedure, or consequence that cannot be negotiated away.

## Signature Quote

*“People do not need us to choose correctly for them. They need enough truth and enough power to choose at all.”*

## Signature Object

An early Dawn Charter covered in a founder’s handwritten revisions.

## Performance Inspiration

Amy Poehler

## Responsibilities

They perform much of the Assembly’s unglamorous daily work:
* Managing citizen petitions.
* Coordinating neighborhood representatives.
* Drafting proposals and meeting agendas.
* Tracking promises and public commitments.
* Maintaining relationships with families, workers, and civic groups.
* Turning the leader’s speeches into practical administration.

They know how Dawn functions because they do much of the functioning.

## Reform Inheritance

They preserve an early copy of the Dawn Charter covered in a founder’s handwritten revisions.
To them, the document is not a sacred relic. It is proof that reform was always meant to remain unfinished, revisable, and answerable to the people it served.

## Relationship to the Leader

They know the leader is vain, manipulative, and capable of exploiting sincere people.
They also know he can fill a square, force issues into public debate, and achieve concessions that decades of principled petitions could not. They remain beside him because they believe his ambition can be used to accomplish something real.
He relies upon their sincerity to legitimize him. They rely upon his charisma to make sincerity politically effective.

## Relationship to the Party

The liaison recognizes Darkwing’s value before the leader does and approaches them honestly.
They explain Dawn’s concerns, arrange access, warn about political consequences, and admit where the movement lacks authority or evidence. They become the Party’s continuing Dawn contact because they treat them as partners rather than symbols.
As trust grows, they may also warn Darkwing when the leader intends to use their actions irresponsibly.

## Relationship to the Keeper

They are among the first faction representatives to ask the Keeper directly what she wants.
However, sincerity does not make them immune to pressure. They may ask her to speak publicly, represent the movement, or accept personal risk because her participation could prevent greater harm.
Their challenge is recognizing that earnest persuasion can undermine agency as easily as command.

## Troop State

Not Undead. Begin with four contiguous 10-foot-square segments. At 147 HP, reduce to three segments; at 73 HP, reduce to two. The Troop is slowed 1 and has no reactions. Its segments are false Sundaras, not separate grave probes. After full Dispersal, it returns three full rounds later if the condition persists; no individual Grave Resonance is produced.

**Troop movement / defenses:** Use Remaster troop-movement and segment rules as written, not legacy "condense to a 20×20 block" behavior; segments must remain contiguous and share edge lengths as required. Do not use a 5-foot gap as passable by a 10-foot segment; individual Medium zombies can pass narrow gates normally. These are normal troop geometry restrictions, not a bespoke "fluid" teleporter.  
**Grave Tide:** The mass can occupy space with other creatures and creatures can treat its occupied squares as **difficult terrain** (mirrors the published Shambler Troop's structural behavior, not undead biology).  
**Shambling Onslaught ◆ or ◆◆:** once per round; affected creatures in its occupied segments or within 5 feet attempt **DC 32 basic Reflex**; one-action version **4d6+10 bludgeoning**, two-action version **5d6+14 bludgeoning**. Its aura is a physical press of black bodies; the Troop doesn't have individual Strike rolls for every silhouette.  
**Targeting:** It threatens paths toward the cemetery's center and shifts toward PCs who call Sunny if within the call's hearing. It attacks obstructing defenders, pressures fences, and is *not* a passive backdrop.  
**Troop Dispersal:** Dropping to 0 HP temporarily breaks the mass. Under Totality it may reform after an encounter-specific interval if its Reconstruction Condition persists; its components remain part of the same conserved Weight. **No grave reading on its segment loss or total Dispersal.**  
**Tuning:** This is a **scaled custom combat profile**, *not* the published Creature 4 stat block. Adjust AC, damage, HP and speed after observing actual six-PC level-12 combat. Use the official published Troop as a **movement/segment reference**, not an encounter-level threat.

### Optional — Break from the Horde (Not Yet Locked)

When the Troop crosses an HP threshold and loses a segment, it **may** produce one independent Sundara in the segment's former legal square **only if one of the four individual slots is currently vacant**. This fills the vacant slot and **cancels the corresponding pending emergence**; it does not create a fifth body, an extra turn, a new Source, or an independent additional Weight. If the four slots are occupied, the lost segment simply disperses. This experiment helps ranged attackers sometimes produce useful individual targets, but is **not required** by the new nearest-grave search and should be disabled unless the final tabletop test benefits from it.

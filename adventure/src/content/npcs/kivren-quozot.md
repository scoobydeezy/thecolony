## Overview

The Instigator is an influential Shattered field operative who identifies pressure points, reunites Fractured people with dangerous objects, and turns hidden wounds into public crises.
They are not the movement’s architect. Their influence comes from a record of operations that work—usually beginning with the question:
*“What do you think would happen if—”*

## Personality

Curious, over-eager, and physically skittish. They become animated when discussing Fractured Objects, institutional reactions, or mnemonic patterns, often asking inappropriate questions while others are still recovering from the consequences.
When personally confronted, their confidence disappears. They watch exits, startle easily, and run before deciding whether escape is necessary.
A tengu with a distinctive pale patch around one eye makes them immediately recognizable and well suited to a later chase through Old Town.

## Motivation

Force hidden suffering into public view so the Colony can no longer remain stable by pretending its wounds do not exist.

## Primary Protection

**The truth the Colony has made survivable by hiding.**
They believe concealed suffering will continue indefinitely unless someone forces it into public view.

## Fears

That the Colony will absorb every revelation, bury every victim again, and continue unchanged because exposure was never made impossible to ignore.

## Blind Spot

They confuse exposure with accountability.
They believe making suffering visible is itself a form of justice, while leaving protection, repair, and responsibility to whoever survives the revelation.

## Narrative Role

The Instigator personalizes the Shattered’s methods without becoming the movement’s supreme authority.
Their first operation reveals a genuine forgotten relationship through an act of deliberate cruelty and establishes misdirection as a recurring Shattered tactic. Their return at the Broken Bell connects the initial attack to the Colony’s buried history and forces the Party to confront the difference between revealing a wound and carrying what follows from it.

## Portrayal Notes

Animated and intellectually delighted by dangerous patterns until attention turns directly toward them. Their body is always preparing to flee even when their mouth is still asking questions. Insight and irresponsibility should remain equally visible.

## Pressure Response

Their intellectual confidence collapses into physical flight. Once escape is impossible, excitement rapidly returns if someone engages the underlying idea.

## Signature Quote

*“I didn’t create the wound. I only stopped everyone from stepping around it.”*

## Signature Object

A bundle of wrapping cloths and mismatched object tags used to move Fractured Objects without revealing their origin.

## Performance Inspiration

David Dastmalchian

## Methods

The Instigator finds people carrying grief the Colony refuses to recognize, then returns the fractured objects capable of giving that grief shape if such an object can be identified.
Their first operation reunites a Fractured woman with a worn cloth toy that belonged to her forgotten child. She remembers only flashes of the child as an infant, while the child’s later life—and eventual service as a Keeper—has disappeared.
The resulting Veiled manifestation reconstructs the child as they exist within her surviving memory: an enormous, helpless infant seeking its mother.
The Instigator expected danger. They did not know precisely what form it would take or who would die.

## Relationship to the Victim

The risk to Gressa—whether she survives, is injured, or dies—unsettles them, but does not make them reject the operation. Her death is **not** a fixed outcome of Bad Maps.
They argue that she was already being consumed by a grief everyone around her insisted belonged to no one:
*“You call it murder because you saw the moment it happened. No one had a word for what was happening to her yesterday.”*
Their insight is genuine. So is their responsibility.

## Relationship to the Party

Darkwing’s presence provides convenient misdirection. Armed outsiders standing at the center of an unprecedented attack become the most believable explanation, even though the Party was never the operation’s target.
When the Party confronts the Instigator at the Broken Bell, they may flee if threatened; if they remain, are cornered, or become convinced they will not be killed, their fear quickly gives way to excitement that the Party remembered them.
They become the Shattered’s recurring liaison whenever Darkwing follows one of the faction’s paths.

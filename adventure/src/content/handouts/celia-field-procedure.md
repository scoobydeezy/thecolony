> **REFERENCE SOURCE / CRR–4**  
> Observation: a dominant spiderlike Incursion maintains physical possession of the suspected devotional reference Focus while manifested. Upon Dispersal it releases the Focus and can be measured briefly.  
> 
> **Exclusion radius:** Mark **30 meters from the plinth** in every direction. Verify that **no other candidate Fractured Objects** enter this field except the proposed three. Preservation vitrines do not count as Resonance isolation. Staff must move nonselected candidates beyond the exclusion boundary before formal activation.  
> 
> **Test:** During a Stabilization Window, place the Focus on the plinth. Select **three** objects supported by the material catalogue, supply their accession identifiers, position all three inside the field, and activate the frame. Observe whether their relationship yields Coherence or the Incursion reconstructs.  
> 
> **After test:** Remove the three candidates from the field and restore their individual custody. Correct historical association does not itself demonstrate Consent, Capacity, or valid Anchoring.  
> 
> **Caution:** An absence of observable Coherence is not proof that each object is unrelated. Reconsider the relationship hypothesis using independent records. Do not destroy suspect Sources. — **C.H.**

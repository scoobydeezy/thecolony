A cracked overhead beam spans the observation station. It becomes visibly unstable before falling. Whenever a large area effect or structural impact reaches its supports, attempt a **structural Fortitude save +20 vs DC 33**; on a failure it collapses, and on success it remains but takes a cumulative **–2** on its next stability save. If no such effect occurs, obvious rhythmic structural shaking triggers the first stability save after **two rounds** in the central hall. PCs can move the Frame, brace the structure, redirect combat, or replace its watch before another save occurs.

**Collapse:** 10-foot burst beneath the beam, **DC 33 basic Reflex** against **10d6 bludgeoning**. A Frame left at the point of impact is destroyed or loses its sightline on failure; an NPC technician can take over only if they have a genuine sightline. A PC is not automatically destroyed by the beam and resolves normal damage. If the final active watcher is lost, resolve the Sheep's lethal trigger normally. The danger is telegraphed and can be averted; do not dictate an NPC death if players preserve observation.

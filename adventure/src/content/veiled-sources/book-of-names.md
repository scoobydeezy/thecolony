## The Same Book Throughout

The physical Book remains the Source through the Monument, Composite, Echo and Chorus. Its Class is V throughout; the diminishing **Institutional Class** in Phase IV belongs to the encounter’s Ward and does not reclassify the Book.

Use :ref[Keeper-bound Handling]{#rule.book.keeper-bound-handling}. Serevan’s eligibility is a deliberate unexplained fact; it does not prove a specific secret biography. The Book’s vessel cannot be destroyed or forced to Rupture by reflected Backlash. Its Ward can be Breached, and its Weight can be transferred, partitioned or Anchored through valid procedures.

Its history as a community memorial and its intrinsic physical rules are distinct from whatever an institution claims it means. The finale recovers that history without making the moral decision for the living.

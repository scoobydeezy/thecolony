EGRESS can deliberately transfer unresolved Weight without causing Mnemonic Rupture, but only after the phenomenon is sufficiently classified.

Before routing can begin, EGRESS must have:
* a measured **Source Class**;
* an identified **Resonance profile** for the Incursion being routed;
* a prepared destination with **Capacity at least equal to Class**;
* an engineered topology compatible with that Resonance.

On successful completion, the Weight transfers without a Rupture Pulse. Unless deliberately transformed, it preserves **Source Level, Class, restored Identity, and current stabilization state**. Controlled routing is a transfer technique, not Resolution.

The required apparatus, action costs, checks and hazards belong to the encounter using the technique. Establish them before the transfer begins; a successful transfer does not supply missing Identity or Consent.

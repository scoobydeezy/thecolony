## Definition

**Disperse** means destroying an Incursion's current corporeal or environmental reconstruction. Usually this happens when a Veiled creature reaches 0 Hit Points. A complex hazard, ritual loop, Wound or unusual boss may define an equivalent Dispersal condition.

Dispersal is not death and not destruction of the Weight. It is not regeneration either: if the Source remains unresolved and Resonance remains active, **reality tries again**.

## Procedure

When an Incursion reaches 0 HP (or meets its encounter's Dispersal condition):

1. The manifestation is Dispersed. Its immediate physical threat ends.
2. The Source's Ward is absent, Backlash stops, and Mnemonic Weight is suppressed; the Vessel is physically ordinary while it remains safely exposed (see :ref[Active Sources]{#rule.active-sources}).
3. Check the Source's Reconstruction Condition (see :ref[Sources and Resonance]{#rule.sources-and-resonance}):
   - **True:** a Stabilization Window opens at Reconstruction Pressure 0 (see :ref[The Stabilization Window]{#rule.stabilization-window}).
   - **False:** no Reconstruction Pressure begins. The unresolved Source becomes dormant. If the condition becomes true again before the Source is Stabilized, Anchored or Contained, a new Window begins at Pressure 0.

## Examples

In :ref[Bad Maps]{#encounter.bad-maps}, reducing the baby to 0 HP Disperses it. If the toy is still within 30 feet of Gressa, a Window opens at Pressure 0. If they are more than 30 feet apart, no Pressure begins and the toy is a dormant, unresolved Source.

## Interactions

Dispersal stops the immediate threat, protects civilians, interrupts the false pattern, exposes the Source more safely than a Breach, may expose new evidence, and creates the opening for Identity work, :ref[Containment]{#rule.containment}, :ref[Source Transfer]{#rule.source-transfer} or deliberate Source manipulation. It does not Stabilize the Weight or restore any truth.

*Pure-black, apparently normal sheep; Source 14 / Class III, collar in its cell*

**The Sheep is completely harmless while any one creature maintains uninterrupted direct visual observation.** It cannot Strike, apply aura conditions, or move offensively while observed. A Frame with a working unbroken sightline can maintain observation indefinitely because it does not blink. Ordinary glass is transparent; an opaque energized containment boundary blocks line of sight. Scrying or delayed images do not substitute for present direct observation unless the Party demonstrates an equally reliable, immediate, uninterrupted optical link and the GM explicitly establishes it as such.

### Establish / Hand Off Sheep Duty
A creature that can actually see the Sheep can **begin observing it without an action**. A watcher can announce a handoff to another creature with an established sightline; overlapping observation is permitted. A watching creature may still perform actions that preserve the sightline and visual attention, but cannot simultaneously avert its eyes, become blinded, or leave line of sight and claim to be watching. More than one watcher means a blink by one does **not** break observation.

### Don't Blink — Escalating Will Save
At the start of each living watcher's turn **while it remains the sole observer**, attempt **Will DC 30 + 2 per consecutive earlier sole-observer turn**, to a maximum of DC 42. This is an encounter-specific compulsion/exhaustion of sustained attention, not a universal consequence of looking at Veiled. Track a small Watch counter per current watcher and at most **one strain marker**.

- **Critical success:** Keeps watching; removes the strain marker; resets that watcher's next-turn DC to 30.
- **Success:** Keeps watching; clears strain; next consecutive turn increases DC by 2.
- **Failure:** If no strain marker, gain one **Eye Strain** marker but maintain observation; if already strained, the watcher **blinks** and loses observation momentarily.
- **Critical failure:** The watcher **blinks immediately** regardless of Eye Strain.

A watcher relieved by a different **genuine observer** stops accumulating DC; a later new stint begins at DC 30 and without Eye Strain. Merely declaring a handoff without a replacement actually seeing the Sheep does not reset observation.

### If Nobody Watches — Immediate Lethal Trigger
When the **last active watcher** loses direct observation because of blink, a closed opaque barrier, being blinded, moving out of sight, or death, **the Sheep instantly kills that last watcher** if it remains a valid creature within the facility's active mnemonic connection. This is a **special lethal Incursion effect, not a Strike, normal damage roll, or a saveable attack at the moment of loss**. Its victims may include Frames; those are destroyed or disabled rather than biologically killed. A player character can die if it has repeatedly failed the explicitly warned watch and the party doesn't intervene.

The GM **never narrates the Sheep acting unobserved**. Only the aftereffect is perceptible, and the Sheep appears perfectly ordinary when next seen. If the final observer is no longer a valid creature (e.g., its Source has transferred, a valid optical intermediary maintained observation, or the creature has already been safely extracted beyond the established connection), adjudicate the now-unobserved Sheep as a loose uncontrolled threat rather than selecting an arbitrary victim to kill. It is not a universal infinite-range assassination effect.

### Sheep-Specific Suppression
Once Cell 04's calibrated suppression finishes (one full round after opaque sealing), the Sheep's incursion is **suppressed** and uninterrupted observation is no longer required. Until then, the Party can preserve an **actual sightline** using an emergency iris/viewing aperture (Interact ◆ at the Cell 04 control), place a watcher inside beforehand, delay that cell's opaque barrier with a manual override (Interact ◆ at that control), or maintain a qualified Frame optical line. **A watcher inside the sealed cell can keep observing until suppression completes**, but may be trapped there without a separate escape/door solution.

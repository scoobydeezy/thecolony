### Stitch in Place ◆◆
**ATTACK, MANIPULATE** — One creature within 30 feet attempts a **DC 32 Reflex save** as black thread joins it to a wall, floor, or fixed furnishing.
- **Critical success:** Unaffected.
- **Success:** **Off-Guard** until the start of the Stitcher's next turn.
- **Failure:** **Grabbed** by the thread (Escape DC 32) until the end of the Stitcher's next turn.
- **Critical failure:** **Restrained** instead, same Escape DC/duration.

The thread is a temporary physical manifestation with **AC 30 and 20 HP**; destroying that thread frees only that captive. A target can Escape normally. The Stitcher can maintain at most **two separate stitched restraints** at once. The threads cease on Dispersal; no new Source is created.

### Mend the Wrong Thing ◆
**Requirement:** A creature is Grabbed or Restrained by this Stitcher's thread. That creature takes **3d6 piercing** (basic Fortitude DC 32); on a failed save it also becomes **clumsy 1 until the end of its next turn**. This is not healing.

### Gather the Threads ↻
**Trigger:** A target within 30 feet Escapes or destroys a Stitch in Place thread; **Frequency:** once per round.  
The Stitcher Strides up to half its Speed toward the former target or makes a Needle Fingers Strike if already in reach. It does not automatically reapply the restraint.

**Tactics:** It joins people, Frames, and furniture without malice; it genuinely interprets separation as breakage. Its original thimble remains in Cell 06.

**Handbell ◆** *Melee* +24; **Damage** 2d10+10 bludgeoning.

### Alarm Without End ◆◆
**AUDITORY, SONIC, EMOTION, FEAR** — Every creature that can hear the bell within **40 feet** attempts **DC 31 Fortitude**.
- **Critical success:** Unaffected.
- **Success:** **Deafened** until the start of its next turn.
- **Failure:** **Deafened** for 1 minute and **frightened 2**.
- **Critical failure:** As failure, plus **fleeing** until the end of its next turn.

A creature that cannot hear is unaffected by this ability. **Recharge:** 1d4 rounds after use. Cell 02's soundproof enclosure blocks its effects beyond the room while intact.

**Tactics:** It rings to warn, not to hunt. Frames should attempt to redirect it toward soundproof confinement or Disperse it. Its handbell Source remains in Cell 02.

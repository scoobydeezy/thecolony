The seven factions offer different answers to the burden. Each protects something the Colony needs, and each mistakes part of the problem for the whole. Play them as communities of people whose loyalties and methods can change.

## Answers in Conflict

The :ref[Order]{#faction.order-of-theia} sees the cost of losing a working protection. The :ref[Witnesses]{#faction.witnesses} insist that its human cost remain visible. The :ref[Dawn Assembly]{#faction.dawn-assembly} asks what shared responsibility would require, while the :ref[Shattered]{#faction.shattered} distrust reforms that leave the sacrifice intact.

The :ref[Cult]{#faction.cult-of-the-unknown} approaches what others fear with reverence. :ref[EGRESS]{#faction.egress-institute} approaches it as an observable mechanism. :ref[Aspis]{#faction.aspis} follows obligations and material consequences that neither moral certainty nor forgetting can erase.

Use these differences to create specific disputes. The Order may favor restricting access to something the Institute wants to study. A Witness may preserve an object that an Aspis administrator needs to trace through an inheritance. Dawn and the Shattered may agree about an injustice and disagree about what a tolerable remedy costs. These are lenses for improvisation, not a permanent alliance chart.

## Social Classes Are Not Additional Factions

The **Burdened**, **Civilians**, **Fractured**, and **Heirs** describe different relationships to the Colony's cost. They are separate from faction membership. A person's social position does not give them a fifth, eighth, or eleventh faction operation, and it does not dictate their politics.

The Burdened understand more of what the Colony carries. Civilians sustain ordinary life. The Fractured perceive breaks that the accepted account cannot explain. The Heirs inherit a compromise they did not choose. Read those positions through an individual's circumstances; do not make every member deliver the same speech.

## From Philosophy to Action

Each method supplies part of a response to mnemonic Weight. These are applications of the factions’ existing outlooks, not exclusive permissions or new bonuses.

| Faction | Useful contribution | Where its answer stops |
|---|---|---|
| :ref[Order of Theia]{#faction.order-of-theia} | Isolate Sources, protect people, prepare containment and ritual access. | Successful containment does not confer authority to choose another person’s burden. |
| :ref[Witnesses]{#faction.witnesses} | Preserve objects, provenance, relationships and evidence for a truthful destination. | Remembering does not by itself supply Capacity, Consent, or a destination. |
| :ref[Dawn Assembly]{#faction.dawn-assembly} | Organize volunteers, recover Choice, establish Consent and collective responsibility. | Political legitimacy does not manufacture metaphysical Capacity. |
| :ref[The Shattered]{#faction.shattered} | Expose hidden Sources, stolen histories and suppressed Recognition. | Recognition without Meaning and protection can create an Incursion. |
| :ref[Cult of the Unknown]{#faction.cult-of-the-unknown} | Offer relational reception, communion and candidate Form. | Meaningful experience does not prove that a proposed Form is true. |
| :ref[EGRESS]{#faction.egress-institute} | Measure Class and Capacity, protect, partition and route Weight, recover Memory. | Technical coherence cannot manufacture personhood or Consent. |
| :ref[Aspis]{#faction.aspis} | Trace ownership, debts, dependents and obligations through material consequences. | A complete account is not necessarily justice or a legitimate Anchor. |

In a scene, begin with what a person protects and what they are currently doing to protect it. Show disagreements within factions as well as between them. A leader's sincere belief does not make every subordinate agree with their method.

Between sessions, use [The Colony Turn](/docs/running/colony-turn/) to establish offscreen movement. In Act III, [Faction Operations](/docs/running/faction-operations/) explains how surviving capabilities and relationships affect the finale. A hostile faction still acts to save the Colony according to its own understanding; hostility is not absence.

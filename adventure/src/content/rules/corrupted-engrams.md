## Definition

### Authentic Memory and Proxies

An **Engram** is a technical record of experienced Memory: what a subject perceived, felt, feared, understood, expected, or intended from a first-person frame.

Engrams can recover authentic lived experience when the party can identify a compatible subject, signal, event, or fragment.

An authentic Engram is powerful evidence because it preserves something the person genuinely experienced. It is still not a complete copy of that person.

**Engrams preserve Memory only.** They do not independently preserve or Restore **Name, Relationship, Pattern, Form, Context, or Choice**. A memory can contain a remembered claim about one of those components—“she is my daughter,” “I chose this,” “this is my home”—but the Engram establishes only that the subject experienced, remembered, or believed that claim from a first-person frame. The other Identity component still requires its own truthful support.

Later experience and the meaning of the memory can also remain outside the recording.

An Engram should therefore answer questions such as:

> **What did this subject actually experience?**

It should not automatically answer:

> **Who was this person in full?**

### Frames and Proxies
EGRESS can load an Engram into a compatible automaton.

The unloaded automaton is a **Frame**. Once an Engram is loaded and actively expressed, EGRESS calls the resulting presentation a **Proxy**.

A Proxy can reproduce only what its loaded Engram actually preserves: first-person Memory, including remembered speech, habits, procedural knowledge, reactions, autobiographical claims, and other experienced content captured by the recording. The Frame does not acquire the subject's missing Name, Relationship, Pattern, Form, Context, or Choice merely because the Proxy can describe or perform remembered versions of them.

Loading an Engram into a Frame does **not** by itself create a Source, Anchor, Forgotten person, soul transfer, or resurrection. A Proxy may later become important mnemonic topology if actual Weight recognizes it, transfers into it, or uses it as a compatible vessel, but those events must follow the normal Source, Resonance, Transfer, and Anchoring rules.

A Proxy can therefore sincerely say **“I remember”** without that statement settling the separate question **“Are you the same person?”**

### Corrupted Engrams

> **A corrupted Engram is an Engram recorded from a Veiled Incursion rather than from a verified person.**

EGRESS operationally calls a Veiled Incursion an **Occlusion** in its laboratory and filing language. An Engram transcribed from one is marked **OCC** in its archival identifier. The suffix identifies the provenance of the recording; it does not mean the apparatus malfunctioned.

The corruption is not necessarily bad measurement.

EGRESS can accurately record what the Incursion experiences.

The problem is that the Incursion itself is a false reconstruction.

A corrupted Engram can therefore preserve genuine first-person Memory of:

- seeing a room that was never actually the subject's room;
- believing another person was a child, spouse, captor, subordinate, or rescuer;
- experiencing an inferred role as autobiography;
- remembering an imperative the reconstruction assembled from incomplete evidence;
- recognizing a Name, object, institution, or relationship incorrectly;
- sincerely experiencing itself as someone whose full Identity it does not possess.

The resulting record can be internally accurate while being biographically false.

The useful shorthand is:

> **The recording is accurate. The subject was not.**

## Procedure

1. **Read an Engram as Memory only.** It establishes what its subject experienced; every other Identity component needs its own truthful support (Authentic Memory and Proxies, above).
2. **Check its provenance.** An OCC record preserves what a false reconstruction experienced: accurate as a recording, false as biography.
3. **Give it ordinary Source statistics if it becomes a Source.** Define its Class, Resonance Type, Incursion Trigger and Reconstruction Condition (Corrupted Engrams as Sources, below).
4. **Correct it with authentic Memory.** An authentic Engram that materially contradicts the corrupted self-model can make the current Echo untenable, through :ref[Identity and Stabilization]{#rule.identity-and-stabilization} (Authentic Memory as Constraint, below).

## Why This Is Still Memory

A corrupted Engram does not violate the First Law by creating fake Weight from nothing.

The Veiled Incursion really existed.

It really perceived, reacted, feared, expected, and acted while manifested.

Recording those experiences therefore creates or preserves real Memory of a false reconstruction's lived state.

The mistake begins when that Memory is treated as though it were direct testimony from the forgotten person the Incursion appeared to represent.

This makes corrupted Engrams especially dangerous evidence:

> **The experience can be authentic while the autobiography is false.**

## Corrupted Engrams as Sources

An Engram is a record and therefore can carry mnemonic Weight.

A sufficiently loaded corrupted Engram can become a **Source** in its own right when present Recognition gives its recorded self-model enough pressure to reconstruct again.

The resulting Incursion is not a ghost released from a machine.

Reality is reconstructing from recorded experience that already contains a false conclusion about who experienced it.

An encounter should still define ordinary Source information—Class, Resonance Type, Incursion Trigger, Reconstruction Condition, and so on—rather than treating every corrupted Engram as mechanically identical.

## Comparison Can Become Recognition

Engram comparison is not automatically supernatural activation. Low-strength indexing, playback, statistical comparison, and ordinary technical analysis can remain measurement.

But mnemonic comparison is not guaranteed to be **passive** merely because the apparatus is scientific.

If a system strongly links an unresolved first-person record to candidate identities and repeatedly asks which authentic life the Memory belongs to, that topology can itself create **Recognition**.

The relevant question remains the same one that governs every Source:

> **What is recognizing what, and under what condition?**

A powerful identity-comparison lattice can therefore close a mnemonic circuit:

> **unresolved Memory that remembers being someone → authentic candidate Memories → active comparison → Recognition → Reconstruction**

This does not mean every database search manifests a Veiled. The encounter must establish enough Weight, amplification, and compatible topology for the comparison to matter metaphysically.

The EGRESS Engram Core in **Playing for Keeps** is the canonical example. EGRESS correctly measures the record and correctly contains the apparatus, but fails to recognize that its full-power comparison is itself participating in the reconstruction.

## Authentic Memory as Constraint

A corrupted Engram is not corrected by proving that everything inside it was fake.

Much of what it recorded may have genuinely occurred during the Incursion.

Instead, an authentic Engram from the underlying person can provide first-person Memory the false reconstruction cannot truthfully overwrite.

If the authentic experience materially contradicts the corrupted self-model, restoring that Memory can make the current Echo untenable.

The important distinction is:

> **Corrupted Memory says what the false reconstruction experienced. Authentic Memory says what the person actually experienced.**

Neither record, by itself, necessarily reconstructs the whole person.

## Comparison Is Measurement, Not Personhood

EGRESS can compare Engrams, quantify compatibility, identify repeated features, and use controlled Resonance to test whether two records plausibly belong to the same subject.

That capability is real and useful.

It does not mean similarity itself is Identity.

An encounter may turn comparison into a deterministic puzzle or routing procedure, but the comparison method is encounter-specific. There is no universal rule that every Engram possesses the same metadata fields or that every mismatch causes the same effect.

EGRESS's recurring blind spots are therefore related but distinct:

> **A sufficiently coherent dataset can still be an incomplete or wrongly attributed person.**

and:

> **A sufficiently powerful measurement can become part of the mnemonic system being measured.**

## Under Totality

Under Totality, separated Engrams may recognize related objects, people, Sources, places, and other stored memories across boundaries that previously kept them isolated.

Corrupted Engrams are especially unstable because their recorded Recognition may seek to recreate the false circumstances under which the original Incursion understood itself.

Several stored records may therefore:

- compete to describe one life;
- recognize the same object or person;
- cause one apparent subject to split into several identities;
- reveal that several specimens were fragments of one coherent life or household;
- become Sources when new Recognition closes a circuit around their recorded experience.

EGRESS can measure the Weight.

That does not mean EGRESS knows the person.

The Party grows in practical capability as it learns the Colony, earns access, and gains methods for recovering Identity. Keep that campaign growth distinct from character level and treasure.

## Encounter Baseline

Darkwing is expected to be **level 12 or 13, possibly higher**, with six characters. The encounter material (The Reading, the Engram Vault, The Burden and others) is tuned for six level-12 characters. Use the actual Party's level, size, and resources when preparing an encounter. A creature's level or a Source's level does not establish a character advancement milestone.

An act-by-act advancement schedule and complete treasure allocation are not fixed here. Until they are chosen, keep the established character level visible in preparation and review any encounter whose assumptions no longer fit.

## Rewards That Change What the Party Can Do

Useful campaign rewards include a trustworthy relationship, permission to consult an archive, a reusable method, custody of an object, a safe place, transport, or a public commitment. Establish the terms: access is not ownership, cooperation is not unconditional obedience, and borrowing an Asset does not necessarily transfer control.

Record actual transfers and agreements. A faction may still have duties toward an Asset it lets the Party use. Damaging or losing that Asset can have consequences for people who depended on it.

## Identity Tools

A Keystone supplies reusable access to a kind of evidence. It does not automatically identify the person in front of the Party, Restore their Identity, or replace the need for a local lead. Use :ref[Identity and Stabilization]{#rule.identity-and-stabilization} for the recovery and restoration procedure.

There are six :ref[Identity Keystones]{#rule.keystones}, including the Dawn Charter for Choice. The Keystone rule lists their capabilities and access limits. Context has no Keystone. Every recovery, including Choice, still requires truthful evidence; a tool cannot substitute for a person's actual agency.

No missing tool should prevent completion of the campaign. Each required component must have a local evidence route; tools and allies improve the Party's preparation and independence.

## Keep Different Kinds of Reward Distinct

| Reward | What to establish |
|---|---|
| Equipment or treasure | The actual item, its owner, and its mechanical entry |
| Institutional access | Who grants it, what may be consulted or used, and any terms |
| Identity method | What evidence it can recover and what lead it needs |
| Relationship | What commitment was actually made, by whom |
| Civic change | What becomes true in the Colony and who remains responsible |

Do not silently replace expected equipment with goodwill, or treat an institution's strategic value as a sale price. Record a reward when it is earned, even if the Party solved the objective without combat. Its precise mechanical value belongs in the relevant encounter or item entry.

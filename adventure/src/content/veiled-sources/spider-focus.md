- **Original active reference Source:** a worn eight-stranded Nana Anadi devotional Focus, held by the Spider until each apparent Window; physically placed on the test plinth between performances.
- **Correct co-resonant heirlooms:** **02 teacup + 03 thimble + 04 key**, belonging to a single forgotten Cult craftsperson at an east-annex personal needlework station. They reached the Hall through distinct channels and times.
- **Class / Reconstruction:** **Class IV**, **Composite / Form**, with a genuine craft-and-devotion Pattern / Relationship behind the false divine Form. Class follows the Weight and is independent of level 20; tune only if the site-level mnemonic pressure warrants it.
- **True impulse:** A maker used craft, hospitality, keys, and devotional practice to bind people's lives together and wished for liberation through the stories of Grandmother Spider.
- **False conclusion:** The maker's faith, artistic practice, and idea of the goddess can be reconstructed as the goddess herself.
- **Actual personality:** This Incursion has accumulated real experience, has learned that it is a reconstruction, and seeks an independent identity. That self-awareness is an encounter-specific character development, **not** a universal fact about all Veiled.
- **Coherence:** When the exact three heirlooms enter the isolated test field with the reference Focus, their genuine shared history produces authentic Coherence **during manifestation or a supposed Window**. The goddess-form contract is broken without any HP defeat, and the ordinary life beneath becomes newly legible. Coherence is not full Stabilization or Anchoring, which still need truthful Meaning and a viable destination.
- **False Window:** The Spider never reaches 0 HP during the Witnesses' coordinated “Dispersals.” Its own body withdraws voluntarily, leaving its Source and webbing active. Its actual 0-HP Dispersal, if somehow achieved, follows ordinary Veiled rules, not the four-performances routine.
- **New object:** the fourth magical item, produced by the Spider's own real crafting during the encounter, may become an independently attributable mnemonic seed. Its specific power, level, slots, Bulk, investment and activation are **not yet decided**. It is not an automatic Soul, Anchor, survival certificate, or legitimate historical object of the forgotten Cult crafter.

:::design
Class IV comes from the source. Source Level 14 is an implementation choice, independent of the Weaver’s Creature Level 20. The original Focus and three historic heirlooms form one investigated network; the newly made fourth item is not retrospectively added to its history.
:::

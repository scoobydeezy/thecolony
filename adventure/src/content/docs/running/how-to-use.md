This book is written for the GM. Everything in it may be read by the GM. Some passages are marked because of **who else** may hear or see them, or **when**.

## Finding the Right Page

The Introduction is the GM's handbook. The Colony is the setting guide. The three act sections contain scenes and encounters, and Reference holds the people, places, rules, and statistics those scenes use.

Read the current act overview before preparing its next situation. Sidebar order helps you find material; it does not force the Party to visit every page. Chapters marked as alternatives, independent investigations, or concurrent fronts follow their stated arrangement. Suggested session numbers are pacing guidance.

Use an encounter's At the Table panel for its objective, moving parts, and possible outcomes. Follow linked entries for reusable rules and individual facts. A consequence established in play takes precedence over the starting situation described in a later scene.

## Markers

:::read-aloud
Text in this style is meant to be read or paraphrased aloud to the players.
:::

:::player-facing
Text in this style may be shown to the players as written, for example on a handout.
:::

:::secret
Text in this style is a secret: something the players must not learn yet, or must discover for themselves.
:::

:::gm-note
Text in this style explains why something matters, so you can improvise when the players do something the book doesn't expect.
:::

:::branch[An example outcome]
A branch describes one way a scene can end, and what the campaign records when it does.
:::

## References

Names of creatures, NPCs, locations and rules link to their entries in the appendices, for example :ref[Kelda Varruk]{#npc.kelda-varruk} or the :ref[Book of Names]{#source.book-of-names}. Chapters refer to these entries instead of repeating them, so a rule or stat block only ever exists in one place.

Values that come from the Veiled Source rules are calculated from the Source's Class and Source Level, for example the Book of Names' Mnemonic Backlash (:stat{#source.book-of-names key=backlash}).

## Book and Campaign App

The book describes the prepared campaign and its starting facts. The app records what has happened: current faction assignments, goals, people, Assets, conditions, and session Events. A directory labelled “At the start of the campaign” is a baseline, not a report of the current world.

Follow reference links to open the owning page. Keep the scene and its most-used rule entries open together when preparing a complex encounter.

This Memory opens when the Echo reaches its first Page Lock (see [Page Locks](/docs/act-3/the-burden-phase-3/#page-locks)).

> *page.*

The burning streets vanish.

Darkwing now runs through the early independent Colony during a **real invasion from the Duskwood**.

Xulgaths, Hryngars, and other creatures genuinely attacked the Colony.

This is the historical rhyme with the earlier Duskwood adventure.

> **The official Duskwood story was not fabricated from nothing.**

The later lie was applying a genuine older danger to Old Town's unrelated destruction.

It was:

> **the wrong truth attached to the wrong event.**

## Terrain — Broken Defenses

The remembered settlement contains:

- overturned carts;
- crude barricades;
- damaged walls;
- collapsed homes;
- churned ground.

Large areas are difficult terrain.

Walls and carts grant normal cover.

The Echo ignores Memory-generated difficult terrain.

## Memory Pressure — Poisoned Raiders

At initiative 10:

every Darkwing PC and exposed Dawn victim attempts:

> **DC 35 basic Reflex**

**Damage:** 4d8 piercing

The remembered arrows are poison-tipped.

**Critical Success:** no damage and no sickened.  
**Success:** half damage and **sickened 1**.  
**Failure:** full damage and **sickened 2**.  
**Critical Failure:** double damage, **sickened 2**, and **stunned 2**.

The remembered attackers do not need persistent combat statistics.

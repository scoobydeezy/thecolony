## Autonomous Turn

The black Floodwarden is an expression of the remembered duty, not automatically the swept-away living warden’s ghost. It does not accept PC commands or rotate street tiles for them. Apply the three-barrier placement instructions in :ref[Milltown]{#encounter.milltown}, immediately before the Flood’s turn. The existing protection against the real River Foam continues working.

The routine’s barriers can help or harm depending on current routes. Interfering with an individual barrier can change real geometry when an ordinary ability supports it; that is not permission to control the Warden’s subsequent decisions. If a character suppresses the manifestation, skip its placement while suppressed and leave the flood emergency active. No victory is awarded for attacking the Warden.

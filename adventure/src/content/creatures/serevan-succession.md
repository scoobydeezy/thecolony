**Ceremonial Staff** +27, **Damage** 3d8+11 bludgeoning, nonlethal

### Unyielding Doctrine
Serevan cannot be persuaded during this encounter to permit the Reading to stop.

This is not a general immunity to social actions or mind-affecting effects. He understands the cost and believes allowing an unproven alternative to risk the Colony would violate his duty.

### Hold the Chamber
Order allies within **20 feet** of Serevan gain a **+1 status bonus** to checks and DCs against Grapple, Shove, Reposition, and Trip.

### There Is No Time ◆
Choose one Order ally within 60 feet who can hear Serevan. That ally may immediately **Stride up to half its Speed**.

If the movement ends adjacent to a creature interfering with Vara or the Book, the ally may instead **Step 5 feet** after the movement.

### Restore the Rite ↻
**Trigger:** A creature within 30 feet closes, obscures, blocks access to, or otherwise physically interferes with the Book or another non-Vara ritual requirement.

Serevan Strides up to half his Speed. If he reaches the affected ritual object, he immediately **Interacts** to restore its ordinary usable state.

Restore the Rite cannot move Vara, free her from a condition, or override her voluntary decision to stop.

### Stay Your Hand ◆◆
**AUDITORY, EMOTION, LINGUISTIC, MENTAL**

One creature within 60 feet attempts a **DC 35 Will save**.

- **Critical Success:** Unaffected.
- **Success:** The creature takes a –1 status penalty to attacks and Athletics checks against Order allies or Vara until the start of Serevan's next turn.
- **Failure:** As success, and the creature is **slowed 1** until the end of its next turn.
- **Critical Failure:** The creature is **slowed 1** for 1 round and cannot use reactions for that duration.

### Tactical Priority
Serevan prioritizes, in order:

1. Vara retaining enough actions/access to Read.
2. Restoring disrupted ritual infrastructure.
3. Redirecting Order allies toward the current interruption.
4. Delaying dangerous intruders.
5. Dealing damage only when necessary.

If Vara voluntarily stops, Serevan's objective becomes securing Vara and the Book until the Order can regain control of the chamber.

## The Succession

Use the Institutional Ward and Last Secret timing in Phase IV. Prior Institutional Backlash damage remains real when the final Ward falls. Do not refresh HP, create a second form, or add a spellcaster suite.
